# Dead Horizon — Weapon Customization System

---

## Overview
- Customization done at gunsmiths in towns
- Options are realistic and grounded — no ridiculous attachments
- If a gun shouldn't have something, it doesn't get the option
- Some grip/barrel options provide stat bonuses (mobility, control, etc.)

---

## Customization Categories

### 1. Varnish (Wood components only)
- Available on guns with wooden parts (stocks, grips, forends)
- Wide selection of different wood types and finishes
- Examples: walnut, cherry, birch, mahogany, ebony, oak, maple

### 2. Metals (Metal components)
- Available on guns made of metal (most guns)
- Different metal finishes and colors
- Examples: blued steel, nickel, stainless, bronze, black oxide, case hardened, gold plated

### 3. Engravings
- **7 different engraving variants**
- Several different colors for engravings
- Can be applied to eligible metal surfaces
- NOTE: Grip engravings are NOT available to the player — only special/unique weapons have them

### 4. Grip
- Different grip materials and styles
- Some grips provide stat bonuses:
  - Mobility
  - Control/recoil management
  - Stability
  - Draw speed

### 5. Barrel
- Length options where applicable
- Suppressors ONLY on guns that realistically support them
- **No suppressors on:** Revolvers, sawed-off shotguns, lever-action rifles
- **Suppressors available on:** Modern pistols (Beretta, M17, Glock), rifles with threaded barrels (M4, M16)

### 6. Sights
- Iron sights, red dots, holographic, scopes
- Only on guns that can realistically support them
- **No optics on:** Revolvers, sawed-off shotguns, derringers
- **Scopes on:** Hunting rifles, sniper rifles, some assault rifles with rails

### 7. Rails/Accessories
- Only on guns that have or can add rail systems
- **AK-47** — no rail by default, but you can ADD a rail as a customization, then attach accessories
- **M4/M16** — has rails, full accessory support
- **Hunting rifles** — limited rail options
- **Shotguns** — pump shotguns can get basic rail, sawed-offs cannot

---

## Customization Rules

### DO NOT add these combinations:
- Suppressor on a revolver
- Suppressor on a sawed-off shotgun
- Scope on a sawed-off shotgun
- Scope on a snub nose revolver
- Rail attachments on guns with no rail and no way to add one
- Extended magazines on revolvers
- Foregrips on pistols
- Bipods on shotguns

### Guns with LIMITED customization:
- **Sawed-off shotguns** — Varnish (if wood), metals, engravings, grip only
- **Snub nose revolvers** — Metals, engravings, grip only
- **Derringers** — Metals, engravings only
- **Cattleman/SAA revolvers** — Varnish, metals, engravings, grip

### Guns with FULL customization:
- **M4/M16** — Everything: metals, engravings, grip, barrel, suppressor, sights, rails, accessories
- **AK-47/AKM** — Metals, engravings, varnish (wood parts), grip, can add rail then accessories
- **Beretta 92FS** — Metals, engravings, grip, barrel, suppressor, sights
- **Hunting rifles** — Varnish, metals, engravings, scope, barrel
- **Sniper rifles** — Full scope options, metals, engravings, barrel, bipod

## Special Honor Weapon

### "The Devil's Hand" — .500 S&W Magnum (Snub Nose Variant)
- **Unlock:** Honor Level 1 (lowest normal honor)
- **Based on:** S&W Bodyguard snub nose shape but chambered in .500 S&W Magnum
- **Appearance:**
  - Pitch black frame — completely black
  - Gold skull engraving on the barrel
  - Intricate gold engravings covering the entire gun
  - Black and red grip WITH engravings (unique — player cannot get grip engravings on any other gun)
  - Compact, concealed hammer design
  - Snub nose barrel
- **The only gun in the game with engraved grips** — makes it instantly recognizable

## Customization Stat Bonuses
- **Grips** — Can affect: Mobility, Control, Stability, Draw Speed
- **Barrels** — Longer = more accuracy/range, shorter = more mobility
- **Suppressors** — Reduced noise, slightly reduced damage
- **Sights** — Better accuracy at various ranges
- **Rails/Accessories** — Flashlights, laser sights (functional)
