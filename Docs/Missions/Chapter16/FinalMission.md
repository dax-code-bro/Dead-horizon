# Chapter 16: "Dead Horizon" — Final Mission

## Created by the user. Jeremiah's last mission.

---

## Setup
- Clark has returned with allies.
- Clark is exposed. Guns pointed at each other.
- All women and some male members already left — they're not present.
- The gang splits into teams: Joseph sides with Clark.
- Both teams dash for cover. Shooting everywhere.

---

## The Final Battle

### The Fight
- Jeremiah tells his team to retreat.
- Jeremiah follows his team to a small ledge, keeps running.
- **Clark jumps onto Jeremiah from above the ledge.** Both hit the ground.
- They get up. Bloody fistfight.
- Clark sprains Jeremiah's ankle. Chokes him.
- Jeremiah kicks Clark in the stomach to break free.
- Both grab their guns — shoot them out of each other's hands.
- Both grab knives. A brutal, bloody knife fight.
- **Joseph arrives.** Tells them to stop.
- Clark tries to get Joseph to side with him.
- Joseph points his gun at BOTH of them.
- Both Jeremiah and Clark talk to Joseph — trying to reach him.

### The Shot
- Joseph puts his gun in his holster.
- **Then quickly draws and shoots Jeremiah in the chest.**
- Jeremiah stumbles back. Wheezing. Coughing. Collapses.

---

## LOW HONOR ENDING

- Clark stumbles over to Jeremiah. Points his gun at Jeremiah's head.
- Jeremiah looks up at Clark. Blood running from his mouth.

**Jeremiah:** *(barely a whisper)* "...Tell him."

- **BANG.** Clark puts a bullet in Jeremiah's head.
- Clark laughs it off.
- He hears the military coming — someone was shouting, alerting them.
- Clark stumbles away in the other direction, down the cliff.
- **Cut to Joseph.** He walks over to Jeremiah's body. Stands there.

**Joseph:** "I'm sorry, son."

- He collapses to his knees.
- **Fade to black.**
- Credits roll.
- Three seconds in: **"Remember."**
- Credits continue.

**Jeremiah's last word: "Tell him."** — Tell Joseph the truth. Tell him what Clark did. Tell him who his son really was. Knowing Clark never will.

---

## HIGH HONOR ENDING

- Clark stumbles over to Jeremiah. Points his gun at Jeremiah's head.
- **The military is coming.** Shouting in the distance. Getting closer.
- Clark is interrupted. Annoyed. He looks at the approaching soldiers.
- Clark stumbles off to avoid being caught. Same with Joseph — both flee.
- **Jeremiah is alone.**
- He tries to crawl away. Grunting. Breathing heavy.
- He realizes he's not going to make it off the cliff.
- He finds a rock. Drags himself to it. Lays against it.
- **The sunrise is rising slowly in front of him.**
- Cut to a shot of Jeremiah turning his head to look at the sunrise.

**He breathes. Counting each one:**
1. *(breath)* — quiet
2. *(breath)* — slightly louder
3. *(breath)* — softer
4. *(breath)* — barely there

**Five.** He doesn't say it. He breathes it. Holds the start of it — a long, two-second exhale. Letting go. Of everything.

**Jeremiah:** *(on the exhale, almost inaudible)* "...I tried."

- The screen slowly backs out. Captures the image — Jeremiah against the rock, the sunrise, the cliff, the world stretching out below him. A dead horizon.
- **The image freezes. Turns black and white.**
- Credits slowly appear and start rolling.
- Three seconds in: **"Remember."**
- Screen cuts to black. Credits continue.

**Jeremiah's last word: "I tried."** — A man who spent his whole life trying to hold everything together. Trying to save everyone. Trying to be enough. His last honest truth.
