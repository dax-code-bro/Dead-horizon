# Chapter 15: "Ashes to Ashes"

## Setting: Abandoned Town → Open Country
## Missions: 5
## Tone: Bittersweet peace. Rebuilding. But the shadow of Clark looms.

---

## Mission 1: "Picking Up the Pieces"

**The gang tries to rebuild as one unit.**

- The town is damaged from the standoff. But it's home. The only one left.
- **Gameplay: Rebuild and repair.** Fix barricades, tend to wounded, reorganize supplies.
- Jeremiah leads. Not because he wants to — because there's nobody else.
- Joseph is mostly gone now. Lucid moments are minutes, not hours. Luisa cares for him full-time.
- The camp feels different. Smaller. But closer. The people who are still here chose to stay.

**Nate:** "Jerry, what happens now?"

**Jeremiah:** "We live. That's what happens."

---

## Mission 2: "The Simple Things"

**A day of peace.** The rarest thing in the game.

- No enemies. No drama. No death.
- Jeremiah goes fishing. Alone. At the river.
- He catches a fish. A good one. Holds it up. Nobody to show.
- He thinks about Josh. The tiny fish Josh caught. "It's huge!"
- He lets it go. Watches it swim away.
- Back at camp, he helps Phil whittle a new cane (his leg won't fully heal).
- Alma tends Charles's grave. Jeremiah brings her flowers. They sit together in silence.
- Hector and Rosa are arguing about something stupid. Normal.
- Earl and Faye are sitting on a porch. Earl is actually smiling. First time.
- **The camp feels like a family again.** Broken. Incomplete. But family.

---

## Mission 3: "A Visitor"

**Bellamy sends word.** A messenger arrives.

- Arthur Bellamy has been watching. With Harding dead and Clark expelled, Bellamy sees an opportunity.
- He sends a message: "The Waterson name dies with me. I'm dissolving the company. Your debt is paid. Leave me alone and I'll leave you alone."
- Jeremiah accepts. The Waterson thread is finally, truly over.
- **The messenger also delivers a package from Marcus.** A drawing from Josh. It's the whole gang — stick figures. Jeremiah is the tallest one. Josh labeled everyone. Marcus, Emily, Josh, Uncle Jerry, Grandpa Joseph, and a figure in the sky labeled "Mr. Charles."
- Jeremiah pins the drawing to the wall of his room.

---

## Mission 4: "Joseph's Garden"

**Jeremiah builds Joseph a garden.**

- Behind one of the houses. A small patch of dirt. Some seeds from the supply stores.
- Not because it'll grow anything. But because Joseph used to talk about wanting a garden. Back when he was lucid. Back when he had dreams.
- Jeremiah digs. Plants. Waters. Joseph watches from a chair.
- Joseph doesn't understand what's happening. But he smiles. A real one.

**Joseph:** "...This is nice."

**Jeremiah:** "Yeah. It is."

**Joseph:** "Who are you?"

*Jeremiah's hand stops. The trowel in the dirt.*

**Jeremiah:** "...I'm Jerry, Joseph. Your son."

**Joseph:** "Jerry. That's a nice name. Did I name you that?"

**Jeremiah:** "...Yeah. You did."

**Joseph:** "Good name."

- Joseph falls asleep in the chair. In the sun. Luisa puts a blanket over him.
- Jeremiah finishes the garden. Washes his hands. Walks away.

*"Who are you?"* — Joseph doesn't recognize him anymore. The last thread just snapped.

---

## Mission 5: "Storm Clouds"

**Clark is coming back.**

- Hector spots tracks outside of town. Boot prints. Fresh. Someone's been watching them.
- Then Nate finds something nailed to a tree at the edge of town. A note.

*"I told you I'd come back. And I'm not alone. — C.J."*

- Clark has allies. Where he got them — the remnants of whoever Harding left behind, mercenaries, criminals he's gathered — it doesn't matter. He's coming.
- Jeremiah reads the note. Folds it. Puts it in his pocket next to the letter to Joseph.
- He walks to the center of town. Looks at his people. The survivors. The ones who stayed.

**Jeremiah:** "Clark is coming. He's not alone. I don't know when. Could be tomorrow. Could be a week. But he's coming."

**Phil:** *(standing with his cane and both sawed-offs)* "Good. I've got unfinished business."

**Hector:** *(knife out)* "He walked away once. He won't walk away again."

**Jeremiah:** "Everyone who wants to leave — leave now. No judgment. You've earned it."

*Nobody moves.*

**Jeremiah:** "Then we get ready."

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 16"** appears.

## Chapter 15 Summary
- The gang rebuilds as one unit — smaller but closer
- A rare peaceful day — fishing, whittling, grave visits, normalcy
- Bellamy dissolves the Waterson company — that threat is over
- Josh sends a drawing of the gang — Charles is a figure in the sky
- Jeremiah builds Joseph a garden
- **Joseph asks "Who are you?"** — doesn't recognize Jeremiah anymore
- Clark sends a warning note — he's coming back with allies
- The gang prepares for the final fight
- Nobody leaves when given the chance
- **The stage is set for Chapter 16 — Jeremiah's final chapter**
