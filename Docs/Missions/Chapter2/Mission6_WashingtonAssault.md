# Chapter 2, Mission 6: Washington Outpost Assault

## Chapter: 2
## Location: The Gorge → Washington Outpost

---

## Setup
- The Washington gang has spread across the gang's territory.
- The gang wants them to back off before things escalate further.
- All 19 men ride out together — full force.

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Assault
- All the boys move in quickly on the Washington outpost.
- **Full arsenal deployed:** Rocket launchers, machine guns, dynamite, throwing bottles (Molotovs).
- Absolute chaos — total destruction of the outpost.
- **~300 Washington gang members at the outpost.**
- The gang clears them all. **Zero casualties on the gang's side.**
- This proves that the gang at full force (19 men) is an unstoppable force.

### Section 2: The Loot
- The gang finds a small wagon at the outpost.
- They hook it up to three horses.
- Load up all the guns and ammunition they can find.
- Weapon and ammo shortage: solved.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- 19 vs 300 — the gang wins decisively with no casualties
- Demonstrates the gang's quality vs Washington's quantity
- Washington throws bodies at problems; Joseph's gang fights smart and fights together
- The gang is now fully armed and supplied
- This will likely provoke a response from Michael Washington
