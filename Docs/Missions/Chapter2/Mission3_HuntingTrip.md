# Chapter 2, Mission 3: (Hunting with Trevor and Lenny)

## Chapter: 2
## Location: Wilderness near the Gorge

---

## Pre-Mission Camp Interactions
- Jeremiah gives Emily money to pay for things for Josh
- Jeremiah comforts Emily and Josh — feeds Marcus's jealousy but also pushes Marcus to step up

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Hunt
- Jeremiah goes hunting with Trevor and Lenny for elk.
- Peaceful ride out — bonding between the three.
- Trevor gives Jeremiah and Lenny each a gift: a **.308 hunting rifle**.

### Section 2: The Bear Attack
- During a peaceful moment, a bear charges out of nowhere.
- The bear throws Jeremiah off his horse.
- Jeremiah puts two bullets into the bear — but it's already on top of Lenny.
- Trevor stabs the bear in the back with a knife.
- Jeremiah runs over and drives the knife straight into the bear's eye.
- The bear jerks its head, knocking Jeremiah back.
- The bear stumbles and falls dead.

### Section 3: Return to Camp
- They help Lenny up and ride back to camp.
- Lenny's injuries: bad scratches on his back. No broken bones, no limb damage.
- He gets bandaged up and is back on his feet soon.
- **Joseph shows genuine care and concern for Lenny** — these are his children, not just recruits.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Trevor is more than a cook — he fights when needed
- Lenny earns his stripes, takes a hit and survives
- Joseph's fatherly nature reinforced — genuinely worried about Lenny
- Jeremiah receives a .308 hunting rifle from Trevor (not a unique weapon, just a gift)
