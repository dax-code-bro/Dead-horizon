# Chapter 2, Mission 1: Settling In, the McAllens

## Chapter: 2
## Time Skip: A few months after Chapter 1
## Location: The Gorge Camp

---

## Changes Since Chapter 1
- Marcus is back on his feet, fully healed
- Emily is still pressuring Marcus to be a father but with less intensity
- Marcus's relationship with his family: small shift beginning (still near rock bottom)
- The gang has settled into the Gorge camp

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Joseph's Announcement (Interrupted)
- Joseph stands before the gang to talk about a nearby town — gunsmith, general store, car dealership.

**Joseph:** "Alright, so I've been doing some scouting. There's a town not too far from here — got a gunsmith, general store, even a car dealership. Now I'm thinking we could—"

- Luisa grabs his arm and pulls him aside before he can finish.

**Joseph:** *(being dragged off)* "I— Jerry, you should probably go check on the McAllens."

### Section 2: The Fight
- Jeremiah walks toward the McAllens' area. He hears yelling before he gets there.
- Marcus and Emily are in a full-blown argument.

**Marcus:** "Don't stand there and act like everything's fine, Emily!"

**Emily:** "I'm not acting like anything! I'm trying to talk to you!"

**Marcus:** "Talk? You want to talk? Let's talk then. Let's talk about how you just decided we were together. Did I get a say in that? Did anyone ask me?"

**Emily:** "That's not what happened and you know it."

**Marcus:** "Do I? Because from where I'm standing, you picked me out and that was that. I didn't get a choice."

**Emily:** "You didn't seem to have a problem with it back then."

**Marcus:** "Yeah well maybe I was young and stupid."

**Emily:** *(like she's been slapped)* "...What?"

**Marcus:** "And Josh — how do I even know he's mine, Emily? Huh? You were with half the camp before me. Could be anyone's."

**Emily:** *(voice shaking)* "Don't you dare. Don't you dare say that about our son."

**Marcus:** "Our son? You sure about that?"

**Emily:** "You know he's yours. You look at him and you KNOW he's yours."

**Marcus:** "I don't know anything anymore."

**Emily:** "What do you want from me, Marcus? What do you want me to say?"

**Marcus:** "I want you to stop pretending like we're some happy family! We're not! We never were!"

**Emily:** "I never said we were! I'm just trying—"

**Marcus:** "And Jerry — your precious Jerry — why don't you just go run off with him? Huh? He's already playing daddy. He's already taking care of you. Just make it official."

**Emily:** "Jerry is your brother. He stepped up because YOU left."

**Marcus:** "Maybe he stepped up because he wanted to."

**Emily:** "You are unbelievable."

**Marcus:** "And you're a liar. You've been lying to me since the day we met."

**Emily:** *(tears starting)* "Everything I have ever done — EVERYTHING — has been for you and that boy. I sold myself to men I didn't love so your son could eat. I held him every night and told him his daddy was coming back when I didn't even believe it myself. So don't you EVER stand there and tell me I'm a liar."

**Marcus:** "...Then maybe you should've just let me stay gone."

- Emily smacks Marcus hard across the face. His cheek goes red.
- Jeremiah steps in, grabs Marcus, and pulls him aside.

**Jeremiah:** "Alright. That's enough. Both of you."

**Jeremiah:** "Get on your horse."

- Jeremiah puts Marcus on Jim and mounts Cerberus. They ride out.

### Section 3: Town Trip — The Gunsmith
- Jeremiah asks Marcus what he wants to do.
- Marcus's list: buy a new gun, and "a better family" — Jeremiah shuts down the second one.

**Jeremiah:** *(snaps)* "Let's start with the easiest part."

- They go to the gunsmith.

**Clerk:** "What are you looking for?"

**Marcus:** "I'm looking for a handgun. Decent recoil, large capacity magazine, ammunition not more expensive than the gun. And it needs to stop a person in its tracks."

- The clerk hands him a **Beretta 92FS Bronze**.
- Marcus holsters it immediately.

### Section 4: The Bar
- Jeremiah takes Marcus to the saloon for a drink. Meant to be three or four. They end up blackout drunk.

*(Full dialogue of drinks, getting wasted, stumbling to horses, riding back, Emily's reaction.)*

- Marcus makes a drunken apology to Emily. She sends them both to bed.

### Section 5: The Morning After — Nothing Changes
- Morning. Marcus wakes up hungover. He doesn't mention the apology. Acts like it never happened.
- Emily walks over to Jeremiah, asks him to take Josh out. She doesn't even look at Marcus.
- Marcus sits there. Watches Jeremiah take his son. Doesn't say a word. Doesn't volunteer.
- Later, Marcus passes Emily in camp. She tries to make eye contact. He looks away.

**Emily:** "You said things last night."

**Marcus:** "I was drunk."

**Emily:** "You said you were sorry."

**Marcus:** "I said a lot of things. I was drunk."

**Emily:** "So none of it was real."

**Marcus:** "I don't remember half of it. Drop it, Emily."

*She watches him walk away. Whatever door opened last night just slammed shut.*

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Marcus and Emily's worst fight yet — he questions if Josh is his, tells her to run off with Jeremiah
- Emily slaps Marcus
- Marcus buys the Beretta 92FS Bronze
- The drunken apology means NOTHING — Marcus takes it back the next morning
- Marcus does NOT volunteer for Josh — Jeremiah takes him as usual
- The relationship is still completely broken
- Marcus and Jeremiah's bond strengthened through the bar scene but Marcus's walls are back up by morning

## Marcus Family Arc Progress
- **Chapter 1:** Completely distant
- **Chapter 2:** Still broken. Drunken apology retracted. Walls back up. No progress with Josh.
- **Chapter 3:** Still broken. Small cracks but Marcus keeps pulling back.
- **Chapter 4:** The REAL shift. Everything changes here.
- **Chapter 5:** Nearly perfect family
