# Chapter 2, Mission 7: The Evacuation

## Chapter: 2 (Final Mission)
## Location: The Gorge → Road → The Hill

---

## Setup
- After a period of rest following the Washington assault, the Marshalls close in.
- Marcus was out scouting and spots an entire militia heading for the Gorge.
- He rides back and warns everyone.
- They have **30 minutes** to pack up and evacuate.

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Evacuation
- **OBJECTIVE: Pack up camp and get everyone out in 30 minutes**
- The entire gang (37 people) mobilizes.
- Everything gets loaded onto wagons and horses.
- They abandon the Gorge — their second camp lost.

### Section 2: The Road
- The gang rides out. First few miles are stressful — looking over their shoulders.
- Eventually the tension eases. Smooth sailing.

### Section 3: Marcus and Emily's Moment
- Emily and Marcus are alone in a wagon. Josh is riding with Jeremiah on Cerberus.

**Marcus:** "Emily... I'm sorry. About what I said back at the Gorge. About Josh not being mine. I didn't mean that."

**Emily:** "I know you didn't."

**Marcus:** "No. Let me say it. I look at that boy and I see my eyes. I see my face. He's mine. I've always known he's mine."

- Marcus admits he's scared of Josh — scared of the way his son looks at him, waiting for him to disappear.
- Emily tells him: "You stay. That's how."
- Marcus asks how old she was when he left. Emily: "Sixteen."
- Marcus: "I should've been there."
- Emily: "Yeah. You should've. But you're here now."
- Marcus acknowledges Jerry was there for her and doesn't snap about it for the first time.
- Emily: "Now I need it to be you."
- **Marcus takes Emily's hand. Fully. Not halfway.** Emily leans her head on his shoulder. He rests his cheek on her head.

**Emily:** "Don't let go."

**Marcus:** "I'm not going anywhere."

*(Full dialogue in mission file.)*

### Section 4: Josh Sees Them
- Jeremiah rides up on Cerberus with Josh.
- Josh sees his parents holding hands.

**Josh:** "...Are you holding daddy's hand?"

**Marcus:** "Yeah. She is."

**Josh:** "Can I come?"

**Marcus:** "...Get over here."

- Jeremiah lifts Josh into the wagon. Josh sits between them.
- Emily puts her arm around Josh. Marcus puts his hand on Josh's head — awkward, stiff, but there.
- Josh leans into Marcus.
- Emily is crying — the good kind.
- Jeremiah watches, gives a small nod, and rides ahead.

### Section 5: Arrival
- The gang arrives at their new home: a **small clearing on a hill overlooking a valley**.
- Open, elevated — they can see anyone coming from miles away.
- The McAllen family arrives together on the wagon.

### Chapter 2 End

## New Camp: The Hill
- **Location:** Small clearing on a hilltop overlooking a valley.
- **Advantage:** Elevated position with long sightlines in all directions.
- **Contrast to the Gorge:** Open instead of enclosed. Standing tall instead of hiding.

## Key Story Points
- Marcus warns the gang about the militia — proves his loyalty
- The Gorge is lost — second camp abandoned
- Marcus and Emily's first real emotional breakthrough
- Marcus acknowledges Emily was 16 when he left, takes responsibility
- Marcus holds Emily's hand — first real physical affection
- Josh sees his parents together and joins them — the McAllen family unit forming
- Marcus says "I'm not going anywhere" — and Emily believes him
- Jeremiah steps back, lets the family have their moment
