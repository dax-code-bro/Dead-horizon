## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Weapon Buy
- Jeremiah goes to check on Joseph, who says the gang needs more weapons.
- Joseph picks the crew: **Joseph, Jeremiah, Trevor, and Phil**.
- They ride into town to the gunsmith.
- They buy **AK-47s** in bulk — standard issue for the gang.
- Phil stands around outside — he's already got everything he needs (three shotguns and dynamite).

### Section 2: The Saloon Trap
- They decide to stop at the saloon. Big mistake.
- A man in his 70s keeps glancing at them.
- Joseph senses something wrong, tries to get them to leave.
- As they head for the exit:
  - Poker players stand up and block the door — armed.
  - The bartender puts a shotgun on the bar.
  - Every "civilian" in the saloon draws weapons.
  - The old man steps forward, shows his badge — he's law enforcement.
  - He pulls out his phone, shows their bounties.

**Officer:** "We are all Americans, but if you ain't gonna be civilized about this, we'll put five bullets in each of these men before they can even rest a hand on the hammer."

- The officer makes an offer:
  - **Hand over Marcus** — they get a few days to say goodbye to families.
  - **Hand over Joseph** — everyone else goes free.

### Section 3: Joseph's Plan
- Joseph agrees to hand over Marcus and turn himself in during the goodbye.
- **The real plan:** Get close to the commanding officer, take him out. Then rob the bank while law enforcement is disorganized without their leader.
- They ride back to the Gorge.

### Section 4: Telling Emily

**Joseph:** "Emily. We need to talk."

**Emily:** *(immediately tense)* "What happened."

**Joseph:** "There's lawmen in town. They know about us."

**Emily:** "...Okay."

**Joseph:** "They want Marcus."

**Emily:** *(face goes white, pulls Josh closer)* "No."

**Joseph:** "Emily—"

**Emily:** "No. Absolutely not. You are NOT handing him over. I just got him back. I just—"

**Jeremiah:** "Emily. Listen. We're not giving him to anyone."

**Emily:** "Then why are you telling me this?"

**Joseph:** "Because we told them we would. It's a play. We get close to their commanding officer, we take him out. Without him, they fall apart. Then we hit their bank while they're scrambling."

**Emily:** "And what if it goes wrong? What if they take him and you can't—"

**Jeremiah:** "It won't go wrong."

**Emily:** "You don't know that, Jerry. You can't promise me that."

**Joseph:** "I'm turning myself in too. That's how we get close. They think they're getting both of us."

**Emily:** *(voice cracking)* "Both of you? So I'm supposed to just sit here and hope you all come back alive?"

**Jeremiah:** "That's exactly what you're going to do. Because we will."

**Emily:** "...If he doesn't come back, I'll never forgive any of you."

**Joseph:** "He's coming back."

### Section 5: Telling Marcus

**Jeremiah:** "We need to talk."

**Marcus:** "Last time you said that I ended up blackout drunk."

**Jeremiah:** "This is serious, Marcus."

**Marcus:** "What."

**Jeremiah:** "There's law in town. They know who we are. They showed us our bounties."

**Marcus:** "...How much am I worth?"

**Jeremiah:** "That's not the point. They want you. Specifically."

**Marcus:** *(sets the Beretta down slowly)* "And what'd Joseph say?"

**Jeremiah:** "He told them he'd hand you over."

**Marcus:** *(stands up fast)* "He WHAT?"

**Jeremiah:** "Sit down."

**Marcus:** "He's giving me up? After everything — after you dragged me out of those chains — he's just—"

**Jeremiah:** "Marcus. Sit. Down. It's a play. We're not handing you over. Joseph's turning himself in too — says it's so you can say goodbye. But really, we get close to their commanding officer, take him out, and hit the bank while they're crippled."

**Marcus:** "...So I'm bait."

**Jeremiah:** "You're part of the plan."

**Marcus:** "Same thing."

**Jeremiah:** "It's not. We've got your back. We had it at the Washington camp and we've got it now."

**Marcus:** "Does Emily know?"

**Jeremiah:** "Joseph told her."

**Marcus:** "How'd she take it?"

**Jeremiah:** "How do you think?"

**Marcus:** "...She probably thinks I'm not coming back."

**Jeremiah:** "Then prove her wrong."

**Marcus:** "...Alright. When do we do this?"

### Section 6: Emily Talks Them Out of It
- Emily persuades them that the plan is too dangerous.
- Through her persistence, they all eventually agree — they can't go through with it.
- The plan is abandoned.

### Section 7: Joseph's Breaking Point
- Joseph is visibly shaken. He had a plan. It got shut down.
- The Washingtons are out there. The law is closing in. His own people won't follow him.
- **Joseph's sanity begins to crack.** The pressure of everything is getting to him.
- The mission ends on this dark note.

### Mission Complete

## Key Story Points
- The law knows where the gang is operating
- Joseph's sanity is starting to crack — first signs of his mental decline
- Emily's influence over the group is significant — she talked them out of the plan
- The old lawman remains a threat — they agreed to hand over Marcus and haven't delivered
- Marcus and Emily still near rock bottom but both showing they care (Emily fighting for him, Marcus asking how she took the news)
- The bank remains unrobbed — potential future mission

## New Threats
- **The Old Lawman** — Badge-carrying officer in his 70s. Has the gang's bounties. Knows their faces. Set up the saloon trap.
- **The Law** — Organized presence in the nearby town. Multiple agents disguised as civilians.
