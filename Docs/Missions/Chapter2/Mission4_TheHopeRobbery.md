# Chapter 2, Mission 4: The Hope Robbery

## Chapter: 2
## Location: The Gorge → Hope (Town) → The Gorge

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Ride to Hope
- The gang needs a score — they've been running low on cash.
- Joseph leads a crew into the town of **Hope** for a general store robbery.
- They ride in and put their masks on.

### Section 2: The Robbery
- They enter the general store.
- Jeremiah slips behind the counter — puts a gun to the clerk's head and a knife to his neck.
- The rest board up the doors.
- Joseph spots a **shotgun hidden under the counter** — grabs it and slides it across the floor to Lenny.
- Joseph opens the register and takes everything: dollars, tens, hundreds.
- Jeremiah grabs necessities (supplies, essentials) on the way.

### Section 3: The Shootout
- Word spreads fast in the small town.
- Shooting starts outside.
- The gang gets to the windows, breaks the glass, and fires back.
- They fight their way out of Hope.

### Section 4: The Ride Back
- Hard ride back to the Gorge.
- They barely make it.
- **Snow (Joseph's albino Mustang) gets shot in the leg** — injured but alive. Needs treatment.

### Section 5: Splitting the Score
- Total haul: **$3,000**
- Performance-based split system:

| Person | Cut | Reasoning |
|--------|-----|-----------|
| Camp Fund | $600 | 20% off the top — food, supplies, upkeep |
| Joseph | $500 | Led the heist, spotted the shotgun |
| Jeremiah | $450 | Controlled the clerk, grabbed extras |
| Clark | $350 | Boarded doors, held windows |
| Lenny | $350 | Fought at windows |
| Marcus | $300 | Solid work |
| Trevor | $250 | Support role |
| Phil | $200 | Backup |

- **Player's cut is flexible:** Do well = $500-550. Average = $450. Bare minimum = $300-350.

### Mission Complete

---

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Performance-based pay system:** The more work you do in a mission, the larger your share of the score.
- Default/average play gives a decent cut but others who did more get more.

## Key Story Points
- First real score for the gang
- Hope is a small town — word travels fast, making robberies risky
- Snow gets injured — Joseph has to treat his horse
- The gang is surviving but not thriving
