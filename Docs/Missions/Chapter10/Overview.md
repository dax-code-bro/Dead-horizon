# Chapter 10: "The Pieces Fall"

## Setting: Abandoned Town (divided) → Various
## Missions: 7
## Tone: Desperation. The gang is dying. Clark tightens his grip. The marshal closes in.

---

## Mission 1: "Without a Foundation"

- Charles is gone. The brain of the gang is buried on a hill.
- Joseph is worse than ever. Clark now controls him almost completely.
- Jeremiah tries to hold his side together but people are starting to talk about leaving.
- **Danny and Nell approach Jeremiah.** They want out. They're young. They haven't done enough to have serious bounties. They could disappear.

**Jeremiah:** "...Go. Take your horse. Head north. Don't stop until you hit Canada."

**Danny:** "Jerry, I—"

**Jeremiah:** "Go before I change my mind."

- They leave at night. Jeremiah watches them ride off. Two fewer people. The gang shrinks.

---

## Mission 2: "The Marshal's Gambit"

**Marshal Harding makes a bold move.** He doesn't attack — he negotiates.

- Harding rides to the town under a white flag. Alone. Just him and his horse.
- He asks to speak with Jeremiah. Face to face.
- Jeremiah meets him at the edge of town. Marcus on a rooftop with a rifle. Just in case.

**Harding:** "You're smarter than Walkns. Always have been."

**Jeremiah:** "What do you want."

**Harding:** "I want this to end. I'm tired. You're tired. Your people are tired."

**Jeremiah:** "Then stop hunting us."

**Harding:** "I can't do that. But I can offer terms. You surrender — just you — and I let everyone else disappear. New identities. Clean slates. The women and children never see the inside of a cell."

**Jeremiah:** "And Joseph?"

**Harding:** "Walkns is a sick man. Anyone can see that. He goes to a hospital, not a prison. I give you my word."

**Jeremiah:** "Your word. You shot Lenny in the head in a bank. You put a bullet near a five-year-old. Your word means nothing to me."

**Harding:** "That boy is alive because I aimed high. I don't kill children, Field. I'm not your gang."

- Jeremiah tells him no. Harding nods. Tips his hat. Rides away.
- But the offer sits in Jeremiah's mind. He could end all of this. His surrender for everyone's freedom.

---

## Mission 3: "The Rattlesnake"

**Arthur Bellamy strikes.** The Waterson heir sends a team to attack the gang's supply lines.

- The gang can't get food, medicine, or ammunition into the town.
- Jeremiah leads a breakout mission — fight through Bellamy's blockade.
- **Gameplay: Combat convoy mission.** Wagons loaded with supplies running a gauntlet of Bellamy's hired guns.
- They punch through but Silas Dunn takes a fatal shot. The religious man dies quoting scripture with his last breath.
- **Death count: Lenny, Trevor, Charles, Silas.**

---

## Mission 4: "Pillow Talk"

**Clark's most insidious move.**

- Clark sits with Joseph at night. Joseph is having a semi-lucid moment — just aware enough to be manipulated.

**Clark:** "Joseph. I need to tell you something. Something I should've told you a long time ago."

**Joseph:** "What."

**Clark:** "Jeremiah has been talking to the marshal. Behind your back. I've seen it."

**Joseph:** "...What?"

**Clark:** "He met with Harding at the edge of town. Just the two of them. And Marcus was there too — watching from a roof. Covering him."

- This is technically true. Jeremiah DID meet with Harding. Marcus WAS on the roof. Clark is using real events and twisting them.

**Joseph:** "Jerry... would never..."

**Clark:** "I didn't believe it either. But I saw it with my own eyes. He's making a deal, Joseph. Trading you for his freedom."

- Joseph's damaged mind can't sort truth from lies. The anger builds. Clark watches it build. Feeds it.

**Clark:** "I'll protect you, Joseph. I always have. I'm the only one who never left your side."

---

## Mission 5: "The Other Offer"

**Marcus comes to Jeremiah with a plan.**

**Marcus:** "Emily and I have been talking. We want you to come with us."

**Jeremiah:** "Come where."

**Marcus:** "Away. All of us. You, me, Emily, Josh. We take what money we have and we disappear. Start over."

**Jeremiah:** "And the rest of them?"

**Marcus:** "The gang is dead, Jerry. Look around. Charles is gone. Trevor is gone. Silas is gone. Danny and Nell left. Joseph doesn't know what year it is. What are we holding onto?"

**Jeremiah:** "I made a promise. To Charles. To Joseph. To everyone."

**Marcus:** "Charles is dead. And Joseph... Joseph isn't Joseph anymore."

**Jeremiah:** "I'm not leaving him."

**Marcus:** "He's not the man who raised you. That man died in a trolley in Las York."

- Jeremiah can't leave. Even now. Even after everything. The loyalty is too deep.
- Marcus understands. He's frustrated but he understands.

**Marcus:** "Then promise me something. When this is over — however it ends — you come find us."

**Jeremiah:** "...I promise."

---

## Mission 6: "Night Raid"

**Bellamy sends a night raid on the town.** Not the marshal — Bellamy's private army.

- **Defense mission.** Hold the town against Bellamy's mercenaries.
- The gang fights together — both sides. For one night, the division doesn't matter.
- Joseph picks up his M4 and fights. Confused but functional. Muscle memory.
- Jeremiah and Clark end up fighting side by side in the same building. Neither acknowledges the other. Just shooting.
- They hold the town. But more unnamed members fall. The gang is shrinking.

---

## Mission 7: "Embers"

**The aftermath.** The town is battered again. Fewer people.

- Jeremiah counts who's left. The numbers are grim.
- Phil's arm is still not 100%. Earl took a graze. Hector has a limp now.
- Joseph is sitting in the street after the battle. Still holding his M4. Luisa tries to take it. He won't let go.
- Clark sits next to Joseph. Takes the M4 gently. "I'll clean it for you." Joseph lets him.

**Jeremiah watches this from across the street.** Clark is replacing him. Taking care of the man who raised him. Holding his gun. Sitting in Jeremiah's spot.

- Emily finds Jeremiah.

**Emily:** "Marcus told me about his offer."

**Jeremiah:** "Emily—"

**Emily:** "I want you to know — there's a place for you. With us. Always. Josh asks about you every day. 'When is Uncle Jerry coming with us?'"

**Jeremiah:** "I can't leave yet."

**Emily:** "I know. Just... don't wait too long."

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 11"** appears.

- Marshal Harding offers terms: Jeremiah surrenders, everyone else goes free
- Arthur Bellamy blockades supply lines — Silas Dunn killed

- Marcus asks Jeremiah to leave with his family — Jeremiah refuses but promises to find them
- Bellamy's night raid — gang fights together briefly
- Clark physically replacing Jeremiah at Joseph's side
- Emily tells Jeremiah there's always a place with them
- **Deaths: Silas Dunn (Ch10)**
- **Total: Lenny, Trevor, Charles, Silas**
