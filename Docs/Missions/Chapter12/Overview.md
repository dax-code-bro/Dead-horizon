# Chapter 12: "Dead Man's Hand"

## Setting: Abandoned Town → Marshal's Territory
## Missions: 6
## Tone: Endgame for the old world. Jeremiah goes on the offensive.

---

## Mission 1: "Hunting the Hunter"

**Jeremiah decides to take the fight to Marshal Harding.**

- No more waiting. No more defending. The marshal has been hunting them since Chapter 2. Time to end it.
- Jeremiah, Phil, Hector, and Earl ride out to find the marshal's base of operations.
- **Gameplay: Tracking/investigation.** Follow leads through towns. Talk to people. Find where Harding operates from.
- They discover Harding runs his operation out of a fortified ranch — formerly owned by a state official. Armed to the teeth. Professional soldiers, not militia.

---

## Mission 2: "The Devil's Deal"

**Arthur Bellamy approaches Jeremiah.** A rider comes with a white flag.

- Bellamy wants to meet. Neutral ground.
- Jeremiah goes — alone. Against everyone's advice.
- Bellamy is polished. Calm. Business-like. The opposite of the Waterson mother's aristocratic cruelty.

**Bellamy:** "I'm not my employers, Mr. Field. The Watersons are dead. I'm a businessman who inherited their assets. I have no personal grudge against you."

**Jeremiah:** "Then why are you blockading us? Why did your men attack our town?"

**Bellamy:** "Because Marshal Harding pays well. And dead outlaws make good press for a man who wants to be governor."

**Jeremiah:** "Governor?"

**Bellamy:** "Harding isn't just a lawman, Field. He's a politician. Every outlaw he brings in is a vote. Your gang is his campaign."

- Bellamy offers a deal: he pulls his men back if Jeremiah gives him information on Harding's operation. Bellamy wants Harding gone too — Harding's been squeezing him for protection money.
- **Jeremiah accepts.** An alliance with the devil he knows against the devil he doesn't.

---

## Mission 3: "Planning the Impossible"

**Jeremiah plans the assault on Harding's ranch.**

- Using Bellamy's intelligence on Harding's security, Jeremiah maps out an attack plan.
- **Gameplay: Planning mission.** Study the map. Assign roles. Choose approach — night raid, dawn assault, or a distraction-based attack.
- Phil will handle explosives. Earl will take a sniper position. Hector handles infiltration.
- This is Jeremiah at his best — the Marine, the strategist, the leader Charles and Joseph trained.
- **But he's planning for fewer people than he's ever had.** Looking at the table and seeing all the empty chairs.

---

## Mission 4: "The Reckoning"

**The assault on Marshal Harding's ranch.**

- **Major combat mission.** The player leads the attack.
- Bellamy's information is good — they know the guard rotations, the weak points, the exits.
- Phil blows the front gate. Earl picks off the outer guards from distance. Hector slips in the back.
- Jeremiah pushes through the main building. Room by room. Soldiers everywhere.
- **The fight is brutal.** Harding's men are professionals, not farmers with guns.
- Phil goes down — not dead, but a serious leg wound. Sadie pulls him out.
- Nate takes a bullet to the shoulder but keeps fighting. The kid who looked up to Jeremiah is a man now.

**The final room.** Jeremiah kicks the door. Harding is there. Behind a desk. His badge on the table. A revolver in his hand. But he's not pointing it at Jeremiah. He's pointing it at his own chin.

**Harding:** "So this is how it ends."

**Jeremiah:** "Put the gun down, marshal."

**Harding:** "I killed that boy in the bank. Lenny. I remember his face. I remember all their faces."

**Jeremiah:** "You killed a lot of people."

**Harding:** "I enforced the law. In a world that forgot what law means."

**Jeremiah:** "You shot at a wagon with a child inside."

**Harding:** "And I'll answer for that. But not to you."

**PLAYER CHOICE:**

**[Let him do it]** — Harding pulls the trigger. Dies at his own desk. Jeremiah stands there. Watches. Walks out.

**[Stop him]** — Jeremiah knocks the gun away. "You don't get the easy way out." Harding is tied up. Left for whatever comes next.

**[Shoot him]** — Jeremiah draws the Cattleman. "You're right. Not to me. To Lenny." One shot.

---

## Mission 5: "Hollow Victory"

**The marshal is dealt with.** The 4th antagonist is gone.

- Jeremiah rides back. Phil on a stretcher behind a horse. The gang won.
- But Jeremiah feels nothing. No satisfaction. No relief.
- He sits in the town square. Alone. Three of the five antagonists are dead. The Watersons. Washington. Harding. The only threats left are Clark and whatever happens with Joseph.
- **Nate sits next to him.**

**Nate:** "We did it."

**Jeremiah:** "Yeah."

**Nate:** "So why don't you look happy?"

**Jeremiah:** "Because the people I'm still fighting are in that building across the street."

- He looks at Joseph's side of the town. Clark's silhouette in a window.

---

## Mission 6: "Letters Home"

**A quiet mission.** Jeremiah writes letters.

- To Marcus and Emily — telling them it's almost over. Telling them where to settle. Telling Marcus to be the man he became, not the man he was.
- To Josh — a short note. "You're the bravest person I ever met. Your daddy is going to take good care of you. Remember what I taught you about being scared — it just means you're paying attention."
- He doesn't send them yet. Puts them in his saddlebag next to the melted toy soldier.

**He writes one more.** To Joseph. It reads:

*"You were my father. You were the best man I ever knew. I'm sorry I wasn't enough to save you. Whatever happens next — I forgive you. Even for Sarah. Even for Thomas and Lily. I forgive you because hating you would mean hating everything you gave me. And you gave me everything. — Your son, Jerry"*

He folds it. Puts it in his coat pocket. Close to his heart.
