# Chapter 9: "The Last Bishop"

## Setting: Abandoned Town (divided) → Road
## Missions: 6
## Tone: Grief. Loss. The foundation crumbles.

---

## Mission 1: "The Other Side of the Street"

- Life in a divided camp. Jeremiah's people on one side, Joseph's on the other.
- **Gameplay: Camp interaction mission.** Talk to people on your side. Some are angry. Some are sad. Some want to leave entirely.
- Alma sits alone. She can't visit Charles's side because Charles is on Jeremiah's side — and she's on... actually, she'd be on Jeremiah's side with Charles.
- Nate asks Jeremiah if they'll ever be one gang again. Jeremiah can't answer.
- **Optional:** Walk to the dividing line. See Joseph's people on the other side. Ray waves at you from across the street but doesn't cross. Owen doesn't look up.
- Clark watches from a window. Always watching.

---

## Mission 2: "Old Bones"

**Charles and Jeremiah ride out together.** Just the two of them. Like old times.

- Charles says he wants to scout but really he just wants to ride.
- They ride through the countryside. Charles talks more than usual.
- He tells Jeremiah stories about his father — real stories, not the surface-level ones from before. How his father used to sing off-key. How he once got into a fistfight with a preacher. How he died with a smile on his face.

**Charles:** "Your old man died the way he lived — causing trouble and not apologizing for it."

**Jeremiah:** "Sounds familiar."

**Charles:** "I'll take that as a compliment."

- Charles coughs. Bad. He has to stop his horse. Takes a minute to recover.
- Jeremiah watches. Really watches this time.

**Jeremiah:** "Charles. What's wrong with you."

**Charles:** "Nothing a good night's sleep won't fix."

**Jeremiah:** "You've been coughing for months."

**Charles:** "I'm seventy-seven years old. Everything coughs at seventy-seven."

- He won't admit it. Jeremiah doesn't push. But he knows something is wrong.
- They find a nice overlook. Sit for a while. Watch the sun set.

**Charles:** "Jerry. Whatever happens from here — you're the best thing Joseph and I ever did."

**Jeremiah:** "Don't talk like that."

**Charles:** "I'll talk however I damn well please. I'm seventy-seven."

---

## Mission 3: "Quick Money"

**The divided gang still needs money.** Jeremiah plans a coach robbery on the road.

- Jeremiah, Marcus, Phil (arm healed), Hector, and Charles ride out.
- **Gameplay: Coach ambush.** Stop the coach, rob the passengers, take the strongbox.
- Clean job. No complications. They need a win and they get one.
- **But during the job, Charles takes a bullet to the ribs.** A guard got a shot off before Phil dropped him.
- Charles says nothing. Holds his side. Mounts his horse. Rides back with everyone.
- Nobody notices the blood soaking through his coat. It's dark. He keeps his hand pressed against it.

**On the ride back:**

**Marcus:** "Good job today. Clean."

**Charles:** "Not bad for an old man."

**Jeremiah:** "You sure you're alright? You're riding slow."

**Charles:** "I always ride slow. Snow would beat this horse in a coma."

- They laugh. Charles laughs too. It hurts. He doesn't show it.

---

## Mission 4: "The Longest Night"

**That night.**

- Charles walks to his tent. Slower than usual. His hand pressed against his ribs under his coat.
- He closes the tent flap.
- **If the player walks near Charles's tent, they hear:**
  - Heavy, labored breathing — worse than usual
  - The wet COPD cough mixed with something new — a gurgling
  - Shifting on the cot. Restless.
  - A muffled groan
  - The breathing getting shorter
  - A long exhale... silence... a sharp inhale
  - More struggling
- **The player CANNOT enter the tent.** The prompt doesn't appear.
- You can only listen.
- If you stand there long enough, the sounds slow. Stop. Start again. Stop longer. Start weaker.

**This is not a mission.** It's a required event that happens at night. The player can choose to stay and listen or walk away. Either way, morning comes.

---

## Mission 5: "Morning"

**Charles didn't wake up.**

- Alma is standing near his tent. She hasn't gone in.
- Jeremiah opens the flap.
- Charles is on his cot. On his side. Hand pressed against ribs — soaked in dried blood. Blanket dark red underneath. His worn 1911 on the ground. Eyes closed. Skin gray.

**Jeremiah:** "...Why didn't you say something."

- He sits on the floor. Holds Charles's hand.
- They find: blood-soaked rag he used to try and stop it, trail of blood drops from entrance to cot.
- Under his pillow: **"If you're reading this, I've stopped walking. Take care of each other. That's all I ever wanted. — Charles"**
- Joseph is brought to see. Has a brief moment of clarity: "You were supposed to outlive all of us, you stubborn old bastard." Then the fog returns. He forgets why he's crying.
- **The camp — both sides — mourns together.** For one morning, the divide doesn't exist. Everyone stands around Charles's tent. Ray crosses the street. Owen crosses. Even Clark stands at a respectful distance.

**Marcus to Jeremiah:** "He was shot yesterday. He didn't tell anyone."

**Jeremiah:** "Because he's Charles. That's why."

---

## Mission 6: "Burial"

**They bury Charles.**

- On the hill overlooking the town. Next to the two unnamed gang members from the battle.
- Jeremiah digs the grave himself. Won't let anyone help.
- **Gameplay: A slow, deliberate sequence.** The player digs. Each shovel of dirt. It takes time. There's no fast-forward. Just Jeremiah, a shovel, and the ground.
- People say words:
  - **Joseph** (lucid for this moment): "Charles was the first man who ever believed in me. Before I believed in myself. He taught me that strength isn't about what you can break — it's about what you can hold together."
  - **Alma:** She doesn't speak. She places a single flower on the grave. Sits beside it. The unspoken love finally visible to everyone.
  - **Jeremiah:** "He told me once — the day you stop walking is the day you start dying. He never stopped walking. Not even last night. He fought all the way to the end. Alone. Because that's who he was."
- They lower Charles's worn 1911 into the grave with him. The gun that was older than most of the gang.

**After the burial:**
- The gang returns to the town. The divide returns. People go back to their sides.
- But something has shifted. Joseph's side lost a founding member. The reality of mortality is settling in.
- Clark puts his hand on Joseph's shoulder. "I'm sorry about Charles." Joseph nods blankly.
- **Jeremiah is now truly alone in terms of father figures.** Joseph is gone mentally. Charles is gone physically. He's the eldest generation now.

## Chapter 9 Summary
- **Charles dies** — shot in the ribs during a coach robbery, bled out alone, hiding COPD
- The player can hear him dying through the tent wall but cannot enter
- His letter: "If you're reading this, I've stopped walking."
- Joseph's brief moment of clarity at Charles's body
- Jeremiah digs the grave alone — slow, deliberate gameplay
- The gang briefly reunites for the burial before splitting again
- **Alma's unspoken love revealed** — a flower on the grave
- Jeremiah has lost both father figures — Joseph mentally, Charles physically
- **Death count: Lenny (Ch3), Trevor (Ch5), Charles (Ch9)**
