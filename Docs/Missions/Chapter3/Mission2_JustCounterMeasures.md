# Chapter 3, Mission 2: Just Counter Measures

## Chapter: 3
## Location: The Hill Camp → Open Country → Waterfall Cave

---

## Setup
- Joseph wants a backup camp location scouted in case they need to move again.
- Jeremiah meets up with Marcus on a scouting route.

## Mission Start
- Player meets Marcus (icon: "MM") for a scouting ride.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Ride
- Jeremiah and Marcus ride out together through open country, scanning for potential camp sites.

### Section 2: The Conversation
- Deep conversation between Jeremiah and Marcus about their families.

**Marcus:** "You ever think about settling down? For real."

- Jeremiah opens up about his wife **Sarah** — met her in North Carolina while stationed there.
- They had two kids: **Thomas** (7) and **Lily** (4) when they died.
- Jeremiah came home from a routine patrol to find the house burned to ashes. Electrical fire. They died in their sleep.
- He kept Thomas's melted toy soldier — carries it in his saddlebag every day.

**Jeremiah:** "You've got what I had, Marcus. A woman who loves you. A boy who wants his father. And you're still here. They're still here. Don't waste that."

- Marcus shares progress with Josh — first real sword fight with sticks. Josh laughed with him for the first time.

**Marcus:** "He was laughing. First time he really laughed with me."

**Jeremiah:** "That's good, Marcus. That's really good."

- Marcus thanks Jeremiah for opening up.

**Marcus:** "You'd have been a hell of a father. You still are. To Josh. To all of us."

### Section 3: The Discovery
- They spot a wooded hillside with a creek running through the base.
- They ride down to investigate.
- They find a **waterfall**.
- Going behind the waterfall reveals a **hidden cave** — a secret area completely concealed from the outside.

### Section 4: Marking the Location
- Perfect backup camp: hidden entrance behind waterfall, fresh water source, natural cave shelter.
- Even better than the Gorge — you'd have to know it's there to find it.

### Mission Complete
