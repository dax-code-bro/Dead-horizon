# Chapter 3, Mission 6: Waterfall Arrival (Final Mission)

## Chapter: 3 (Final Mission)
## Location: The Hill Camp → Waterfall Cave

---

## Setup
- The gang has fled the Hill camp after the militia battle.
- Lenny is dead — left in the bank.
- Joseph has brain damage nobody knows about.
- Marcus has a shattered ankle and left arm.
- They ride through the forest at night to the waterfall cave Jeremiah and Marcus found during scouting.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Arrival at the Waterfall
- The gang arrives at the waterfall in silence.
- Jeremiah leads them behind the waterfall into the hidden cave.

### Section 2: Settling In
- Key dialogue moments as everyone processes the night:

**Jeremiah and Charles at the cave entrance:**
- Jeremiah: "Lenny's still in that bank."
- Charles: "The choice was him or all of us. He was already gone."

**Trevor alone in the back of the cave:**
- Haunted by touching Lenny's shoulder and walking away.
- "I should've stayed longer."

**Marcus and Emily:**
- Marcus: "He didn't even stop. Joseph ran right past him."
- Marcus: "I'm done following him blind, Emily."
- Marcus: "If it comes down to him or us — I'm choosing us."

**Joseph:**
- Disoriented, slumped against wall, Luisa holding his hand.
- Trying to talk about counting money. Luisa forces him to sit.

**Other couples:**
- Phil and Faye sit in silence. Neither loud for once.
- Mae takes Ray's bottle. "Not tonight." He agrees.

**Jeremiah alone:**
- Sits by the entrance. Takes out Thomas's melted toy soldier. Thinks about Thomas. Thinks about Lenny. Two people he couldn't save.

### Mission Complete — End of Chapter 3

---

## Camp 4: The Waterfall Cave
- Hidden behind a waterfall
- Secret entrance invisible from outside
- Fresh water source
- Spacious cave interior
- Camping setup: girls on east side, boys on west side, divided by medicine/provision wagons

## Camp Layout
- **East side (Women):** Half-tents with roofs only, sleep on the ground
- **West side (Men):** Full tents with walls and cots
- **Divider:** Medicine wagon, provision wagon, etc.
- **Later (Chapter 4):** Marcus moves Emily and Josh into his full tent

---

## Random Interaction: Marcus Sees the Immortal Strategy

*Marcus catches Joseph playing the Immortal chess strategy — sacrificing all pieces.*

- Marcus recognizes it. Confronts Joseph. Joseph brushes it off.
- Marcus takes Emily to a chess board and teaches her the strategy.
- He maps each piece to a gang member:
  - **Pawns:** Lenny (already dead), Nate, Danny, Ray — front line, first sacrificed
  - **Rooks:** Phil, Trevor — go after the pawns
  - **Knights:** Marcus, Clark — go after the rooks
  - **Bishops:** Jeremiah, Charles — last before the end
  - **Queen:** Luisa — the only piece Joseph protects
  - **King:** Joseph — doesn't care about himself
- Marcus tells Emily: "If something happens to me, take Josh and run."
- Marcus doesn't know how to tell Jeremiah — Jerry would never believe Joseph would sacrifice him.
- Emily promises.

---

## Key Story Points
- The gang is grieving Lenny
- Third camp lost — now at waterfall cave
- Marcus's loyalty has fully shifted from Joseph to his family
- Marcus understands Joseph's Immortal strategy — knows who's next
- Trevor is haunted by leaving Lenny
- Joseph is deteriorating — can't focus, disoriented
- The gang's faith in Joseph is cracking
- Jeremiah still loyal to Joseph but carrying grief
