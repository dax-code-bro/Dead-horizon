# Chapter 3, Mission 5: (Name TBD — Bank Robbery / Lenny's Death)

## Chapter: 3 (Second to last mission)
## Location: The Hill Camp → Big City → The Hill Camp → Waterfall Cave

---

## Setup
- No one has had time to rest after the trolley heist ($250,000).
- Marcus has a shattered ankle and left arm.
- Joseph — secretly suffering brain damage from the trolley crash — pushes for an immediate bank robbery.
- No preparation, no proper measures. Everyone tries to stop him. He refuses.
- Everyone is tired and just wants to get it over with.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Bank Robbery
- The gang rides into the big city.
- Jeremiah causes a distraction.
- Everyone moves in behind him.
- A man reaches for a gun — Joseph blasts him before his finger touches the trigger.
- Phil kicks down the bank door.
- There's a second door Phil can't get past — he uses his shotgun to break the glass and hops through. Everyone follows.
- Phil tosses dynamite into the vault room without thinking.
- Cash spills everywhere — visible even from behind the door.
- Joseph runs in, everyone stuffs their bags to the brim.
- They make three trips carrying money out.

### Section 2: Lenny's Death
- The old lawman from the saloon appears — gun pointed at Lenny's head.
- "If you move one step, I'll put a bullet through his head."
- Nobody moves.
- Joseph, Clark, Jeremiah, and Phil all try to shoot — they hit the officer in the arm.
- The officer starts laughing. Hand on Lenny's shoulder.
- He pushes Lenny out into the middle of the bank. Everyone watching.
- **"This was a warning."**
- He shoots Lenny.
- Lenny grunts, moans, stumbles forward, crouches, collapses on the ground wheezing.

### Section 3: The Reactions
Each character's response to Lenny's body:
- **Clark** — runs right past. Doesn't stop.
- **Joseph** — runs right past. Doesn't stop. (His "child" dying and he doesn't even pause.)
- **Charles** — crouches down briefly. Pays respects. Leaves.
- **Trevor** — touches Lenny's shoulder. Starts to crouch. Stands back up. Walks backward for a few seconds looking at him. Turns and runs.
- **Jeremiah** — stays the longest. Already knows he's dead. Can't walk away. Eventually forces himself to leave.

### Section 4: The Escape
- Joseph refuses to drop the money at first.
- Eventually tells the slower members to drop their bags.
- They mount their horses and gallop through the city.
- Barely make it out — a whole militia chasing them.
- **They lead the militia straight back to the Hill camp.**

### Section 5: Battle at the Hill
- Full-scale battle at camp. Everyone fighting — men, women, everyone.
- They kill most of the militia.
- The old lawman is left with a small force. He decides enough is enough and retreats.

### Section 6: Evacuation
- Camp is compromised. Third camp lost.
- Everyone immediately begins packing up in the middle of the night.
- **Marcus — without thinking — grabs Josh and pulls Emily out of bed.** Pure instinct. Family first. The major shift.
- They evacuate to the **waterfall cave** that Jeremiah and Marcus found during scouting.

---

## Key Story Points
- **Lenny is dead** — killed by the old lawman as a warning
- **Joseph's brain damage driving reckless decisions** — no prep, no planning, wouldn't drop the money
- **Joseph ran past Lenny's body** — the man who calls the gang "his children" didn't stop
- **Marcus's major shift** — grabbed Josh and Emily first without thinking. Instinct over hesitation.
- **Third camp lost** — the Hill is gone
- **The old lawman** — recurring threat, made good on his saloon threat, retreated but will return
- **The gang relocates to the waterfall cave** — the backup location from Mission 2

1. Chapter 2: Sanity cracks when Emily talks him out of the lawman plan
2. Chapter 2: Trolley crash — brain damage (secret)

4. Chapter 3: Refuses to drop money while being chased
5. Chapter 3: Runs past Lenny's dying body without stopping

- **Chapter 1:** Completely distant
- **Chapter 2:** Small shift — fished with Josh, held Emily's hand
