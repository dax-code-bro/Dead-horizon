# Chapter 3, Mission 4: He's At It Again

## Chapter: 3
## Location: The Hill Camp → Las York (Big City) → River

---

## Setup
- No one has rested after the Waterson horse theft.
- Joseph — suffering secret brain damage — pushes for an immediate trolley robbery.
- No preparation, no proper measures. Everyone tries to stop him. He refuses.
- Target: A Waterson bank trolley. Heavily armored, heavily guarded, carrying a massive amount of cash.
- Waterson's Stabling Co. covers: transportation, oil, foundations, ranching.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Approach
- The gang waits behind a corner in Las York.
- Cinematic zoom on the trolley as it passes — Waterson's cow logo in red letters on the side.
- Jeremiah hops onto the back, grabs a handle. Helps Joseph on. Marcus climbs on top.
- Others secretly follow the trolley.

### Section 2: Breaching
- Marcus uses a highly illegal lock brake to crack the top hatch.
- Marcus drops inside — flashes of yellow as gunfire erupts.
- Others surround and enter the trolley.
- Interior: cramped but long. 11 seats. Boxes above each seat filled with belongings.
- Large safe at the very back.

### Section 3: The Vault
- Phil tosses dynamite into the vault room without thinking.
- Explosion blows out the entire back of the trolley.
- The safe falls off — but it's open. Cash everywhere.
- They knock out the driver.

### Section 4: The Escape
- Cops coming fast. They grab the money and load back into the trolley.
- **Gameplay: Jeremiah drives.** Player navigates through the city losing cops.
- Jeremiah isn't paying attention — the trolley goes off a bridge into the water.

### Section 5: The Crash
- Trolley sinks in shallow water. Fills up.
- The trolley protected them from the fall impact — would have killed them otherwise.
- **Joseph slams into the wall — BRAIN DAMAGE** (secret cause of his mental decline going forward)
- **Marcus shatters his ankle and left arm**
- Cinematic: Joseph holds his head, bleeding. Tells them to grab the money.
- Everyone makes it to shore. Joseph bleeding. Phil dragging bags of cash.

### Section 6: The Score
- **Total haul: $250,000**
- Biggest score the gang has ever pulled.

---

## Key Story Points
- Joseph's brain damage occurs here — the trolley crash is the secret origin
- Nobody knows about the brain damage yet — they think it's just a head wound
- Marcus re-injured — shattered ankle and left arm
- The $250,000 gives Joseph confidence for the bank robbery (Mission 5)
- Phil finally almost got to use dynamite properly
- The Watersons are hit for the second time — trolley + horses
