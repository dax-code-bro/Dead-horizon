# Chapter 3, Mission 1: The One Thing We Have in Common

## Chapter: 3
## Location: The Hill Camp → Waterson Ranch → Forest → Clearing (Buyers)

---

## Setup
- The gang is broke — $3,000 from the Hope robbery went fast.
- Joseph devised a plan with **Barry**, a man who wants the Watersons to fall because they blackmailed his family.
- Barry bribed one of the Waterson guards to give the gang access to the stables.
- The Watersons are an innocent, powerful family — 500,000 acres, 50,234 guards, a legitimate empire called **Waterson's Stabling Co.**
- **The Watersons become the second antagonist because Joseph chose to rob them.**

## Mission Start
- Player walks up to Joseph's tent (icon: "JW").
- Joseph is inside with Charles, Marcus, and Clark, planning the heist.
- Joseph spots Jeremiah and invites him in.
- Player can accept or decline the mission. Declining doesn't remove it — can return later.
- Jeremiah is told to gather some of the boys for the mission.
- They meet in the center of camp and saddle up.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Riding to the Ranch
- The crew rides out to the Waterson ranch.

### Section 2: Infiltration (Stealth)
- They ride up toward the mansion gate but slip off into the forest beside it.
- There's a separate side gate leading to the stables.
- The bribed guard lets them through.
- **Stealth gameplay:** Player must stay quiet. Must knife-kill a few guards to reach the stables.

### Section 3: The Stable Owner
- A man spots the gang inside the stables — the **stable owner**.
- He thinks they're just buyers. He's friendly, relaxed.
- He tells them about the premium horses — their names and facts:
  - **Red Tree** — Red Arabian
  - **Sea** — Blue Roan Thoroughbred
  - **Silver** — Gray American Paint
  - **Glacier** — White Turkoman
- After he finishes talking, the gang **stealth kills him**.
- This is an innocent man just doing his job.

### Section 4: Identifying the Horses
- There are other generic horses in the stables that look like copies of the premium ones.
- Each premium horse has a **tag around its neck**.
- Joseph tells them: grab anything with **first number 3, last letter A**.
- They check tags and identify the four premium horses.

### Section 5: Leading the Horses Out
- Each gang member leads a horse out:
  - **Joseph** — leads **Glacier** (White Turkoman)
  - **Marcus** — leads **Silver** (Gray American Paint)
  - **Trevor** — leads **Red Tree** (Red Arabian)
  - **Jeremiah** — leads **Sea** (Blue Roan Thoroughbred)
- They lead them to the side gate where **Barry** has their own horses ready at a hitching post.
- They tie the stolen horses to their own horses and mount up.

### Section 6: Barry's Death
- Right as they pay Barry, a **shot rings out**.
- Barry collapses — **half his head blown off** by a Waterson guard.
- The shot startles the horses.
- **Glacier breaks the rope and runs off into the forest.**

### Section 7: The Chase
- Jeremiah tells the others to run — he'll get Glacier.
- The others escape with the remaining three horses.
- **Gameplay: Tracking sequence** — Glacier runs into the forest and Jeremiah loses him.
- Player must track Glacier through the forest.
- Jeremiah finds Glacier, pats his neck to calm him down.
- A guard shoots at them — **scares off Cerberus** (Jeremiah's horse).
- Jeremiah mounts Glacier and rides out.

### Section 8: The Sale
- Jeremiah regroups with the gang in a clearing.
- Four buyers are standing at a gate, ready to purchase.
- **PLAYER CHOICE:**
  - **Keep one of the stolen horses** — reduced pay for the gang
  - **Sell all four** — full $25,000 payout

**Horse values:**
| Horse | Price |
|-------|-------|
| Glacier (White Turkoman) | $8,000 |
| Red Tree (Red Arabian) | $7,000 |
| Sea (Blue Roan Thoroughbred) | $5,500 |
| Silver (Gray American Paint) | $4,500 |
| **Total** | **$25,000** |

### Section 9: Post-Mission
- Cerberus is not lost — player can return to the forest and whistle for him at any time.
- Smart players can keep a horse AND retrieve Cerberus later, building their stable.

### Mission Complete

---

## New Characters
- **Barry** — Had a vendetta against the Watersons for blackmailing his family. Met Joseph at a saloon. Served as the inside man. **Killed during the escape** — shot by a Waterson guard.
- **The Stable Owner** — Innocent employee at the Waterson ranch. Killed by the gang during the theft.

## Antagonist Introduction
- **The Watersons** — Second main antagonist.
  - 500,000 acres across the state
  - **Waterson's Stabling Co.** — legitimate business empire
  - **50,234+ guards/hired people** — hundreds of times bigger than the Washington gang
  - Family of 9: the mother (controls most of it), the father, and 7 sons
  - **Completely innocent** — they became villains because Joseph chose to rob them
  - They are now aware someone stole their premium horses

## Game Systems Introduced
- **Mission accept/decline system** — walk up to character icon, can accept or decline, mission remains available
- **Stealth gameplay** — knife kills, staying quiet, avoiding detection
- **Horse tracking** — following a runaway horse through terrain
- **Player choice** — keep a horse (reduced pay) or sell all (full pay)
- **Horse stable system** — 11 slots, 3 active horses at a time, buy saddles/equipment/provisions

## Key Story Points
- Joseph's declining judgment continues — robbing an innocent empire
- Barry died helping them — consequences of Joseph's plan
- An innocent stable owner killed — the gang is not heroic
- The Watersons will retaliate — the gang just made a powerful enemy
- Cerberus temporarily lost but can be recovered
