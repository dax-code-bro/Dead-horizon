# Mission 4: A New, Better Place

## Chapter: 1 (Final Mission)
## Location: Old Camp → Road → The Gorge (New Camp)

---

## Setup
- Joseph announces the gang needs to move — the Washington gang could retaliate and the law could be closing in after the raid.
- Everyone loads up onto wagons and horses.
- Emily helps Marcus into the back of a wagon. Joseph drives. Josh rides with them.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Journey Begins
- The gang moves out as a convoy — wagons and riders.

**Joseph:** "Alright, listen up! We keep moving until I say we stop. No one falls behind, no one wanders off."

**Clark:** *(riding alongside the wagon)* "Where exactly are we headed, Joseph?"

**Joseph:** "Somewhere they won't find us."

**Clark:** "That's not exactly a plan."

**Joseph:** "It's the only one you're getting. Now ride."

### Section 2: Emily and Marcus in the Wagon

**Emily:** "You need to keep this elevated. Trevor said—"

**Marcus:** "I know what Trevor said."

**Emily:** "Then stop moving it."

**Josh:** "Mama, is daddy okay?"

**Emily:** "He's fine, baby. He's just resting."

**Marcus:** "...Kid's right there, Emily. Don't talk about me like I'm not here."

**Emily:** "Then talk to him yourself."

**Emily:** *(quieter)* "How long were they... how long did they have you?"

**Marcus:** "Long enough."

**Emily:** "What did they want?"

**Marcus:** "Information. About the gang. About Joseph."

**Emily:** "And you didn't—"

**Marcus:** "I didn't say a word. You think I'd sell out my own people?"

**Emily:** "That's not what I meant. I just... I was scared, Marcus. I thought you were dead."

**Marcus:** "...Well I'm not."

**Emily:** "I know. I just... when they told me you were gone, I couldn't breathe. I sat in that tent holding Josh and I couldn't breathe."

**Emily:** "I can't do this without you. I know you think I can, but I can't."

**Marcus:** "You were doing just fine before they brought me back."

**Emily:** "No I wasn't. I was surviving. That's not the same thing."

**Marcus:** *(barely above a whisper)* "...I didn't think anyone was coming for me."

**Emily:** "Jerry would've burned that whole town down for you. You know that."

**Marcus:** "Yeah. Jerry."

**Emily:** "Don't do that. Don't make this about him. I'm the one sitting here. I'm the one who hasn't left your side."

**Marcus:** "...I know."

**Emily:** *(softer)* "Do you? Because sometimes I don't think you do."

*(Josh tugs on Emily's sleeve.)*

**Josh:** "Mama, I'm cold."

**Emily:** *(pulling Josh close)* "Come here, baby."

### Section 3: Phil Takes Josh

**Phil:** *(riding alongside, leans into the wagon)* "Hey, little man! Josh! How about you come ride with me and your Uncle Jeremiah for a while?"

**Josh:** *(looking up at Emily)* "Can I, mama?"

**Emily:** *(hesitates, then nods, smoothing his hair)* "Go on. Stay close to Jerry, okay?"

**Phil:** *(lifting Josh out)* "There we go. Your parents need a little time to talk grown-up stuff."

**Phil:** *(riding up to Jeremiah)* "Got a passenger for you."

**Jeremiah:** *(looks down at Josh, slight smile)* "Well. Riding with the big guys now, huh?"

**Josh:** "Uncle Jerry, have you ever seen a bear?"

**Jeremiah:** "A bear? Yeah. A few."

**Josh:** "Were you scared?"

**Jeremiah:** "...You know what, I was. But that's okay. Being scared just means you're paying attention."

**Josh:** "Daddy doesn't get scared."

**Jeremiah:** *(glances back at the wagon)* "...Yeah he does, kid. He just doesn't like to show it."

### Section 4: Marcus and Emily Alone

**Emily:** "He calls Jerry 'uncle.' You know that, right?"

**Marcus:** "I know."

**Emily:** "He'd call you 'daddy' more if you let him."

**Marcus:** "Emily—"

**Emily:** "I'm not pushing. I'm just... I'm telling you the door is open, Marcus. It's always been open. You just have to walk through it."

*(Marcus closes his eyes. His hand shifts slightly — just barely touching Emily's. She looks down at it. She doesn't grab it. She just lets it stay.)*

### Section 5: Arrival at the Gorge
- The wagon stops before the moment can go further.
- Everyone gets out.
- They're in an empty grass clearing leading into a gorge.

**THE GORGE CAMP:**
- A narrow canyon path leads into the gorge — natural chokepoint, easy to defend.
- At the back of the gorge: a deep cave.
- Inside the cave: sleeping quarters, armory, leadership area, supplies.
- The gorge widens into a clearing: stables, fire pit, common area, daily camp life.
- Wooden walls and fences built on the open side for defense.

### Section 6: Settling In
- Everyone begins setting up the new camp.
- Emily gets Marcus settled into a spot in the cave.
- Luisa is talking to Josh — caring for him while the adults work.
- Jeremiah and Joseph sit down together and have a smoke.
- A quiet moment of peace.

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 2"** appears on screen.

### Mission Complete — End of Chapter 1

---

## Camp Details: The Gorge
- **Location:** Grass clearing leading into a narrow gorge with a deep cave.
- **Defense:** Narrow canyon entrance acts as a natural chokepoint. Only needs a few guards.
- **Cave (Interior Camp):** Sleeping quarters, armory, supply storage, leadership tents. Lanterns hung from rock walls.
- **Gorge Clearing (Exterior Camp):** Stables, wagons, fire pit, cooking area, common gathering space.
- **Lookout:** Post on top of the hill/cliff above the cave entrance.

## Key Story Points
- Marcus and Emily's closest emotional moment yet — hand touch — interrupted by arrival.
- The gang has left their original camp permanently.
- Josh bonds with Jeremiah ("Uncle Jerry") and Phil during the ride.
- Joseph's destination is the gorge — a defensible, hidden location.
- Chapter 1 ends on a quiet, hopeful note.
