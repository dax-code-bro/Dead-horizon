## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Walk
- Player follows Joseph through the blizzard.
- They are lost, returning from a supply run, trying to find camp.

**During the walk — Joseph and Jeremiah talk about the train heist:**

**Joseph:** "...Still can't believe Bond's gone."

**Jeremiah:** "He held on longer than anyone expected."

**Joseph:** "Three days with a bullet in his arm. Three days. And Marcus falls off a damn train with a hole in his side and somehow he's still breathing."

**Jeremiah:** "Marcus is stubborn."

**Joseph:** "Stubborn doesn't cover it. Bond was tough. Tougher than Marcus. Tougher than most of us. And he's in the ground."

**Jeremiah:** "It doesn't make sense. It never does."

**Joseph:** "Davis goes off a cliff. Bond bleeds out. Maria... Maria gets choked to death ten feet from me. Finn takes five bullets and the law puts him down like a dog. And Jamie..."

*Joseph looks around at the blizzard. The same storm.*

**Joseph:** "Jamie's out here somewhere. In this. And we just keep walking."

**Jeremiah:** "We can't go back for him, Joseph."

**Joseph:** "I know. That's what makes it worse."

**Jeremiah:** "Marcus survived. Phil got him out."

**Joseph:** "Phil carried him on his back for two miles. You know that?"

**Jeremiah:** "I know."

**Joseph:** "Five dead. Marcus half dead. And for what? A train full of money we didn't even get."

**Jeremiah:** "We got out. That counts for something."

**Joseph:** "Does it? Because right now I'm walking through a blizzard with no food, no shelter, and five fewer people than I started with. Tell me what that counts for."

*Silence. Just wind and snow.*

**Jeremiah:** "It counts because we're still here. And because the people who are left need you to keep walking."

**Joseph:** *(long pause)* "...Yeah. Keep walking."

### Section 2: The Tension
- Footsteps are heard approaching.
- Jacob grabs a 1911 pistol and tosses it behind him to Jeremiah.
- **Cutscene/Animation:** Jeremiah catches the 1911, takes out the magazine to check it's loaded, cycles the slide, and aims into the blizzard.

### Section 3: Clark Arrives
- Clark Jones emerges from the blizzard in his white coat, walking confidently.
- Clark: "You lost?"
- Jeremiah and Jacob sigh in relief.
- Clark tells them to follow him — he brought their horses.

### Section 4: Objective — Ride Back to Camp
- **OBJECTIVE: Ride back to camp**
- Player mounts a horse and rides with Clark and Jacob back to camp.

### Section 5: Arrival at Camp
- Luisa Walkns greets Jacob with a warm hug.
- Trevor takes Jeremiah and Clark to get warmed up and back in shape.

### Section 6: Jeremiah's Tent
- Jeremiah is taken to a tent with a small wooden bed.
- He sits down, grabs a gun, and starts cleaning it.
- Marcus McAllen walks in — Jeremiah's brother in arms, 26. He's moving slow. His side is still bandaged from the train heist bullet.

**Marcus:** "Jerry."

**Jeremiah:** "You should be resting."

**Marcus:** "I've been resting for days. I'm going out of my mind."

**Jeremiah:** "You fell off a train, Marcus."

**Marcus:** "And Bond got shot in the arm and died three days later. I got shot in the side and I'm standing here. How's that fair?"

**Jeremiah:** "It's not."

**Marcus:** "Davis. Bond. Maria. Finn. Jamie. All dead. And I'm the one who lives? The one who fell off the back of a moving train?"

**Jeremiah:** "Phil carried you two miles so you could have this conversation. Don't waste it feeling guilty."

**Marcus:** "I don't feel guilty. I feel... I don't know what I feel."

**Jeremiah:** "You feel alive. That's enough for now."

### Section 7: Chores
- Clark tells Jeremiah ("Jerry") that Jacob wants them to do some chores.
- **OBJECTIVE: Do camp chores**
  - Brush the horses
  - Feed the horses
  - Organize camp supplies
