# Mission 3: Fine, We'll Get Him Back

## Chapter: 1
## Location: Camp → Washington Gang Town → Camp

---

## Setup
- Trevor has found the location where Marcus is being held.
- War table meeting in the stables: Joseph, Jeremiah, Clark, and Trevor.
- Clark hands Jeremiah an AKM and ammo.
- They saddle up: Jeremiah on Cerberus, Joseph on Snow, Clark on Gunpowder. Trevor rides with the group.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Ride
- The group rides out toward the Washington gang's location.
- Tense dialogue on the way:

**Clark:** "So what's the plan when we get there? We just ride in shooting?"

**Joseph:** "You'd like that, wouldn't you."

**Clark:** "I'm just saying — they took one of ours. That's not something you answer with a polite conversation."

**Jeremiah:** "And getting ourselves killed doesn't get Marcus home either."

**Clark:** "Always the optimist, Jerry."

**Trevor:** "I counted at least a dozen men when I was scouting. Could be more inside."

**Joseph:** "Then we do this smart. We watch, we wait, and when we see our moment — we take it."

**Jeremiah:** "You alright, Joseph?"

**Joseph:** *(pause)* "...These people took something from me a long time ago. Now they've taken another. I'm not losing anyone else."

### Section 2: The Town
- They see smoke in the distance.
- They ride up — it's a small town packed with Washington gang members.
- They see a house. Two members are dragging Marcus between buildings — blood all over him, beaten and tortured. They bring him into another house.
- Joseph changes the plan — forget stealth, they're going in.

### Section 3: The Assault (Major Combat)
- They walk up to the entrance.
- Joseph holds his gun up with one hand, fires two bursts into the air.
- Two guards at the front immediately raise their weapons.
- **Player takes control.**
- **COMBAT ENCOUNTER:** ~100 Washington gang members.
- Fight through the town toward the house where Marcus is held.

### Section 4: The Rescue
- Joseph kicks down the door.
- Two members inside — Jeremiah drops one, Clark drops the other.
- In the back of the room: Marcus hanging from two chains around his wrists. Blood pooled beneath him.
- Marcus looks up: *"It's about time."*
- They shoot off the chains. Marcus drops.
- He tries to stand — his right leg is shattered. He can't.
- Clark and Trevor help carry him out to the horses.
- Marcus is placed on Trevor's horse (too injured to ride — slumped over). Trevor walks alongside.

### Section 5: The Ride Back

**Clark:** "He doesn't look good."

**Jeremiah:** "He's breathing. That's enough for now."

**Joseph:** "Trevor, how bad?"

**Trevor:** *(checking Marcus while walking)* "Right leg's done. Both hands are swelling up bad. He's burning up — fever's setting in."

**Joseph:** "Will he make it?"

**Trevor:** "...If we get him back tonight, maybe. He needs rest, clean water, and something for that fever. I can set the leg but it's not gonna be pretty."

*(Marcus groans, barely conscious.)*

**Jeremiah:** "Hey. Stay with us, Marcus. You hear me?"

**Marcus:** *(weak, slurred)* "...Told them... nothing..."

**Jeremiah:** "I know you didn't. Just keep breathing."

**Clark:** "Tough bastard."

*(Silence. Just hooves on snow.)*

**Joseph:** "Washington's gonna answer for this. Every last one of them."

**Jeremiah:** "One thing at a time. Let's get him home."

### Section 6: Return to Camp
- Phil and Jeff help get Marcus off the horse.
- They try to put him down on his right side.
- Jeremiah: *"Hey! Idiots! Watch his leg! His right leg is shattered, put him down on his left!"*
- Emily pushes Phil and Jeff out of the way, kisses Marcus on his cheek, hugs him.

**Emily:** *(voice cracking)* "Oh God... Marcus... I'm here. I'm right here."

**Emily:** "What did they do to you..."

*(She turns to Jeremiah, tears streaming.)*

**Emily:** "Thank you. Thank you, Jerry. Thank you so much."

*(She looks at Joseph, Clark, Trevor.)*

**Emily:** "Thank you. All of you. I mean it. Thank you."

**Walking with Marcus toward the barn:**

**Emily:** "You're gonna be okay. You hear me? You're gonna be fine. I'm not going anywhere."

**Emily:** "Josh has been asking about you. Every single day. 'Where's daddy?' Every day, Marcus."

**Emily:** *(wiping her eyes)* "Don't you ever do this to me again. Don't you ever leave us again."

*(Marcus manages to lift his hand slightly toward her. She grabs it, careful of his broken fingers.)*

**Emily:** "I got you. I always got you."

### Section 7: The Barn
- Marcus is laid on a bed in the barn where other gang members are.
- Trevor bandages him up, sets his broken leg.

**Trevor:** "Hold still. This leg needs to be set before I wrap it."

**Marcus:** *(through gritted teeth)* "Just do it."

**Emily:** "Can I get you anything? Water, food—"

**Marcus:** "I'm fine, Emily."

**Emily:** "You're not fine. Look at you."

**Marcus:** "I said I'm fine."

**Jeremiah:** "Let her help you, Marcus."

**Marcus:** *(looks away)* "...Water. Water's fine."

*(Emily goes to get water, then turns back.)*

**Emily:** "Josh wanted to see you. He's been worried sick."

**Marcus:** "...He doesn't need to see me like this."

**Emily:** "He needs to see his father."

*(Emily brings Josh in. The five-year-old stands in the doorway.)*

**Josh:** *(quiet, small voice)* "...Daddy?"

**Marcus:** "...Hey, kid."

**Josh:** "Are you hurt?"

**Marcus:** "Just a scratch. I'm alright."

**Josh:** "Mama said you were gone."

**Marcus:** "...Yeah. But I'm back now."

**Josh:** "Are you gonna leave again?"

*(The barn goes silent.)*

**Marcus:** "...Go on back to your mama, Josh."

*(Josh walks back to Emily. She picks him up, looks at Marcus with hurt in her eyes, and walks out.)*

**Jeremiah:** "You know he rode Trevor's horse halfway here to see you."

**Marcus:** "Don't start, Jerry."

**Jeremiah:** "I'm not starting anything. I'm just telling you what I saw."

*(Marcus closes his eyes.)*

**Trevor:** "Get some sleep. You're gonna need it."

- Emily thanks everyone again for their help.

### Mission Complete
