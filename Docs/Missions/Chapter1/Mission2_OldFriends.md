# Mission 2: Old Friends, and Time with an Old Mentor

## Chapter: 1
## Location: Camp → Wilderness → Camp

---

## Setup
- The gang hasn't eaten in a while. Joseph assigns tasks:
  - **Marcus and Clark** — scout the Washington gang's camp
  - **Joseph and Jeremiah** — go hunting for food

### Before Departure
- Joseph and Jeremiah go to tell Marcus and Clark about the scouting trip.
- As Marcus is about to grab his gun, Emily stops them — reluctant to let Marcus go.
- Marcus brushes her off and gets on his horse (Jim).
- Marcus and Clark saddle up and ride out.
- Joseph takes Jeremiah to the stables to get Cerberus. Joseph rides Snow.
- The two groups split.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Ride
- Joseph and Jeremiah ride out together, talking along the way.
- Bonding time between mentor and "son."

### Section 2: The Clearing
- They ride into a small clearing.
- Jeremiah puts his hand up, stopping Joseph dead in his tracks — Joseph wasn't paying attention.
- In the clearing: a male deer and several females, plus a pack of wolves nearby.
- They decide to go for the wolves — more meat, and they want to let the deer reproduce for future hunts.

### Section 3: The Shot (Gameplay)
- Jeremiah dismounts Cerberus and sets up on a rock with Joseph.
- Joseph hands Jeremiah his M4.
- **Player takes control of the weapon.**
- **FAIL CONDITION:** Shooting at the deer or scaring away the animals fails the mission (checkpoint restart).
- Jeremiah takes a clean shot — drops a wolf instantly.
- Joseph: *"I always knew you were a natural."* — pats Jeremiah on the back.

### Section 4: The Charge
- They stand up, but something's wrong — one wolf isn't running.
- It charges straight at them at full speed.
- Before they can react, it's right on them.
- Joseph pulls his pistol and drops it with two shots.

### Section 5: Loading Up
- They ride into the clearing on their horses.
- Joseph takes two large metal plates and tin foot rope off Snow.
- They build a makeshift sled attached to the back of the horse.
- Load both wolves onto the sled.

### Section 6: The Hill (Gameplay)
- **OBJECTIVE: Transport the wolves back to camp**
- Slow journey back — must ride up a hill to reach camp.
- **FAIL CONDITIONS:**
  - Go too fast on horse → rope snaps
  - Sharp turns → rope breaks or wolf slides off the edge of the hill
- Player must be steady and patient.

### Section 7: Return to Camp
- They arrive back at camp. Something's wrong — everyone is acting strange.
- The gang fills them in: **Marcus has been captured** during the scouting mission.
- Emily is furious, blaming Clark for getting Marcus captured.
- Clark tries to fight Emily — he's not the type to take blame.
- Jeremiah breaks them up and reassures Emily they're going to find Marcus.

### Mission Complete

---

## Optional Camp Interactions (Post-Mission 2)
- **Emily** — Very distant from the rest of the gang. Constantly urges Jeremiah to go back for Marcus. Pushy and desperate. Available if player stays around camp before starting Mission 3.

---

## Horses
- **Cerberus** — Jeremiah's horse
- **Snow** — Joseph's horse
- **Jim** — Marcus's horse

## Key Story Points
- **Michael Washington** — leader of the Washington gang. Old rival of Joseph. They go way back — constantly stealing scores from each other. Michael killed Joseph's brother.
- Marcus captured by the Washington gang during scouting.

## Gameplay Mechanics Introduced
- Shooting (M4 on rock — scoped/steady aim)
- Fail conditions (shooting wrong target, scaring animals)
- Transport mechanic (sled behind horse, speed/turn management)
- Checkpoint system on failure
