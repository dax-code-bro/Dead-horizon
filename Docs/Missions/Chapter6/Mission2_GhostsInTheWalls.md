# Chapter 6, Mission 2: Ghosts in the Walls

## Chapter: 6
## Location: Forest Camp → Abandoned Town

---

## Setup
- Clark suggests the gang move to an abandoned town he "found" while scouting.
- Marcus and Jeremiah know this could be another trap — but the forest camp is unsustainable.
- They agree to go but Jeremiah sends Marcus ahead secretly to check it out first.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Marcus Scouts Ahead (Playable as Marcus)
- **Second playable Marcus moment.**
- Marcus rides ahead of the gang to the abandoned town.
- The town is real — empty buildings, overgrown streets, a church with a broken steeple.
- Marcus searches for signs of a trap: fresh tracks, hidden positions, supplies that shouldn't be there.
- **He finds nothing.** The town is genuinely abandoned. No trap this time.
- But he finds something else — letters in the old post office. Addressed to the marshal. From Clark. Dated months ago.
- Clark has been writing to the law from every town they've been near. Marcus takes the letters.

**This is the physical proof they've needed.**

### Section 2: The Gang Arrives
- Marcus rides back and meets the gang on the road. Tells Jeremiah "it's clean" loudly — then whispers "I found something" privately.
- The gang moves into the abandoned town. First real shelter with walls since the waterfall cave.
- People spread out. Finding beds in old houses. A roof over their heads.
- It feels wrong — sleeping in dead people's homes. But it's better than the forest floor.

### Section 3: Settling In
- **Camp chore gameplay** — help set up in the town.
  - Board up broken windows
  - Clear debris from the main street
  - Set up a cooking area in the old saloon
  - Post guards at the town entrances
- Clark is directing everything again. Smooth. Confident. The gang follows.

### Section 4: The Letters
- Night. Everyone's settled. Jeremiah and Marcus meet in the old church. Alone.
- Marcus shows Jeremiah the letters.

**Marcus:** "Every town. Every camp. He's been writing to the marshal since at least Chapter 2. Giving up locations, numbers, Joseph's condition. Everything."

**Jeremiah:** *(reading)* "...He mentions the waterfall. He mentions the Hill. He even mentions the snow camp."

**Marcus:** "He's been doing this from the start, Jerry. From the very beginning."

**Jeremiah:** "The blizzard. Chapter 1. He 'found' us in the blizzard."

**Marcus:** "What if he didn't find us? What if he was already following us?"

*That sits in the air.*

**Jeremiah:** "We tell Charles. Tomorrow. Just Charles."

**Marcus:** "And then?"

**Jeremiah:** "And then we figure out how to do this without tearing the gang apart."

**Marcus:** "It's already tearing apart, Jerry."

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Marcus finds physical proof of Clark's betrayal — letters to the marshal
- Clark has been informing since the beginning — possibly since before he "found" them in the blizzard
- The abandoned town is clean — Clark didn't set a trap this time (building false trust)
- The gang now has real shelter but it's another temporary home
- Jeremiah and Marcus plan to tell Charles — the last person they fully trust
- The question: was Clark planted in the gang from the start?
- Second playable Marcus section — building toward the protagonist switch
