# Chapter 6, Mission 1: The New Normal

## Chapter: 6
## Location: Forest Camp (new, unnamed)

---

## Setup
- The gang is camped in the forest after fleeing the waterfall cave.
- No proper camp — just tents thrown up between trees.
- Trevor is gone. No one is cooking. No one is patching people up.
- Morale is at rock bottom.
- Clark is being helpful. Too helpful.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Morning
- Jeremiah wakes up. The camp is quiet. Wrong kind of quiet.
- He walks through camp. People sitting around doing nothing. No fire. No food cooking.
- Delia Harmon is trying to fill Trevor's role as medic but she's overwhelmed.
- Someone left a pot of water on an unlit fire. Nobody thought to light it.

**Jeremiah lights the fire. Starts boiling water. Starts making something — badly.**

**Phil:** "What is that."

**Jeremiah:** "Breakfast."

**Phil:** "It looks like mud."

**Jeremiah:** "It's oatmeal."

**Phil:** "Trevor's oatmeal didn't look like that."

**Jeremiah:** "Trevor's not here."

*Silence. Phil takes a bowl. Eats it without another word.*

### Section 2: Clark Steps Up
- Clark walks through camp organizing people. Assigning chores. Checking on the wounded.
- He's doing what Joseph should be doing. What Jeremiah should be doing. But he got there first.

**Clark:** "Ray, help Hector set up the perimeter. Owen, get some firewood — we need more than one fire tonight. Nell, can you help Delia with the medical supplies?"

- People listen. They're used to taking orders and Clark is giving them.
- Jeremiah watches from across camp. Marcus walks up beside him.

**Marcus:** *(low)* "Look at him. Playing leader."

**Jeremiah:** "I see it."

**Marcus:** "Everyone's listening to him, Jerry."

**Jeremiah:** "Because nobody else is talking."

**Marcus:** "Then maybe we should be."

### Section 3: Joseph's Tent
- Jeremiah goes to check on Joseph. He's in his tent with Luisa.
- Joseph is sitting on his cot. Staring at the ground. Luisa is next to him holding his hand.
- He looks up when Jeremiah enters. His eyes are glassy.

**Jeremiah:** "Joseph. We need to figure out our next move."

**Joseph:** "...Where's Charles?"

**Jeremiah:** "Charles is at the supply tent. Joseph, we need to—"

**Joseph:** "Tell Charles to come see me."

**Jeremiah:** "I will. But right now—"

**Joseph:** "And where's Trevor? He was supposed to bring me coffee."

*Jeremiah freezes. Luisa closes her eyes.*

**Jeremiah:** "...I'll bring you some coffee, Joseph."

*He walks out. Luisa follows him.*

**Luisa:** "He forgot again. About Trevor."

**Jeremiah:** "How often?"

**Luisa:** "Every morning. Every morning he asks where Trevor is. And every morning I have to tell him."

**Jeremiah:** "Jesus."

**Luisa:** "He cries every time. Like it's the first time hearing it. Then ten minutes later he asks again."

### Section 4: The Mission
- Jeremiah decides the camp needs structure. He takes Marcus, Phil, and Hector out to scout the surrounding area and secure resources.
- **Gameplay: Explore the forest area around camp.**
  - Mark good hunting spots
  - Find a water source
  - Identify defensive positions
  - Check for any signs they were followed
- No combat. Just practical survival work.
- During the ride, Jeremiah and Marcus discuss what to do about Clark — quietly, away from the others.

**Jeremiah:** "We can't expose him yet. Half the camp thinks he's the only one holding things together."

**Marcus:** "Because he IS the only one right now. That's the problem."

**Jeremiah:** "If we accuse him without proof the others can see, it splits the gang."

**Marcus:** "The gang's already split, Jerry. They just don't know it yet."

### Section 5: Return
- They come back. Clark has gotten a fire going, food distributed, and organized the camp layout.
- People are thanking him.

**Nate:** "Clark got us set up pretty good."

**Ray:** "Better than sitting around waiting for someone to tell us what to do."

*Jeremiah hears this. Knows exactly what's happening. Clark is replacing Joseph in the gang's eyes — and positioning himself above Jeremiah too.*

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Trevor's absence is everywhere — no food, no medic, no structure
- Joseph has forgotten Trevor is dead. Asks for him every morning. Cries every time he's told.
- Clark stepping into the leadership vacuum — organizing camp, giving orders
- The gang is starting to follow Clark because nobody else is leading
- Jeremiah and Marcus discuss exposing Clark but can't without proof
- Luisa reveals Joseph's memory is cycling — forgets and re-grieves Trevor daily
