# Chapter 6, Mission 5: The Storm Is Here

## Chapter: 6
## Location: Abandoned Town

---

## Setup
- Clark has been dealt with (exiled, locked up, or executed based on player choice).
- But the damage is done — the marshal knows where they are.
- They have days at most. Maybe hours.
- Jeremiah takes charge. Not Joseph. Not Clark. Jeremiah.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Jeremiah Leads
- First time Jeremiah explicitly takes command of the gang.
- He stands in front of everyone. Not in Joseph's spot. His own spot.

**Jeremiah:** "Clark already told them where we are. That means the law is coming. Could be tomorrow. Could be tonight. We have two options — run again, or make a stand."

**Charles:** "We can't keep running. The women and children can't take another evacuation."

**Marcus:** "And running into what? Clark scouted every route we've ever taken. They know our patterns."

**Jeremiah:** "Then we dig in. This town has walls. Buildings. Chokepoints. If they're coming, they're coming to us. And we'll be ready."

### Section 2: Fortifying the Town (Gameplay)
- **Defense preparation mission.** Player sets up the town for battle.
  - Barricade the main road into town
  - Set up Phil's landmines at the entrances
  - Position shooters in second-floor windows
  - Stock ammunition in key buildings
  - Set up a fallback point at the church
  - Evacuate women and children to the basement of the church
  - Place dynamite charges on the bridge leading into town (if there is one)
- Each task involves interacting with different gang members. Everyone has a role.
- Phil rigs explosives. Hector sets traps. Earl takes a sniper position on the church steeple. Marcus covers the east entrance. Charles sits on the saloon porch with his worn 1911 — he's not moving.

### Section 3: The Wait
- Night falls. Everything is set. The town is dark. Quiet.
- The gang waits. In windows. Behind barricades. On rooftops.
- **Quiet character moments during the wait:**

**Marcus finds Emily in the church basement with Josh.**

**Marcus:** "Whatever happens — you take Josh through the back of the church. There's a cellar door that leads outside. You run and you don't look back."

**Emily:** "Marcus—"

**Marcus:** "Promise me."

**Emily:** "I'm not leaving you."

**Marcus:** "You're not leaving me. You're saving our son."

*Emily grabs his face. Kisses him.*

**Emily:** "You come back."

**Marcus:** "I always do."

---

**Jeremiah finds Charles on the saloon porch.**

**Jeremiah:** "You should be inside."

**Charles:** "I've been fighting since before your daddy was born. I'll fight from wherever I damn well please."

**Jeremiah:** "Charles—"

**Charles:** "Sit with me a minute."

*Jeremiah sits.*

**Charles:** "Whatever happens tonight — you did good, Jerry. You held these people together when nobody else could."

**Jeremiah:** "I learned from two old men who wouldn't shut up."

**Charles:** *(laughing, then coughing)* "...Yeah. I suppose you did."

### Section 4: They're Here
- Dust on the horizon. Torches. The marshal's militia. Dozens of them. Maybe a hundred.
- They stop outside the town. A voice calls out through a megaphone.

**Marshal:** "This is your last chance. Send out Joseph Walkns and Jeremiah Field. Everyone else walks free."

*Silence from the town.*

**Marshal:** "You have five minutes."

*Jeremiah looks at Charles. At Marcus in the window across the street. At Phil crouched behind a barricade with dynamite in each hand. At Earl on the steeple with his rifle scope catching moonlight.*

**Jeremiah:** *(to himself)* "Five minutes."

*He stands up. Walks to the barricade at the main road. Stands on top of it. Silhouetted against the dark town behind him. Every eye on him — the gang's and the marshal's.*

**Jeremiah:** "You want us? Come get us."

*The first shot rings out. The town erupts.*

### Section 5: The Battle of the Town (Major Combat)
- **Largest combat encounter in the game.**
- Waves of militia pushing into the town from multiple directions.
- Phil's landmines blow the first wave apart.
- Earl picks off officers from the steeple.
- Marcus holds the east entrance.
- Hector's traps catch flankers.
- Charles is firing from the saloon porch — every shot counts.
- Jeremiah moves through the town — the player pushes from position to position, rallying the gang, holding the line.
- **Dynamic battle** — the tide shifts back and forth. Buildings catch fire. Barricades break. The gang falls back to secondary positions.

### Section 6: The Marshal
- The battle reaches its climax. The militia is breaking through.
- Jeremiah fights his way to the main road. The marshal is there. The old man from the saloon in Chapter 2. The one who killed Lenny.
- They see each other across the burning street.

*This isn't resolved here. The marshal retreats when his forces take too many losses. But he'll be back.*

### Section 7: Aftermath
- Dawn. The town is scarred. Buildings burning. Bodies in the street. But the gang held.
- Casualties among the gang — some of the unnamed members are wounded. Maybe one or two dead.
- But they held. For the first time, they didn't run. They stood their ground.

**Charles:** *(on the saloon porch, covered in dust, reloading his 1911)* "Not bad for a bunch of outlaws."

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 7"** appears.

### Mission Complete — End of Chapter 6

## Key Story Points
- Jeremiah takes command of the gang for the first time
- Clark publicly exposed and dealt with (exile/jail/execution — player choice)
- The gang digs in and fights instead of running — first time
- Largest combat encounter in the game
- The marshal attacks but retreats after heavy losses
- Marcus and Emily have a goodbye-just-in-case moment
- Charles and Jeremiah share what might be their last conversation
- Jeremiah's "Come get us" moment — defining the character
- The town is damaged but held
- The marshal will return
- Clark is still out there (if exiled) or locked up (if jailed)
