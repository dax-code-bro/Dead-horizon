# Chapter 6, Mission 4: The Trial of Clark Jones

## Chapter: 6
## Location: Abandoned Town — Main Street

---

## Setup
- Charles called a meeting. The entire gang. Main street. Everyone.
- Clark doesn't know what's coming. He walks in confident. White coat. White shotgun on his back. Smiling.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Gathering
- Everyone assembles in the main street. All 30+ people. Women, children, everyone.
- Charles stands at the front. Jeremiah and Marcus flanking him.
- Joseph is there — Luisa guiding him. He's having a foggy day.
- Clark stands in the crowd. Arms crossed. Relaxed.

**Charles:** "I called you all here because there's something you need to see. Something that explains why we've been running. Why Lenny is dead. Why Trevor is dead. Why a five-year-old boy has a scar on his shoulder."

*The camp goes dead quiet. Josh unconsciously touches his shoulder. Emily pulls him close.*

### Section 2: The Letters
- Charles holds up the stack of letters.

**Charles:** "These are letters. Written by a member of this gang. Addressed to the US Marshal's office. They contain the location of every camp we've ever had. The number of men, women, and children. The condition of our leader. And instructions on how to set up ambushes on routes that this person volunteered to scout."

*Murmurs through the crowd. People looking at each other.*

**Charles:** "The snow camp. Hope. The Gorge. The Hill. The waterfall cave. Every single one — given away."

**Ray:** "Who wrote them?"

*Charles looks directly at Clark.*

**Charles:** "Clark Jones."

*Dead silence. Every head turns to Clark.*

### Section 3: Clark's Response
- Clark doesn't run. Doesn't panic. He laughs.

**Clark:** "You're joking."

**Charles:** "I'm holding the proof."

**Clark:** "Those could be forged. Anyone could've written those."

**Marcus:** "I watched you meet with a badge man in an alley in Las York. I heard you give him our location. I saw him hand you money."

**Clark:** "Marcus has had it out for me since—"

**Jeremiah:** "I pulled a fresh letter from under a rock at the edge of this town yesterday. In your handwriting. With this town's location. You've already told them where we are."

*Clark's smile fades. Just slightly. His hand drifts toward his M11.*

**Hector:** "Don't."

*Hector has his knife out. Phil has both sawed-offs leveled. Earl has his rifle up. The inner circle was ready.*

### Section 4: The Gang Reacts

**Ray:** "Clark... is this true?"

*Clark looks around. Calculating. Looking for allies. Looking for an exit.*

**Owen:** "You told me Joseph was the problem. You told me Jerry was losing it. You said we should—"

**Clark:** "I said what needed to be said to keep this gang alive!"

**Emily:** *(stepping forward, cold fury)* "My son has a bullet scar because of you. He's FIVE."

**Sadie:** "Trevor is dead. Is that because of you too?"

*Clark doesn't answer that one. His jaw tightens.*

**Phil:** "Answer her."

**Clark:** "Trevor made his own choice."

**Phil:** *(raising both sawed-offs higher)* "Wrong answer."

### Section 5: Joseph
- Joseph has been standing there. Luisa holding his arm. The fog is thick today. But Clark's name keeps cutting through.
- Something registers. Joseph pushes past Luisa. Walks toward Clark. Slow.

**Joseph:** "You... I let you in. In the snow. I let you in."

**Clark:** "Joseph—"

**Joseph:** "You're one of mine. My children. I called you... I called you my..."

*His hands are shaking. The white 1911 is on his hip.*

**Luisa:** "Joseph, don't—"

**Joseph:** "You killed my boy. Trevor. You killed him."

**Clark:** "I didn't pull the trigger—"

**Joseph:** "YOU SENT THEM TO US!"

*The scream echoes through the empty town. Joseph's eyes are wild — not lucid, not gone, somewhere in between where everything hurts and nothing makes sense.*

*Charles steps in front of Joseph. Gently.*

**Charles:** "Not like this, Joseph. Not like this."

### Section 6: PLAYER CHOICE

**Charles turns to Jeremiah.**

**Charles:** "What do we do with him?"

**[Execute Clark]**
- Jeremiah draws the silver Cattleman. Walks to Clark. Clark's smile is completely gone now.
- Clark: "Jerry. Come on. We're friends. I called you Jerry."
- Jeremiah: "Yeah. You did."
- BANG.
- *(But this choice may not be available — Clark needs to survive for the endgame with Joseph)*

**[Exile Clark]**
- Jeremiah: "Take his guns. Take his horse. Take everything. And let him walk."
- Clark is stripped of his weapons. The white sawed-off. The black M11. Everything.
- He's marched to the edge of town.
- Clark: "You're making a mistake. The marshal knows where you are. He's coming. And when he gets here—"
- Jeremiah: "Then we'll be ready. Now walk."
- Clark walks into the wilderness. No weapons. No horse. Just the white coat on his back.

**[Lock him up]**
- They tie Clark up in the old jail cell in the town. One of the only buildings with bars.
- Keep him alive. Use him as leverage against the marshal.
- Clark sits in the cell. Still smiling. "You think bars hold me? The law is coming regardless."

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Clark publicly exposed in front of the entire gang
- Physical proof presented — letters in Clark's handwriting
- Marcus's testimony corroborates
- Clark responsible for: every camp being found, the ambush that grazed Josh, Trevor's death, possibly Lenny's death
- Emily confronts Clark about Josh's scar
- Joseph has a moment of clarity — screams at Clark about Trevor
- Charles controls the situation — prevents Joseph from executing Clark on the spot
- Player choice determines Clark's immediate fate (but he survives regardless for endgame)
- The gang is united against Clark — but the marshal already knows where they are
- The clock is ticking
