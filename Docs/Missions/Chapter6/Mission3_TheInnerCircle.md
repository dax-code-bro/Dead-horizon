# Chapter 6, Mission 3: The Inner Circle

## Chapter: 6
## Location: Abandoned Town — Church

---

## Setup
- Jeremiah and Marcus have Clark's letters — physical proof of betrayal.
- They need to tell Charles. He's the only one with the authority and respect to handle this.
- But Joseph is getting worse and Clark is getting bolder.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Finding Charles
- Morning. Jeremiah finds Charles sitting on the church steps. Smoking. Coughing more than usual. His COPD is getting worse but nobody knows.
- Jeremiah sits next to him.

**Jeremiah:** "We need to talk. You, me, and Marcus. Somewhere private."

**Charles:** "About what."

**Jeremiah:** "Clark."

*Charles takes a long drag. Doesn't look surprised.*

**Charles:** "I've been waiting for someone to say that name."

### Section 2: The Church Meeting
- Jeremiah, Marcus, and Charles inside the old church. Doors closed.
- Marcus lays out the letters on the altar.
- Charles reads them. One by one. His face hardens with each page.

**Charles:** "...How long have you known?"

**Marcus:** "I followed him to a meeting with a badge man in Las York. Weeks ago."

**Charles:** "And you're just telling me now?"

**Jeremiah:** "We couldn't prove it until those letters. And we couldn't tell Joseph."

**Charles:** "Joseph can't even remember Trevor's dead. No. You were right not to tell him."

*Charles puts the letters down. Looks at the cross on the church wall.*

**Charles:** "That boy walked into our camp in a blizzard. I welcomed him. Fed him. Gave him a place."

**Jeremiah:** "I know."

**Charles:** "Trevor is dead because of him. Lenny might be too — the law knew about the bank."

**Marcus:** "So what do we do?"

**Charles:** "We don't do anything rash. Clark's got half the camp thinking he's their savior right now. If we execute him without explaining, we lose people."

**Jeremiah:** "So we explain."

**Charles:** "We need the right moment. When Clark's mask slips in front of everyone. Not our word against his — his own actions condemning him."

**Marcus:** "And if the law shows up before then?"

**Charles:** "Then we deal with Clark first and the law second."

### Section 3: Watching Clark
- **Gameplay: Observation mission.**
- Player follows Clark through his day without being detected.
- Watch who he talks to, where he goes, what he does.
- Clark talks to Joseph — poisoning him more.
- Clark talks to Ray and Owen — building allies.
- Clark goes to the edge of town and leaves a folded note under a rock near the road.
- **Jeremiah collects the note after Clark leaves.** It's another letter to the marshal with the town's location.

### Section 4: The Decision
- Back in the church. Jeremiah shows Charles and Marcus the fresh note.

**Charles:** "He's already told them where we are."

**Jeremiah:** "We have days. Maybe less."

**Charles:** "Then we move fast. Tomorrow, I want everyone gathered. Every last person. And I want Clark standing in the middle."

**Marcus:** "What are you gonna do?"

**Charles:** "I'm going to let the letters speak for themselves. And then we'll see who Clark really is."

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Charles now knows about Clark — the inner circle is Jeremiah, Marcus, and Charles
- Charles reads the letters, connects Clark to Trevor's death and possibly Lenny's
- Clark is still informing — left a new note with their current location
- Charles plans a public confrontation — let the letters condemn Clark, not accusations
- Charles's COPD is worsening — coughing more, but nobody knows
- The clock is ticking — the law could arrive any day
- Clark has been building allies (Ray, Owen) and poisoning Joseph
