# Chapter 8: "The Mask Slips"

## Setting: Abandoned Town
## Missions: 7
## Tone: Suspicion. Paranoia. Clark's mask begins to crack. The 4th antagonist escalates.

---

## Mission 1: "Whispers"

- The gang is on edge after the deadline passed. People are suspicious of everything.
- Clark overplays his hand — tells Owen and Ray that Jeremiah is "losing control" and "going to get everyone killed."
- Owen, drunk, repeats it to Marcus. Marcus tells Jeremiah.
- Jeremiah doesn't react outwardly. But he starts keeping a mental list of who Clark talks to.
- **Gameplay: Social observation.** Walk through camp. Listen to conversations. Note who's been talking to Clark.

**Key moment:** Jeremiah overhears Clark telling Joseph that "Jerry's been making deals behind your back." Joseph's foggy eyes narrow. The seed is planted deeper.

---

## Mission 2: "The Accountant's Trail"

- Jeremiah follows up on Arthur Bellamy. Rides to a nearby town to gather information.
- **Solo investigation mission** — like Ashes Don't Lie.
- Learns Bellamy has been hiring men. Rebuilding the Waterson private army. And he's been talking to Marshal Harding.
- **The Watersons and the marshal are now connected.** Bellamy wants revenge. Harding wants arrests. They're working together.
- Jeremiah finds a wanted poster with his face on it. $10,000 reward. Joseph's is $15,000. Marcus is $5,000.
- He takes the poster. Rides back.

---

## Mission 3: "Cracked Glass"

**Clark makes a mistake.** The first real one.

- A small scouting party goes out — Clark, Hector, and Nate.
- They come back and Clark reports the area is clear.
- But Hector pulls Jeremiah aside later.

**Hector:** "Clark left us for twenty minutes during the scout. Said he was checking a ridge. I followed him. He wasn't on any ridge. He was at the old post office in the next town. Writing something."

- Jeremiah sends Marcus to check the post office the next day. Marcus finds a letter Clark left — addressed to the marshal. It contains the town's defenses, number of fighters, and a proposed plan: "I'll open the east gate at midnight on the 15th."

**Clark is planning to let the marshal's army walk right in.**

- Jeremiah, Marcus, and Charles now have the letters from Chapter 6 Mission 2 AND this new one. The proof is overwhelming. But they still need to handle this carefully.

**This is the moment it becomes "very clear" to the player who the 5th antagonist is.**

---

## Mission 4: "Father and Son"

**Jeremiah confronts Joseph.** Not about Clark — about his family.

- Joseph is having a semi-lucid day. Jeremiah seizes the moment.
- He asks Joseph directly: "Did you know about my father's debt to the Watersons?"

**Joseph:** "Your father... he borrowed money. A long time ago."

**Jeremiah:** "Did you know the Watersons killed my family because of it?"

**Joseph:** *(long pause)* "...They told me they'd settle the debt. I didn't know how."

**Jeremiah:** "You knew. You knew they were going to do something and you didn't warn me."

**Joseph:** "I thought... I thought they meant money. I didn't think they'd..."

**Jeremiah:** "My wife. My son. My daughter. They burned them alive, Joseph. For twelve thousand dollars. And you KNEW there was a debt."

**Joseph:** *(breaking)* "I'm sorry. Jerry, I'm sorry. I didn't know they'd—"

**Jeremiah:** "But you didn't tell me. You let me think it was an accident for years."

- Jeremiah walks out. Joseph is left alone. Crying. Luisa rushes in.
- This is the deepest crack in the father-son bond. Joseph may not have known they'd kill his family — but he knew about the debt and said nothing.

**Jeremiah ↔ Joseph drops to 4/10.**

---

## Mission 5: "The 15th"

**Clark's plan is for the 15th.** They know the date. They use it.

- Jeremiah, Marcus, and Charles set a counter-trap.
- They DON'T expose Clark. Instead, they let him think his plan is working.
- On the night of the 15th, Clark sneaks to the east gate to open it.
- **But the east gate is a kill zone.** The gang has repositioned. The gate opens onto a corridor lined with Phil's explosives and Earl's rifle.
- Clark opens the gate. The marshal's advance team rushes in.
- **BOOM.** Landmines. Gunfire from every angle. The advance team is wiped out.
- The marshal pulls back again — heavier losses this time.
- Clark realizes the trap was reversed. He's standing at the open gate. Turns around. Jeremiah, Marcus, and Charles are standing there.

**Jeremiah:** "Close the gate, Clark."

**Clark:** "...Jerry, I can explain—"

**Charles:** "Close. The. Gate."

- Clark closes it. He knows he's caught. But he also knows Joseph is still under his influence.
- Clark is disarmed and confined. But NOT killed — not yet.

---

## Mission 6: "The Reckoning"

**Public confrontation — but it goes wrong.**

- Charles addresses the gang. Shows the letters. The evidence. Clark's plan to open the gate.
- Most of the gang is horrified. Emily confronts Clark about Josh's scar. Phil and Hector are ready to execute him.
- **But Joseph intervenes.**

**Joseph:** *(foggy, confused, Clark's conditioning kicking in)* "No. Clark is... Clark takes care of me. Clark is loyal."

**Jeremiah:** "Joseph, he's been selling us to the marshal since Chapter 1."

**Joseph:** "You're lying. You've been lying. Clark told me — you and Marcus, you're planning to take over. You want to throw me away."

**Jeremiah:** "That's not true—"

**Joseph:** "GET AWAY FROM ME!"

- Joseph pulls Clark to his side. Clark stands there — smirking.
- **The gang splits.** Not evenly. Most side with Jeremiah and the evidence. But Joseph, Luisa (who follows Joseph), and a handful of members who are afraid of change stay with Clark and Joseph.

**Split:**
- **Jeremiah's side:** Marcus, Emily, Josh, Charles, Phil, Sadie, Hector, Rosa, Earl, Faye, Nate, Danny, Nell, Alma, Delia, Silas
- **Joseph's side:** Clark, Luisa, Ray, Owen, Tommy, Jeff, Wade, a few others

---

## Mission 7: "Two Camps"

**The gang physically separates within the town.**

- Jeremiah's people take the west side. Joseph's take the east.
- The town that was supposed to protect them is now divided down the middle.
- **A painful mission.** Walking through a camp that's half empty. Seeing people you've known for years on the other side of the street. Not talking.
- Marcus checks on Jeremiah.

**Marcus:** "How are you doing?"

**Jeremiah:** "I just lost my father. Again."

- Clark is back in Joseph's ear immediately. Poisoning. Whispering. Building.
- Charles coughs more. Hides it. But Jeremiah notices this time.

**Jeremiah:** "You alright?"

**Charles:** "Old lungs. Don't worry about it."

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 9"** appears.

## Chapter 8 Summary
- **Clark's betrayal becomes undeniable** — Hector catches him, Marcus finds the gate plan
- **Clark's plan to open the gate on the 15th is reversed** — counter-trap
- The marshal's name confirmed: **Samuel Harding**, 4th antagonist
- **Arthur Bellamy** rebuilding Waterson empire, allied with the marshal
- **Jeremiah confronts Joseph about the debt** — Joseph knew about it, didn't warn him
- Joseph's trust in Jeremiah shatters (Clark's conditioning + brain damage)
- **The gang splits** — Jeremiah's side vs Joseph/Clark's side
- Wanted posters: Jeremiah $10K, Joseph $15K, Marcus $5K
- Charles coughing more — Jeremiah notices but Charles deflects
