# Chapter 11: "The Unraveling"

## Setting: Abandoned Town → Various
## Missions: 6
## Tone: Fracture. Goodbyes. The end of an era.

---

## Mission 1: "Deserters"

- More people leave. Owen and June pack up quietly one morning. Ray tries to stop them.

**Ray:** "Owen, don't—"

**Owen:** "I'm done, Ray. June's done. We're old. We're tired. And I don't want to die in a town that isn't mine fighting a war I didn't start."

**Ray:** "Where will you go?"

**Owen:** "Anywhere. Everywhere. Nowhere. Doesn't matter."

- They ride out. June looks back once. Owen doesn't.
- Mae sits Ray down. Tells him they should go too. Ray refuses. "I'm not leaving."
- **The gang is bleeding members.** What was 30+ is now closer to 20.

---

## Mission 2: "The Widow's Offer"

**Alma Reyes approaches Jeremiah.**

- She hands him a box. Inside: Charles's personal effects. A pocket watch. A photograph of a young Charles with people Jeremiah doesn't recognize. And a letter — different from the one under the pillow. This one is addressed to Jeremiah.

**Charles's letter to Jeremiah:**
*"Jerry — If you're reading this, I'm either dead or too stubborn to hand it to you in person. Probably dead. There's something I never told you. Your father didn't just owe the Watersons money. He stole from them. He stole $12,000 to fund the gang's first real camp. Joseph knew. I knew. We used that money to build everything we have. Your family died for a debt that built the ground you're standing on. I'm sorry. I should have told you years ago. Don't hate Joseph. He's a fool but he loved your father. And he loves you. Even if he can't show it anymore. — Charles"*

- Jeremiah reads it. Sits down. The gang was literally built on his family's blood.
- Everything Joseph has. Everything the gang is. Paid for with stolen money that got Sarah, Thomas, and Lily killed.

---

## Mission 3: "Burning Bridges"

**Jeremiah confronts Joseph one final time.** This is their last real conversation.

- Joseph is having a rare semi-lucid moment. Jeremiah pushes through Clark (who tries to block him) and sits down.

**Jeremiah:** "Did you know my father stole from the Watersons?"

**Joseph:** "...Your father was a complicated man."

**Jeremiah:** "Did you use that money to build this gang."

**Joseph:** *(long silence)* "...Yes."

**Jeremiah:** "My wife and children burned to death for twelve thousand dollars that you used to buy tents and horses."

**Joseph:** "Jerry—"

**Jeremiah:** "Every meal we've eaten. Every camp we've made. Every bullet we've fired. Paid for with my family's lives."

**Joseph:** "I didn't know they'd come for your family. I swear to God, I didn't—"

**Jeremiah:** "But you knew the money was stolen. And you never gave it back. And when they came collecting, it wasn't your door they knocked on. It was mine."

- Jeremiah stands up. Looks at the man who raised him. The man who taught him to ride, to shoot, to survive. The man whose brain is rotting. Whose eyes can barely focus.

**Jeremiah:** "I loved you like a father. You were the only father I ever knew. And you let my family die for this."

- He walks out. Joseph calls after him but can't form the words. Clark appears at his side immediately.

**Clark:** "See? I told you. He's turning on you."

---

## Mission 4: "One Last Ride"

**Jeremiah takes Marcus on one final ride together.** Just the two of them. Like old times.

- They ride out to the waterfall cave. It's been months since they were here. Nature has started reclaiming it.
- They sit behind the waterfall. Where the gang used to sleep.

**Marcus:** "Remember when Josh found that fish?"

**Jeremiah:** "The one he caught at the river?"

**Marcus:** "No, the one at the stream near here. He tried to grab it with his hands and fell in."

**Jeremiah:** *(laughing)* "Emily lost her mind."

**Marcus:** "She didn't talk to me for an hour because I was laughing instead of helping."

- They talk about the good times. The bar night in Chapter 2. The fishing. The sword fights. The time Phil yelled "GOOD MORNING McALLENS."
- Then it gets real.

**Marcus:** "Jerry. Come with us. Please."

**Jeremiah:** "I have something I need to finish."

**Marcus:** "Joseph?"

**Jeremiah:** "Joseph. Clark. All of it. I need to see it through."

**Marcus:** "You're going to get yourself killed."

**Jeremiah:** "Maybe."

**Marcus:** "Jerry—"

**Jeremiah:** "If something happens to me — you take care of Josh. You be the father I know you can be. The father you already are."

**Marcus:** "Don't talk like that."

**Jeremiah:** "And tell him... tell him Uncle Jerry kept his promise."

- They ride back in silence.

---

## Mission 5: "Goodbye"

**Marcus, Emily, and Josh leave.**

- Early morning. Marcus has a wagon loaded. Emily is inside with Josh. Their things packed.
- The camp watches. Nobody stops them. Everyone understands.
- Jeremiah stands at the edge of town.

**Josh:** *(running to Jeremiah)* "Uncle Jerry! Come with us!"

**Jeremiah:** *(kneeling)* "I can't, buddy. Not yet."

**Josh:** "But you promised you'd always take care of me."

**Jeremiah:** "And I will. But right now I need your daddy to do that. Can you let him?"

**Josh:** *(bottom lip trembling)* "...Okay."

**Jeremiah:** "You be good for your mama and daddy. Okay?"

**Josh:** "Okay."

- Jeremiah hugs him. Holds him tight. Then lets go.
- Emily walks up. She doesn't say much. She puts her hand on Jeremiah's face.

**Emily:** "Come find us."

**Jeremiah:** "I will."

**Emily:** "Liar."

- She knows. She can see it in his eyes. He's not planning to come find them. He's planning to die here. But she doesn't argue. She just holds his gaze.

**Emily:** "Thank you. For everything. For Josh. For Marcus. For me."

**Jeremiah:** "Go take care of your family, Emily."

- Marcus is last. They stand face to face. Two men who went from reluctant brothers to real ones.

**Marcus:** "I'm not saying goodbye."

**Jeremiah:** "Good. I hate goodbyes."

**Marcus:** "I'll see you soon."

**Jeremiah:** "Yeah. Soon."

- They clasp hands. Pull each other in. A hug. Brief. Tight.
- Marcus climbs onto the wagon. Emily is inside. Josh is looking out the back.
- The wagon rolls out. Josh waves. Jeremiah waves back.
- He watches the wagon until it's a dot on the horizon. Then it's gone.

**Jeremiah stands there. Alone. The melted toy soldier in his pocket. The silver Cattleman on his hip. The dead horizon in front of him.**

---

## Mission 6: "What's Left"

**Jeremiah returns to what remains of the gang.**

- It's small now. Jeremiah's side: Phil, Sadie, Hector, Rosa, Earl, Faye, Nate, Alma, Delia. Maybe a few others.
- Joseph's side: Clark, Luisa, Ray, Tommy, Jeff.
- The town feels like a ghost of itself.
- Jeremiah sits on the roof of the tallest building. The same spot from Chapter 7. Looking at the dead horizon.
- Phil comes up.

**Phil:** "You let them go."

**Jeremiah:** "They deserve better than this."

**Phil:** "We all deserve better than this."

**Jeremiah:** "Yeah. We do."

**Phil:** "So what now?"

**Jeremiah:** "Now we end it. One way or another."

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 12"** appears.

## Chapter 11 Summary
- Owen and June leave the gang
- Charles's letter reveals: Jeremiah's father STOLE from the Watersons, the gang was built on that money
- Jeremiah confronts Joseph — "my family burned for this gang"
- Clark immediately twists the confrontation to poison Joseph further
- Jeremiah and Marcus's last ride together — reminiscing, saying things they need to say
- **Marcus, Emily, and Josh leave the gang** — the goodbye scene
- Josh: "You promised you'd take care of me" — Jeremiah: "Let your daddy do that"
- Emily knows Jeremiah isn't coming. "Liar."
- The gang is a skeleton of what it was
- Jeremiah decides to end it — one way or another
