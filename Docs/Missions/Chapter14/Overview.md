# Chapter 14: "The Immortal Game"

## Setting: Abandoned Town
## Missions: 5
## Tone: War. Brother against brother. The chess board plays out.

---

## Mission 1: "Sunrise"

**Morning. The divide becomes a battle line.**

- Jeremiah wakes up to shouting. Joseph's side has barricaded the east end of the main street.
- Tommy and Jeff have rifles pointed at Jeremiah's side. Ray is standing behind them looking uncertain.
- Clark is standing next to Joseph. Joseph has the gray M4 in his hands.

**Clark:** *(loud enough for everyone)* "Jeremiah Field has been working with the marshal! He made a deal to hand Joseph over! We're not letting that happen!"

**Jeremiah steps into the street.**

**Jeremiah:** "Joseph. Look at me. This isn't real. Clark is lying to you."

**Joseph:** *(confused, gripping the M4)* "You... you met with Harding. Clark saw it."

**Jeremiah:** "I met with Harding to PROTECT us. To end the threat. I would never hand you over."

**Clark:** "Then why did Marcus have a rifle pointed at the meeting? Insurance? In case the deal went bad?"

- People on both sides are shouting. Hector has his knife. Phil has his shotguns. Earl is on a roof.
- On the other side, Tommy is scared. Ray is sweating. Jeff has his gun up but his hands are shaking.

**Jeremiah:** "EVERYONE STOP. Nobody fires. NOBODY."

- A standoff. The entire town. A street between them.

---

## Mission 2: "The Bridge"

**Luisa breaks the standoff.**

- She walks out from Joseph's side. Into the middle of the street. No gun. Hands up.

**Luisa:** "ENOUGH. All of you. ENOUGH."

- She turns to Joseph. "Joseph. Put the gun down. Please."

**Joseph:** "He's trying to—"

**Luisa:** "He raised you, Joseph. Jerry would die before he betrayed you. You KNOW that. Somewhere in there, you know."

- For a moment, Joseph's grip loosens. His eyes search for something — the memory of who Jeremiah is.
- **Then Clark whispers something in his ear.** Joseph's grip tightens again.

**Joseph:** "Get back, Luisa."

**Luisa:** "No."

**Joseph:** "I SAID GET BACK!"

- Luisa doesn't move. She stands in the middle of the street. The Queen on the chess board — standing between two armies.

**Clark sees an opportunity.** If shooting starts with Luisa in the middle, Joseph will blame Jeremiah's side forever.

- **But Jeremiah sees it too.**

**Jeremiah:** *(to his people)* "Lower your weapons. All of you. Now."

**Phil:** "Jerry—"

**Jeremiah:** "NOW."

- Jeremiah's side lowers their guns. Hector sheaths his knife. Earl comes down from the roof.
- Jeremiah walks into the street. Toward Luisa. Toward Joseph. Hands at his sides. The silver Cattleman still holstered.
- Every gun on Joseph's side is pointed at him.

**Jeremiah:** "Joseph. I'm not armed. Look at me."

- He stops in front of Joseph. Father and son. The M4 pointed at Jeremiah's chest.

**Jeremiah:** "I'm your son. You raised me. Whatever Clark told you — look at ME. Do you want to kill your own son?"

- Joseph is shaking. The M4 trembling. His eyes are wet. The fog is fighting the truth.

**Joseph:** *(cracking)* "...Jerry..."

**Clark:** "He's manipulating you, Joseph. Just like he always does."

**Jeremiah:** "Shut up, Clark."

- A beat. Joseph looks at Clark. Then at Jeremiah. The M4 lowers. Just an inch.

**Joseph:** "...I don't know what's real anymore."

**Jeremiah:** "I know. And that's not your fault."

- Clark sees it slipping. His control. His plan. He needs to escalate.

---

## Mission 3: "Fracture"

**Clark forces the issue.**

- Clark grabs Tommy's rifle and fires a shot into the air. "THEY'RE ATTACKING!"
- Panic. Joseph's side opens fire. Jeremiah's side scrambles for cover.
- **NOT a full battle** — chaotic, brief, terrifying. Shots fired from both sides before people realize Clark started it.
- In the chaos: **Ray gets shot.** Not fatally — shoulder. But Mae screams from across the street.
- Mae runs across the divide to Ray. Nobody shoots at her. The fighting stops when they see a woman running to her husband.

**Mae:** "RAY! Oh God, Ray—"

**Ray:** "I'm alright. I'm alright."

- The shooting stops. Everyone staring at Mae holding Ray in the middle of the street.
- Clark is standing alone. His rifle still smoking. Everyone saw him fire first.

**Ray:** *(looking at Clark)* "...You started this. You fired first."

**Clark:** "I was protecting—"

**Ray:** "You started this. I saw you."

- Tommy puts his gun down. Jeff puts his gun down. Even they can see it now.

---

## Mission 4: "Exile"

**Clark is expelled.**

- The evidence is overwhelming. The letters. The gate plan. Starting the firefight. Ray's testimony.
- Even Joseph's side turns. Nobody stands with Clark except silence.
- Joseph is sitting on a bench. Luisa beside him. He's mumbling. He doesn't understand what's happening.

**Jeremiah strips Clark's weapons.** The white sawed-off. The black M11 with the skull. Takes them personally.

**Clark:** *(being pushed toward the edge of town)* "You're making a mistake. Without me, you have nothing."

**Jeremiah:** "Without you, we have everything we need."

**Clark:** "The marshal is dead. The Watersons are dead. Who do you think is left? It's just you and your broken old man and a bunch of tired outlaws."

**Jeremiah:** "Walk."

**Clark:** "I'll come back, Jerry. And when I do—"

**Jeremiah:** "I said walk."

- Clark walks into the wilderness. White coat getting smaller. He looks back once. That smile. Then he's gone.
- **But Clark will come back. Chapter 16. To finish what he started.**

---

## Mission 5: "Together Again"

**The gang reunites.** The divide is over.

- Joseph's people and Jeremiah's people come back together. Slowly. Awkwardly.
- Ray gets patched up by Delia. Mae won't leave his side.
- Tommy apologizes to Jeremiah. "I didn't know. About Clark. I'm sorry."
- Jeff says nothing. Just extends his hand. Jeremiah shakes it.
- The barricades come down. The street is open again.
- Joseph is brought to Jeremiah by Luisa.

**Luisa:** "He has something he wants to say. He might not remember it in an hour. But right now, he knows."

**Joseph:** *(looking at Jeremiah, eyes clearer than they've been in weeks)* "...I'm sorry, son."

**Jeremiah:** "I know."

**Joseph:** "I don't know what I said. What I did. It's all... it's all mixed up. But I know I hurt you. And I'm sorry."

**Jeremiah:** "It's okay, Joseph."

**Joseph:** "Is it?"

**Jeremiah:** *(long pause)* "...It will be."

- The gang is one again. Wounded. Smaller. But one.
- For now.

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 15"** appears.

## Chapter 14 Summary
- Armed standoff between Jeremiah's side and Joseph's side
- Luisa breaks it by standing in the middle of the street
- Jeremiah walks unarmed toward Joseph — M4 pointed at his chest
- Clark fires a shot to start a firefight — Ray gets shot
- Ray and Mae's moment stops the violence
- **Clark exposed finally — fired first, everyone saw it**
- Clark expelled from the gang — stripped of weapons, walks into the wilderness
- **Clark will return in Chapter 16**
- The gang reunites
- Joseph apologizes to Jeremiah in a lucid moment — "I'm sorry, son"
