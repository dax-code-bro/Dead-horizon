# Marcus Epilogue — Mission 7: "A Good Night" (Full Dialogue)

---

## The Drive to Town

**Emily is in the passenger seat. Hair done. A dress she hasn't worn in years. Marcus keeps glancing at her.**

**Emily:** "Eyes on the road."

**Marcus:** "I'm driving fine."

**Emily:** "You've looked at me four times."

**Marcus:** "You look nice."

**Emily:** "I look nice every day."

**Marcus:** "You look... extra nice."

**Emily:** "You're not getting out of trouble with compliments."

**Marcus:** "I know. But you do."

---

## Arriving

**Marcus gets out first and opens Emily's door.**

**Emily:** "Since when do you open my door?"

**Marcus:** "Since I'm trying not to sleep in the barn again."

**Emily:** "Smart man."

---

## The Restaurant

**Small restaurant in town. White tablecloths. Candles. Soft music. A waiter leads them to a corner table.**

**Emily:** *(looking around)* "This is nice."

**Marcus:** "Don't sound so surprised."

**Emily:** "The last time you took me to dinner was..."

**Marcus:** "..."

**Emily:** "I actually can't remember."

**Marcus:** "That's on me."

**Emily:** "Yes it is."

---

## Ordering

**Emily gets the most expensive steak on the menu. Looks Marcus dead in the eye while she does it.**

**Marcus:** "You didn't even read the rest of the menu."

**Emily:** "Didn't need to."

**Marcus:** "I'll have the same."

**Waiter:** "And to drink?"

**Marcus:** "Whiskey."

**Emily:** "Water."

**Marcus:** "You sure?"

**Emily:** "Someone has to drive us home if you turn into Chapter 2 Marcus."

**Marcus:** "That was one time."

**Emily:** "You couldn't find the truck."

**Marcus:** "It was dark."

**Emily:** "It was right in front of you."

**Marcus:** "...It was dark."

---

## Remembering Jerry

**Emily:** "Josh asked me the other day if he could get a motorcycle."

**Marcus:** "He's twelve."

**Emily:** "That's what I said."

**Marcus:** "What'd he say?"

**Emily:** "He said Uncle Jerry would've let him."

*Both go quiet. The candle flickers.*

**Marcus:** "...He would've, too. That man had no sense when it came to that kid."

**Emily:** *(small laugh)* "Remember when Jerry let him ride Cerberus alone?"

**Marcus:** "He was five. I almost killed Jerry."

**Emily:** "Josh talked about it for weeks. 'Cerberus is MY horse now, daddy.'"

**Marcus:** "And Jerry just stood there smiling like he didn't almost give me a heart attack."

**Emily:** "That was Jerry. He never worried about the small stuff."

**Marcus:** "He worried about everything. He just didn't show it."

**Emily:** "I miss him."

**Marcus:** "Every day."

**Emily:** "Josh still has that drawing. The one he made of all of us. It's on his wall."

**Marcus:** "I know. I see it every time I go in his room."

**Emily:** "He drew Jerry the tallest."

**Marcus:** "Jerry was the tallest."

**Emily:** "Not by that much."

**Emily:** "Do you think he'd be proud of us?"

**Marcus:** "Yeah. I think he would."

**Emily:** "I think so too."

**Marcus:** "He'd probably say something like 'took you long enough.'"

**Emily:** *(laughing)* "That's exactly what he'd say."

---

## Marcus's Whiskey

**The plates are cleared. Marcus orders another whiskey. Emily steals a sip.**

**Marcus:** "I thought you were having water."

**Emily:** "I'm having YOUR whiskey. There's a difference."

**Marcus:** "That's still drinking."

**Emily:** "That's marriage. What's yours is mine."

---

## Marcus Gets Serious

**Marcus:** "Emily."

**Emily:** "Don't ruin this with an apology."

**Marcus:** "I'm not apologizing. I'm just... I want you to know something."

**Emily:** "What."

**Marcus:** "Every morning I wake up and you're next to me. And every morning I think about how I almost didn't have this. How I almost ran. How I almost lost you and Josh and all of it."

**Emily:** "Marcus—"

**Marcus:** "And every morning I choose this. Not because I have to. Because I want to. You're not my second chance, Emily. You're my only chance. And I'm not wasting it."

**Emily:** "When did you get good at words?"

**Marcus:** "I've been practicing."

**Emily:** "On who?"

**Marcus:** "The cows."

**Emily:** *(laughing, really laughing)* "You practice speeches on the cows?"

**Marcus:** "Oreo is a great listener."

**Emily:** "Oh my God."

**Marcus:** "Buck walks away but Oreo just stands there."

**Emily:** "Stop. I'm going to cry in this restaurant."

**Marcus:** "You wanted to know."

---

## The Flower

**After dinner. Walking through town. A street vendor closing up. Bucket of flowers on the cart. Marcus stops.**

**Emily:** "What are you doing?"

**Marcus:** "Hold on."

*He picks out a single flower. Pays. Walks back. Holds it out.*

**Emily:** "...A flower?"

**Marcus:** "Don't make fun of me."

**Emily:** "I'm not. It's just... you've never bought me a flower. In all the years I've known you. Not once."

**Marcus:** "I know."

**Emily:** "Why now?"

**Marcus:** "Because I should've been buying you flowers since the day I met you. And I can't go back and fix that. But I can start now."

*Emily takes the flower. Holds it carefully.*

**Emily:** "It's a daisy."

**Marcus:** "Is that good?"

**Emily:** "It's my favorite."

**Marcus:** "...I know."

**Emily:** "You knew?"

**Marcus:** "I pay attention. Sometimes."

*Emily kisses him. Soft. Quick. In the middle of the street under a flickering lamp.*

**Emily:** "...I'm still a little mad at you."

**Marcus:** "I know."

**Emily:** "But less mad."

**Marcus:** "I'll take it."

---

## The Drive Home

**Emily leans against Marcus in the truck. His arm around her. Her head on his shoulder. The flower in her lap. Dark fields. Stars overhead.**

**Emily:** "Marcus?"

**Marcus:** "Yeah."

**Emily:** "Thank you. For tonight."

**Marcus:** "Thank you for letting me take you."

**Emily:** "You owed me."

**Marcus:** "I always owe you."

**Emily:** "That's true."

**Emily:** "The daisy was a nice touch."

**Marcus:** "Oreo helped me pick it."

**Emily:** "I'm going to kill you."

**Marcus:** *(smiling)* "Worth it."

---

## Home

**They pull up to the ranch. Porch light on. Silver asleep on the porch. Jim barks once from inside.**

**Inside: Josh is asleep on the couch. Trevor's stew bowl empty on the table. TV on static.**

**Emily puts a blanket over Josh. Marcus turns off the TV.**

**Emily puts the daisy in a glass of water on the kitchen table. Steps back. Looks at it.**

**Marcus:** "Coming to bed?"

**Emily:** "Yeah."

*She touches the daisy one more time. Follows Marcus.*

**Final shot: the kitchen. Empty. Quiet. The daisy in a glass of water. The ranch through the window. Moonlight on the field. Everything still.**
