# Marcus Epilogue — Final Missions: The Redwood

---

## Mission: "Phil and Delia Arrive"

### Setup (Three weeks after the hunting mission)
- Phil and Delia arrive at the McAllen ranch in Phil's truck.
- Phil and Sadie got married — Sadie is in South America bounty hunting, back in a week.
- Emily is reluctant about Delia being there but warms up over a few days.
- Emily and Delia bond in the garden. Phil and Marcus catch up on the porch.

### The Real Reason
- Delia reveals to Emily: they think they know where Clark and Joseph are.
- Clark has a 24-member gang. Joseph is his puppet — cold, empty, barely human after 7 more years of brain damage.
- Clark has killed 3 former gang members and murdered a family near Redwood.
- Phil and Delia have been hunting former members — found Hector, Earl, and Ray so far.
- Clark is heading toward Phil's old house.
- Plan: ambush Clark when he arrives at Phil's abandoned house.

### Emily's Decision
- Emily goes to the bedroom. Pulls a crate from under her side of the bed.
- Inside: Marcus's old clothes, his Beretta 92FS Bronze, a rifle, ammunition. She's kept it cleaned and maintained for 7 years.
- They nod at each other. No words needed.
- Delia goes with Phil and Marcus instead of staying. They need a medic.
- Marcus tells Emily: if he's not back by sunrise, check under the porch. There's a box with money, documents, and bank keys. Take Josh. Take the horses. Ride south. Don't take roads.
- The escape plan has been under the porch since the day they moved in. Seven years.

## Mission: "Redwood"

### The Drive
- Marcus, Phil, and Delia drive Phil's truck into Redwood.
- Massive trees, red bark, dark forest floor. Canopy blocks sunlight. Misty. Quiet.
- They pass evacuation posters, abandoned Boy Scout camps. Clark cleared out the entire region.

### Clark's Men
- They see Clark's men dragging a burnt/beaten dead body from a wagon.
- The men destroy Phil's OLD truck at his house — slash tires, rip out the engine, tear out the alternator.
- Phil is furious about the irreplaceable parts.

### The Ambush Reversed
- They follow the wagon tracks on a dirt path branching from the road.
- It's a trap. Clark appears on a cliff with 14 armed men on a ledge below him.

**Clark:** "It's been a long time. Couldn't say I missed you. You know, once this is all over, how about I go pay Emily a visit? Like the good old days. Anyway, I don't wanna monologue you before your death. Finish them off, boys."

- Clark walks away. They fight the 14 men — easy work for three veterans.

### The Chase
- They find horses and chase Clark through the redwoods.
- Scripted chase — Clark can't die, his horse can't die. He shoots back but misses.
- Three followers can be killed during the chase.
- Chase leads to a small camp. Clark throws a grenade — kills the horses, knocks everyone off.

### The Camp — Five Houses
Player checks houses in any order:
1. Empty
2. A few of Clark's men — combat
3. Children — let them go
4. A few people
5. Clark appears behind Marcus with his arm around Delia's neck, gun to her head

### The Confrontation

**Clark has Delia hostage. Marcus aims the Beretta at Clark.**

- Clark taunts Marcus about the farm, about Emily.
- Phil is standing next to a door.
- **The door gets kicked open — JOSEPH appears.** Shoots Phil in the stomach.
- Joseph has new dual revolvers. Points one at Phil, one at Marcus.
- Joseph is cold, flat, empty — seven years of brain damage and Clark's conditioning.

### The Dialogue
- Marcus tries to reach Joseph — "It's me. It's Marcus."
- Clark: "He doesn't know you. He doesn't know anyone."
- Joseph: "You left. Everyone left."
- Marcus tells Joseph about Jeremiah's letter — "He said he forgives you. Even for Sarah. Even for Thomas and Lily."
- Joseph's gun arm drops half an inch.
- Clark screams at Joseph to focus.
- Marcus: "You shot him, Joseph. Do you remember what you did?"
- Joseph breaks through the emptiness: "...What did I do?"

### Joseph Turns
- **Joseph points a revolver at Clark.**
- The old Joseph surfaces through the damage — Marcus's words and Jeremiah's letter broke through.

## Shooting System (Game Feature)

### Not in Cover:
- **Aim + fire** — proper aimed shot, accurate
- **Fire only (no aim)** — hip fire, gun held low, inaccurate
  - Revolvers: pull hammer each shot manually
  - Semi-auto pistols: just fire, but after rapid shots need to work the action
  - Rifles/shotguns: hip fire spray

### Behind Cover:
- **Fire only (no aim)** — blind fire over/around cover, inaccurate but safe
- **Aim + fire** — pop out, expose yourself, aimed shot, duck back. Risk vs reward.
