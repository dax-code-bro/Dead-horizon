# Marcus's Epilogue — 2026

## Setting: McAllen Ranch — Hill overlooking a valley, 20 acres, fenced, cattle ranch
## Time: 2026 — Seven years after Jeremiah's death

---

## Status in 2026
- **Marcus McAllen** — 33 years old. Rancher. Married to Emily. Trying to go clean.
- **Emily McAllen** — Late 20s/early 30s. Wife. Settled. Running the household.
- **Josh McAllen** — 12 years old. 5'4". Looks like Marcus with Emily's features. Some say he has Joseph's hair. Bullet scar on his arm from Chapter 4. Ranch hand. Good kid.
- **The world** — Still recovering from the 2012 riots. Infrastructure rebuilding. Cars are back but society is scarred. Mix of modern and old ways.

## The Ranch
- Small barn-style home (barndominium) on a hill overlooking a valley
- 20 acres of fenced land
- Livestock: 4 female Angus cows, 1 Black Angus bull (Buck), 1 Ankole-Watusi bull (Oreo — new)
- A large working barn to the left
- Marcus's horse: **Mary** — rose gray American Quarter Horse
- Marcus's truck: beat-up pickup with a cattle trailer
- Marcus is trying to leave the outlaw life behind

---

## Mission 1: "Some Honest Work"

### The Morning
- Marcus wakes up. Emily beside him. Pajamas. Small barndominium.
- "Seven Years Later" appears on screen in white bold text.
- Marcus puts on rancher clothes. Steps outside.
- Goes to the big barn to find Josh.

### Finding Josh
- Josh is scooping cow manure. Doesn't look up. "Morning dad."
- Reports that one of the cows (Daisy — brown with white patch) isn't looking good. Not eating. Laying down. Limping.

### The Sick Cow
- Marcus goes to check on Daisy. She's limping.
- Buck (the Black Angus bull) is being dominant — won't let Marcus get close.
- Marcus: "You have got to be kidding me."
- Goes to the side of the barn where Mary is parked. Rides out on Mary.
- The horse is bigger than Buck — Buck backs off.
- **Gameplay: Lasso the cow without scaring the others. Calm her while lassoed.**
- Marcus takes Daisy to the barn. Puts her in a clamping machine. Rotates her on her side.
- Finds barbed wire stuck in her leg. Bloody but not terrible.
- Removes the barbed wire. Wraps the wound.
- Josh brings bandages and catches blood in a bucket — helps without being asked.
- Puts Daisy in a recovery pen in the barn. Doesn't let injured cows out with the herd.

### Mission Complete

## Mission 2: (Name TBD — "New Blood" or similar)

### Emily's Errand
- Emily tells Marcus they need to pick up the bull they bought.
- Marcus was recently robbed — lost most of his cattle. Down to 4 females and 1 male (Buck).
- Needs the new bull to rebuild the herd. It's breeding season.

### The Drive
- Marcus and Josh take the pickup truck with a cattle trailer.
- **First time player drives a vehicle instead of a horse** — jarring contrast after 16 chapters on horseback.
- Josh talks about what he wants to do in the future.
- Marcus talks about how much the world is changing.
- They pass through areas still recovering from the 2012 riots — rebuilt sections next to ruins.

### The Expo Barn
- Massive indoor livestock expo — based on Navarro County Expo. Metal roof, steel beams, dirt floor arena, wooden bleachers, blue metal fencing, sponsor banners. Crowded with ranchers and buyers.
- They enter the wrong section first — the market/show area. Crowded. Auction happening.
- They go to the right — the livestock breeding sales area. Sheep, rabbits, pigs, goats.
- Marcus tells Josh he can go pet the animals while he picks up the bull.

### Meeting the Seller / Buying Oreo
- Marcus finds the seller. Behind him: a massive cage with a very large Ankole-Watusi bull — white with black spots.
- His name is **Oreo.**
- They talk. Marcus hands over **$15,000.**
- **Why Oreo:** Ankole-Watusi produce two calves at once (rebuilds herd fast). It's breeding season. Oreo was bred for defense — protects the herd. After being robbed, Marcus needs a guardian, not just a breeder.

### Loading Oreo
- They load Oreo into the trailer. He's not cooperative.
- Oreo gives Marcus a **nasty gash on the arm** during loading.
- Marcus finds Josh petting a black rabbit, talking to an old man.
- They leave. Josh constantly looking back at Oreo through the rear window.

### Truck Dialogue — Josh Sees the Gash
- Josh notices Marcus's arm is bleeding.
- Marcus: "Oreo and I had a disagreement about getting in the trailer."
- Josh pulls up his sleeve — shows his bullet scar. "Now we match."
- Marcus: "Yours is from a bullet. Mine is from a cow." / Josh: "Exactly."
- Josh worries about how Buck will handle Oreo.

### Arrival Home — The Bull Fight
- They unload Oreo. Buck immediately charges.
- Marcus pulls his gun. Tells Josh to get behind him. Instinct — the outlaw is still in there.
- He lets them fight. It's dominance.
- **Oreo breaks Buck's horn clean off.** Gives Buck a gash on his leg.
- They both just... walk away and start grazing. It's over.
- Josh: "They almost killed each other and now they're just eating?" / Marcus: "That's how bulls work."
- Josh wants a baby Oreo. Marcus explains that's exactly what's going to happen — breeding season.

### Emily's Reaction
- Emily comes out. Sees Buck limping with a broken horn. Sees the massive Ankole-Watusi. Sees blood on Marcus's arm.
- "What IS that." / Marcus: "That's Oreo."
- Emily: "That's not a cow. That's a truck with horns."
- She's furious about the danger, the cost, Marcus's arm.
- "You are sleeping in the barn tonight." / "With your investment."
- She softens slightly — cleans his arm. But he's still in trouble.

### Mission Complete
