# Marcus Epilogue — Final Scene

---

## Clark's Death
- Marcus shoots the gun out of Clark's hand.
- Shoots both his shoulders.
- Sprints to him, grabs him by the white coat collar, pins him against the door.
- Beretta against Clark's forehead.
- **Marcus:** "You took everything from us. Our home. Our family. Our brother. And you smiled while you did it. So I want you to know — I'm not smiling."
- Clark looks afraid for the first time in the entire game.
- Marcus pulls the trigger.
- Drops Clark to the ground.
- **Marcus:** "That was for Jerry."
- Walks away. Doesn't look back.
- Clark Jones is dead. The white coat turning red. The smile gone forever.

## Joseph
- Joseph holstered his revolvers. Looked at everyone coldly. Backed up. Turned around. Walked into the redwoods.
- He chose not to be a killer anymore.
- His fate is left unknown — walked away into the forest.

## The Return Home
- Almost sunset. Marcus and Delia carry Phil back to the truck.
- They drive home. Emily and Josh are on the porch. Waiting. Watching the road.
- Emily and Josh run to Marcus when the truck arrives.
- Marcus: "I'm home."

## Phil and Delia Leave
- A few days later. Phil is patched up. Alive. Complaining.
- Phil and Delia saddle up and ride south.
- Phil: "Take care of that family, Marcus."
- Emily to Delia: "Us girls gotta stick together." / Delia: "Yeah. We do."
- They ride away.

## Final Shots
- **Soft music. Guitar. Warm.**
- Cookie (Oreo's calf) fighting Shadow (Buck's calf) in the field. Two babies bumping heads. New life figuring itself out.
- Oreo watches. Buck watches. Neither intervenes.
- Marcus on the porch. Emily beside him. Josh on the steps. Silver asleep at Josh's feet. Jim in the field. Sheep on the hillside. Mary and Bob at the horse pen.
- The sun going down. The valley. The horizon — alive. Green. Full.
- **Not dead. Not anymore.**
- Camera pulls back slowly. The whole ranch. The whole valley. The sky turning gold and purple.
- Everything Marcus built. Everything Jeremiah died for.
- Cookie and Shadow still bumping heads.
- **Fade to black.**
- **Silence.**
- **Credits roll. Soft music.**
- **Three seconds in: "For those who tried."**
- Credits continue. Music fades.
- **Final text: "Dead Horizon"**
- **The game is over.**

### Credits Messages
- **Jeremiah's death credits (both endings):** "Remember."

### The Title
- "Dead Horizon" — first referenced in Chapter 7 when Jeremiah stands on a roof looking at the empty land stretching to the edge of the world.
- By the epilogue, the horizon is no longer dead. It's alive. Full. Green. Marcus and his family made it that way.
