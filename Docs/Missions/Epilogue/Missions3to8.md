# Marcus Epilogue — Missions 3-8

---

## Mission 3: "Why?"

### Setup
- Marcus receives a letter from the state marshal — he's wanted. $100,000 bounty from his outlaw days.
- Instead of running, Marcus drives to town to pay it off. Going clean.

### Gameplay
- Marcus drives to the marshal's office in town.
- Pays the $100,000. Nearly wipes out their savings.
- The clerk doesn't give him grief — just takes the money, stamps the papers. Clean.
- Marcus drives home. Doesn't tell Emily.
- They are nearly broke but Emily doesn't know.

### Key Story Points
- Marcus chose the legal path — the old Marcus would have run or fought
- He's protecting Emily from stress by keeping the secret — but secrets are what almost destroyed them before
- $100,000 gone in one payment

---

## Mission 4: "Thinning the Flock"

### Setup
- Marcus needs money. Decides to sell 20 sheep at the livestock market.
- Still has 29 remaining after the sale.

### Gameplay
- Marcus and Josh load 20 sheep into a trailer.
- Drive to the market. Sell them.
- **$800,000 return.** Animals are still incredibly valuable in the recovering world.
- Marcus is relieved — financially stable again.
- But breeding season is coming for everything — cows, sheep, goats. Lots of work ahead.

### Key Story Points
- The financial crisis is solved but Emily still doesn't know it happened
- Marcus carried the weight alone — just like Charles used to

---

## Mission 5: "Welcome to the World"

### Setup
- Breeding season hits. Every animal on the ranch is giving birth.
- Marcus and Josh spend an entire day delivering babies.

### Gameplay
- **Audio-only births** — camera stays outside the pen. Player hears the mother struggling, Marcus pulling, then the baby animal's first cry.
- Josh helps clean up after each birth.
- Mother licks the membrane off the baby — the player sees this part. Natural and wholesome.
- Player goes between multiple pens throughout the day.

### The Births and Josh's Reactions

**Oreo's first calf — "Cookie":**
- Half Watusi, half Angus. Black and white patches. Tiny horn bumps.
- Josh: "He looks like a cow wearing a dalmatian costume."
- Josh names him Cookie. "Oreo. Cookie. It works."

**Buck's first calf — "Shadow":**
- Pure Black Angus. Already trying to run.
- Josh: "This one's all Buck."
- Names him Shadow.

**Oreo's twins — "Salt" and "Pepper":**
- One white with black spots. One black with white belly.
- Josh: "We're a seasoning ranch now."

**The lambs:**
- Multiple ewes giving birth across the field. Josh and Marcus sprinting between them. Jim the Border Collie going crazy.
- Josh holds a tiny lamb: "Dad this one fits in my hands."
- Marcus: "Don't get attached." / Josh: "Too late."
- Names her Marshmallow. Marcus: "We're not naming the sheep." / Josh: "Marshmallow doesn't care what you think."
- Could be 30-40 lambs total.

**The goat kids:**
- A baby goat immediately headbutts Josh in the shin. Three times.
- Josh: "This goat hates me." / Marcus: "That's how they play."
- Josh names him Brick. "Because his head feels like one."

**End of day:**
- Marcus and Josh sit on the fence. Covered in mess. Field full of newborns wobbling on new legs.
- Josh: "This is way better than the expo."
- Marcus puts his arm around Josh. They watch the sun go down.

## Mission 6: "The Secret's Out"

### Setup
- Emily finds a bank statement in the mail. Sees the $100,000 is gone. Figures out what happened from the statements — the payment, the sheep sale, the deposit.
- The money is back. That's not the problem. The secret is.

### The Confrontation

**Emily:** "The problem is you didn't TELL me."

**Marcus:** "I didn't want you to worry."

**Emily:** "I'm not some girl at camp you need to protect from bad news. I'm your wife."

- Emily is furious — not about the money, about the lie.
- She connects it to the old Marcus: "That was the OLD Marcus. The one who ran away."
- Marcus explains: $100K bounty from his past. Paid it to keep the family safe. Didn't want her to see the man he used to be.
- Emily: "I already know who you used to be. I was THERE."
- Emily: "No more secrets. Promise me." / Marcus: "I promise." / Emily: "On Josh's life." / Marcus: "On Josh's life."

### The Date Offer
- Marcus: "Let me take you out. Dinner. Just us."
- Emily: "I'm still angry." / Marcus: "Be angry at me over a steak."
- Emily agrees reluctantly. Josh stays home with Trevor's stew recipe.

**Josh:** "What'd you do?" / Marcus: "Something stupid." / Josh: "Again?" / Emily: "Again."

## Mission 7: "A Good Night"

### Setup
- Marcus and Emily go on a date in town. Just the two of them. First time in a long time.

### The Date
- Marcus drives Emily into town in the truck.
- They go to a restaurant — nicer than anything they had during the gang days. White tablecloth. Candles. Real glasses.

**Emily:** "I don't remember the last time we did this."

**Marcus:** "Neither do I."

**Emily:** "That's the problem."

**Marcus:** "I know. I've been so focused on the ranch and keeping everything running that I forgot to... be here. With you."

**Emily:** "You're here now."

**Marcus:** "Yeah. I am."

- They eat. They talk. Not about the money. Not about the past. About Josh. About the ranch. About the future.

**Emily:** "Josh asked me the other day if he could get a motorcycle."

**Marcus:** "He's twelve."

**Emily:** "That's what I said."

**Marcus:** "What'd he say?"

**Emily:** "He said Uncle Jerry would've let him."

*Both of them go quiet for a second. The name still carries weight.*

**Marcus:** "...He would've, too. That man had no sense when it came to that kid."

**Emily:** *(small laugh)* "Remember when Jerry let him ride Cerberus alone?"

**Marcus:** "He was five. I almost killed Jerry."

**Emily:** "Josh talked about it for weeks."

*A comfortable silence. The kind that only comes from years together.*

**Emily:** "I miss him."

**Marcus:** "Every day."

**Emily:** "Do you think he'd be proud of us?"

**Marcus:** "...Yeah. I think he would."

- After dinner, they walk through town. Marcus buys Emily a flower from a street vendor. Simple. Nothing fancy.

**Emily:** "A flower?"

**Marcus:** "Don't make fun of me."

**Emily:** "I'm not. It's just... you've never bought me a flower."

**Marcus:** "First time for everything."

**Emily:** *(holding it)* "...I'm still a little mad at you."

**Marcus:** "I know."

**Emily:** "But less mad."

**Marcus:** "I'll take it."

- They drive home. Emily leans against Marcus in the truck. His arm around her. Just like the wagon in Chapter 2 — except now there's a ring on his finger and seven years of proof that he stayed.

### Key Story Points
- First real date Marcus and Emily have had in the game
- Jerry's name still comes up naturally — he's part of their family even in death
- Emily: "Do you think he'd be proud of us?" — the question that matters most
- The flower — Marcus's first romantic gesture in the entire game that isn't in a tent
- Emily forgives him. Not all the way. But enough.

## Mission 8: "Old Friends"

### Setup
- A few days later. Marcus is working on the fence when a truck pulls up the driveway. Silver stands up on the porch. Barking.
- Marcus reaches for the gun on his hip instinctively. Then stops. Recognizes the truck.
- A man steps out. Late 50s. Weathered. Cowboy hat. Big smile.
- **This is Hank Dawson** — an old friend of Marcus's father. Not the gang. Before the gang. From Marcus's childhood. A legitimate rancher who knew Marcus's family before everything went wrong.

### The Visit

**Hank:** "Marcus McAllen. Look at you. Last time I saw you, you were yea high."

**Marcus:** "Mr. Dawson. It's been... a long time."

**Hank:** "Your daddy would be proud seeing this place. Twenty acres. Cattle. You did good, son."

**Marcus:** "Forty acres."

**Hank:** "Even better."

- Hank comes inside. Meets Emily and Josh. He's warm, genuine. Tells stories about Marcus's father — the good ones, from before.
- Josh listens wide-eyed. He's never heard stories about his grandfather from someone outside the gang.

**Josh:** "You knew my grandpa?"

**Hank:** "Knew him? I grew up with him. We used to race horses down Miller's Creek. He always won. Cheated, but he won."

- Emily makes coffee. It's a normal visit. Just people talking.

### The Check

- Before Hank leaves, he pulls Marcus aside.

**Hank:** "Listen, Marcus. Your daddy... he lent me money years ago. Before things went south for him. I never got to pay him back. He wouldn't take it. Stubborn fool."

**Marcus:** "Mr. Dawson, you don't have to—"

**Hank:** "I know I don't have to. I want to. Your daddy was a good man before the world made him otherwise. And you turned out alright despite everything."

- Hank hands Marcus an envelope. Inside: a check for **$250,000.**

**Marcus:** "I can't take this."

**Hank:** "It's not charity. It's a debt. Your father's money. It belongs to you."

**Marcus:** "Two hundred and fifty thousand—"

**Hank:** "Your daddy lent me fifty thousand in 1998. That's fifty thousand plus twenty-seven years of interest and guilt. Take the damn check, Marcus."

*Marcus stares at it. His father — a man he barely knew — left something behind that wasn't pain or debt or shame. He left something good.*

**Marcus:** "...Thank you, Mr. Dawson."

**Hank:** "Call me Hank. And take care of that family. They're the real treasure."

- Hank tips his hat to Emily and Josh. Gets in his truck. Drives off.
- Marcus stands in the driveway holding the check. Emily walks up behind him.

**Emily:** "What was that about?"

**Marcus:** "My father lent him money. Years ago. He's paying it back."

**Emily:** "How much?"

**Marcus:** "...Two hundred and fifty thousand."

**Emily:** "Marcus."

**Marcus:** "I know."

**Emily:** "Your father—"

**Marcus:** "I know."

*They stand there. Looking at the check. The ranch. The valley. Josh running after Brick the goat in the field. Silver barking at nothing on the porch.*

**Emily:** "Maybe we should tell Josh. About his grandfather. The good parts."

**Marcus:** "Yeah. Maybe we should."

### Key Story Points
- **Hank Dawson** — connection to Marcus's father from before the outlaw life
- Marcus's father was a good man before the world broke him — parallels Marcus's own arc
- **$250,000 check** — the ranch is financially secure for real now
- Josh hears stories about his grandfather for the first time
- Marcus's past delivers something positive for once — not a bounty, not a threat, but a gift
- Emily: "Maybe we should tell Josh the good parts" — the family is ready to face the past honestly
