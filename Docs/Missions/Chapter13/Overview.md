# Chapter 13: "The Long Walk"

## Setting: Abandoned Town
## Missions: 5
## Tone: Exhaustion. Acceptance. The calm before the final storm.

---

## Mission 1: "Last Ones Standing"

**The gang is a skeleton.**

- Jeremiah's side: Phil (wounded), Sadie, Hector, Rosa, Earl, Faye, Nate, Alma, Delia. Maybe 12 people total including non-combatants.
- Joseph's side: Clark, Luisa, Ray, Tommy, Jeff. Maybe 8.
- About 20 people left from the original 37.
- The town feels empty. Half the buildings are abandoned again.
- **Gameplay: Walk through the town.** Talk to who's left. Each conversation feels like it could be the last.
- Alma sits at Charles's grave every morning now. She's aged years in months.
- Phil can walk but limps badly. Sadie won't leave his side.
- Earl and Faye are the same as always. Steady. But even they look tired.

---

## Mission 2: "The Prodigal Son"

**Jeremiah crosses the street.**

- He walks to Joseph's side of the town. Clark immediately blocks his path.

**Clark:** "He doesn't want to see you."

**Jeremiah:** "I wasn't asking you."

**Clark:** "Jerry—"

**Jeremiah:** *(hand on the Cattleman)* "Move."

- Clark moves. For now. He knows the endgame is coming.
- Jeremiah finds Joseph in a room. Sitting in a chair. Staring at the wall. Luisa is there.
- Joseph doesn't recognize him at first. Then a flicker.

**Joseph:** "...Jerry?"

**Jeremiah:** "Hey, Joseph."

**Joseph:** "Where have you been? I've been... I was looking for you."

**Jeremiah:** "I've been right here. Across the street."

**Joseph:** "Right. Right. The street."

- They sit together. Joseph talks in fragments. Jumps between years. Mentions Jeremiah's father. Mentions Charles as if he's alive. Asks about Trevor.
- Jeremiah doesn't correct him. Just lets him talk.

**Joseph:** "Jerry... am I dying?"

**Jeremiah:** "...No, Joseph. You're not dying."

**Joseph:** "It feels like I am. Everything's... it's all slipping. Like water through my hands."

**Jeremiah:** "I know."

**Joseph:** "Stay with me? For a while?"

**Jeremiah:** "...Yeah. I'll stay."

- They sit in silence. Two men who used to be father and son. The gap between them filled with lies, death, and damage. But for a moment — just a moment — it's quiet.
- Clark watches from the doorway. His jaw tight.

---

## Mission 3: "Ghosts"

**Jeremiah has a dream sequence.**

- He falls asleep in the town church. The screen goes hazy.
- **He's back in the snow camp from Chapter 1.** Everything is intact. The tents. The stables. The fire.
- He walks through camp. Everyone is there. Everyone.
- **Lenny** is sitting by the fire, cleaning a gun. He looks up and smiles. Doesn't speak.
- **Trevor** is cooking. Stirs a pot. Tips his hat at Jeremiah.
- **Charles** is in his chair. The worn 1911 in his hand. He nods.
- **Joseph** — the real Joseph, lucid, sharp, clear-eyed — is standing by the stables. He turns.

**Joseph (dream):** "There you are. I've been waiting."

**Jeremiah:** "Waiting for what?"

**Joseph (dream):** "For you to come home."

- Then the dream shifts. The camp burns. Slowly. Everyone fades to ash. Lenny first. Then Trevor. Then Charles. Then Joseph.
- Jeremiah is standing alone in the snow. Ashes falling like snowflakes.
- He wakes up in the church. Gasping. The melted toy soldier has fallen from his pocket onto the floor.

---

## Mission 4: "Clark's Endgame"

**Clark makes his final move.**

- He tells Joseph that Jeremiah is coming to kill him. That the visit in Mission 2 was Jeremiah "scouting" to find the right moment.
- Joseph — damaged, confused, afraid — believes him.
- Clark has armed Joseph's side. Tommy, Ray, Jeff — they've been told Jeremiah's people are planning an attack.
- Clark approaches Ray privately.

**Clark:** "When it starts, you need to be ready."

**Ray:** "When what starts?"

**Clark:** "Jeremiah's going to make a move on Joseph. We need to protect him."

**Ray:** "Jerry wouldn't—"

**Clark:** "He already met with the marshal. He's been planning this for months. I've been protecting Joseph from it, but I can't do it alone anymore."

- Ray doesn't fully believe it. But he's scared. And scared people do what they're told.

---

## Mission 5: "The Eve"

**The night before everything ends.**

- The air is different. Everyone can feel it. Something is going to happen tomorrow.
- Jeremiah walks through his side of the town one last time.
- **He visits everyone:**

**Phil:** "Whatever happens tomorrow, you give 'em hell."

**Hector:** *(sharpening his knife)* "I'll be there. Right behind you."

**Earl:** *(nods)*

**Alma:** *(at Charles's grave)* "He'd be proud of you, Jerry."

**Nate:** "I'm not scared."

**Jeremiah:** "Good."

**Nate:** "...Okay, I'm a little scared."

**Jeremiah:** "That just means you're paying attention."

- Jeremiah goes to the roof one last time. The dead horizon. The same view. But different now. He's not looking at it wondering what's out there. He's looking at it knowing he might not see it again.

- He reaches into his pocket. The letter to Joseph. He reads it one more time.

*"I forgive you."*

- He puts it back. Takes out the melted toy soldier. Holds it up to the moonlight. A tiny deformed piece of plastic that means everything.

**Jeremiah:** *(to himself, to Sarah, to Thomas, to Lily)* "I'll be there soon."

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 14"** appears.

---

## Chapter 13 Summary
- The gang is barely 20 people split in a dead town
- Jeremiah visits Joseph — a heartbreaking moment of near-recognition
- Joseph asks "Am I dying?" — Jeremiah lies: "No."
- Dream sequence: the snow camp from Chapter 1, everyone alive, then everything burns
- Clark tells Joseph that Jeremiah is coming to kill him — arms Joseph's side
- Clark manipulates Ray into being ready for a "defense"
- Jeremiah visits everyone on his side — saying goodbye without saying it
- On the roof: "I'll be there soon." — talking to his dead family
- The stage is set for the final confrontation
