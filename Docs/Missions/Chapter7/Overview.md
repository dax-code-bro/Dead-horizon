# Chapter 7: "Borrowed Time"

## Setting: Abandoned Town (Post-Battle)
## Missions: 6
## Tone: Uneasy peace. The gang won a battle but they're bleeding out slowly.

---

## Mission 1: "Dust and Stitches"

**The morning after the battle.** The town is wrecked. Buildings smoking. Bodies in the streets. The gang won but it doesn't feel like winning.

- Jeremiah organizes cleanup and repair. He's the de facto leader now — Joseph can't function.
- **Gameplay: Town repair mission.** The player helps rebuild barricades, clear bodies, treat the wounded.
- Delia is overwhelmed as sole medic. Two unnamed gang members died in the fight. Several wounded.
- Clark is visibly shaken — or acting like it. He "helps" with the wounded. People thank him.
- Joseph wanders the main street confused. Asks Luisa why there are bodies. She has to explain the battle to him. He doesn't remember it.

**Key moment:** Jeremiah finds a dead militia soldier with a family photo in his pocket. Wife and two kids. He stares at it. Puts it back. Walks away.

---

## Mission 2: "The Letter"

**The marshal sends a message.** A rider approaches the town under a white flag. He's not military — just a courier. He delivers a sealed letter addressed to "The Walkns Gang."

The letter reads: *"You won a battle. I have an army. The next time I come, I won't stop at your walls. I'll burn them down with you inside. You have one week to send out Field and Walkns. After that, there is no mercy. — Marshal Samuel Harding."*

**The marshal now has a name: Samuel Harding.**

- The gang debates what to do. Voices are split.
- Clark quietly says "Maybe we should consider it. For the women and children."
- Marcus nearly hits him. Jeremiah holds him back — but this time it's harder.
- Charles: "We don't negotiate with men who shoot children through wagons."
- **Jeremiah burns the letter in front of everyone.** Statement made.

**Key moment:** After the meeting, Clark finds the courier before he leaves town. Gives him a folded note. "Make sure the marshal gets this personally." Nobody sees.

---

## Mission 3: "Supply Run from Hell"

**The gang needs supplies badly.** Medicine especially — Delia's running out.

- Jeremiah takes Marcus, Phil, and Hector to raid a military supply wagon on the road.
- **Gameplay: Ambush and robbery.** Set up on the road, stop the wagon, take what they need.
- It goes wrong — the wagon has a military escort. Bigger than expected.
- **Major gunfight on the road.** Phil uses dynamite to block the road. Marcus covers from elevation. Hector flanks with his knife.
- They get the supplies but Phil takes shrapnel in the arm. Not life-threatening but he's down for a while.
- On the ride back, Jeremiah and Marcus talk about the marshal.

**Marcus:** "One week. He gave us one week."

**Jeremiah:** "We're not giving him anyone."

**Marcus:** "Then we need a plan that isn't just 'sit here and wait for them to come back.'"

**Jeremiah:** "I'm working on it."

**Marcus:** "Work faster."

---

## Mission 4: "Bedtime Story"

**A quiet mission.** Night. The town is still. Guards posted.

- Jeremiah walks through camp. Checks on people.
- Finds Emily reading to Josh by candlelight in one of the old houses. Marcus is asleep next to them — exhausted from the supply run.
- Emily invites Jeremiah to sit. Josh wants Uncle Jerry to tell a story.
- **Jeremiah tells Josh a story** — but it's really about his own life. A soldier who traveled far from home, found a family, lost them, and found another family he didn't expect.
- Josh falls asleep before the ending. Emily looks at Jeremiah.

**Emily:** "How does it end? The story."

**Jeremiah:** "...I don't know yet."

**Emily:** "Make it a good ending, Jerry."

- Jeremiah walks back to his spot. Sits alone. The town is quiet. For one night, nothing goes wrong.

**Key moment:** This is Jeremiah writing his own story in his head. He's starting to sense the end is coming — not specifically, but generally. The feeling that time is running out.

---

## Mission 5: "The Con"

**A different kind of job.** Jeremiah plans something smart instead of violent.

- There's a wealthy merchant passing through the region with a horse caravan.
- Instead of robbing him, Jeremiah, Marcus, and Charles pose as horse traders looking to buy.
- **Gameplay: Social/deception mission.** The player has dialogue choices. Say the right things, don't raise suspicion, negotiate a deal.
- They get close to the merchant's operation and discover he's actually a Waterson business partner — someone who survived because he wasn't at the ranch.
- His name is **Arthur Bellamy.** He's been running Waterson operations since the family was killed.
- Jeremiah realizes the Watersons aren't truly dead — the empire survived.
- They complete the "deal," steal three horses during the handoff, and ride out before Bellamy realizes.

**Key moment:** The Waterson threat isn't over. Arthur Bellamy is rebuilding. And he'll want revenge for the family.

---

## Mission 6: "One Week"

**The marshal's deadline arrives.**

- The week is up. Everyone knows it. The tension is unbearable.
- Jeremiah expects an attack. The town is fortified. Everyone in position.
- **But the attack doesn't come.** The day passes. Then the night. Nothing.
- It's worse than an attack. The waiting. The not knowing.
- Clark makes a comment: "Maybe he changed his mind." But his eyes say something different.

**Why the marshal didn't come:** The player doesn't know yet. But Clark's note to the courier said: "Don't come yet. I need more time. I'll deliver them both." The marshal is waiting for Clark.

- The chapter ends with the gang in limbo. Safe for now. But everyone knows it's temporary.

**Final scene:** Jeremiah stands on the roof of the tallest building. Looking out at the dark horizon. Nothing moving. No dust. No torches. Just empty land stretching to the edge of the world. A dead horizon.

*The title of the game. This is where it comes from.*

### Chapter End

## Chapter 7 Summary
- Marshal has a name: **Samuel Harding** — the 4th antagonist
- Clark still hidden, still informing, told the marshal to wait
- Phil wounded (shrapnel)
- **Arthur Bellamy** discovered — Waterson business partner, rebuilding the empire
- The Waterson threat lives on
- Jeremiah tells Josh a story that's really about himself
- Emily: "Make it a good ending, Jerry."
- The marshal's deadline passes without attack — more unsettling than combat
- The game's title explained: Jeremiah staring at a dead horizon
