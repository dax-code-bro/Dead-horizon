# Chapter 4, Mission 4: (Name TBD — Waterson Ranch Raid)

## Chapter: 4
## Location: Waterfall Cave → Waterson Ranch → Waterfall Cave

---

## Setup
- Joseph is questioning loyalty. His brain damage makes him paranoid and aggressive.
- Threatening letters from the Watersons have been found near camp (Marcus, Joseph, and Phil received them).
- The Watersons know where the gang is.
- Joseph decides to take ALL 18 men to the Waterson ranch to shut them down permanently.
- This leaves all women and children at the waterfall cave completely unprotected.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Departure
- Joseph demands every man rides. No one stays behind.
- Ray, Owen, and Tommy push back — "We started this. This is your grudge."
- Joseph: "I built this gang. You are my children. And now you're telling me you won't ride?"
- Silas: "We'll ride. But some of us might not ride back."
- Joseph: "Then you die with family."
- They ride out. Emily watches Marcus leave. Marcus looks back: "I'm coming back."

### Section 2: Night Assault — Front Gate
- They arrive at night. Jeremiah kicks the front gate open.
- Gang floods in from both sides.
- Guards scramble — 54 guards on the front grounds.
- Joseph walks through the gate. One of the Waterson sons steps onto the porch.
- **Joseph:** "I knocked on your door. The least you could do is die with some manners."
- Joseph draws the white 1911 — fastest draw he's ever done. One shot. The son drops.
- **COMBAT: 54 guards across the front grounds.**

### Section 3: Inside the Mansion
- Phil kicks the front door. Grand foyer — red carpet, crystal chandeliers, oil paintings.
- They clear room by room:

**First room (parlor):** Empty.

**Second room (sitting room):** One son + four armed men. Quick firefight. Son captured. Joseph shoots his knee for information. Son reveals the mother is on the fourth floor study.

**Third room (library):** Two sons + six guards barricaded behind desks. Phil wants to use dynamite. Jeremiah: "You'll bring the whole house down." Firefight among exploding books. One son killed, one surrenders.

**Fourth floor study:** The father (Harold), two more sons, and the mother sitting in a leather chair drinking whiskey. Completely calm.

### Section 4: The Confrontation
- The father waves a revolver, shaking. The mother tells him to put it down.
- The mother addresses Joseph calmly: "So you're the one who stole my horses. Robbed my trolley. Killed my son outside."
- She warns: "I know about the cave. I know about the waterfall. I know about the women and children you left behind tonight."
- Charles and Jeremiah urge Joseph to leave — the message is sent.
- **Joseph refuses.** He shoots the father. Clark and Phil drop the two sons. Joseph presses the 1911 to the mother's forehead.
- **The Mother:** "You're a dead man."
- **Joseph:** Pulls the trigger.
- The entire Waterson family is dead.

### Section 5: Return to Camp
- The ranch burns behind them. Someone lit it.
- They ride back to the waterfall exhausted.
- Each couple has their reunion moment:
  - Luisa grabs Joseph's face — he pulls away, walks into the cave
  - Emily holds Marcus — "Don't ever leave me like that again"
  - Faye and Earl exchange nods
  - Mae grabs Ray — "Don't you ever scare me like that"
  - Sadie asks Phil about the dynamite — "Jerry wouldn't let me"
  - Danny sits next to Nell in silence
  - June tells Owen he's too old for this
  - Alma and Charles sit together — "He killed all of them"
  - Trevor tells Delia he watched a man execute an old woman in a chair

### Section 6: The Fallout
- Marcus confronts Jeremiah about Joseph: "He executed a whole family tonight."
- Marcus: "I'm done following him blind."
- Jeremiah admits: "I know something's wrong with him."
- They agree to figure it out together.
- Marcus goes to his tent where Emily and Josh are waiting. Takes Emily's hand in the dark.
- Jeremiah sits alone with the melted toy soldier.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- The entire Waterson family killed — second antagonist eliminated (all 9 members)
- Joseph's most disturbing moment — executing an unarmed woman in a chair
- The mother's warning about the cave/women being unprotected was true
- Marcus's loyalty has fully shifted from Joseph to his family
- Jeremiah finally acknowledges something is wrong with Joseph
- The gang's faith in Joseph is at its lowest point
- Every couple's reunion reflects their relationship dynamic
