# Chapter 4, Mission 5: Turns Out Scouting Pays Off (Final Mission)

## Chapter: 4 (Final Mission)
## Location: Waterfall Cave → Road → Ambush Site → New Camp

---

## Setup
- Clark claims he had a close call with lawmen in Las York.
- Clark pushes the gang to move — says he found a good spot.
- In reality, Clark has sold out the gang and is leading them into a law enforcement ambush.
- The gang packs up the waterfall cave and moves out.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Packing Up
- Short, simple mission. Everyone packs up.
- Jeremiah helps load wagons, get people ready.
- They leave the waterfall cave — fifth location abandoned.

### Section 2: The Ambush
- On the road, Joseph thinks he sees a scope glare on a hill.
- Jeremiah brushes it off.
- Then Jeremiah sees what looks like hats of two men.
- Marcus looks through a gap in the wagon — sees 12 lawmen.
- Immediately they bring out metal flaps and cover the front of the wagon.
- Everyone hops in the wagon. Josh is smooshed into a corner.

### Section 3: The Kill Box
- They roll into a circle of armed lawmen.
- The lawmen open fire.
- Both backup horses attached to the wagon are killed.
- Bullet holes riddle the wood.
- The lawmen think everyone inside must be dead.
- One lawman goes to check the back of the wagon.

### Section 4: The Counterattack
- The gang bursts out of the back of the wagon.
- **COMBAT: 40 lawmen**
- The gang wins — but at a cost.

### Section 5: Josh Is Hit
- **Josh has been grazed on the shoulder by a bullet.**
- Emily screams for Trevor.
- Marcus drops his Beretta, checks Josh: "It's a graze. He's okay."
- Emily: "He is NOT okay. He's FIVE."
- Trevor patches him up — clean graze, will leave a scar.

### Section 6: Reactions

**Marcus:**
- Blames himself. Sits outside tent with Beretta on his lap.
- "I put him in that corner. I picked that spot."
- Emily: "Don't go backwards. Not now."

**Charles:**
- Confronts Clark: "Who picked this route?"
- "A child just took a bullet."

**Marcus connects the dots:**
- To Jeremiah: "Clark keeps being where the law is."
- "I'm saying it's a pattern. And my son just bled for it."

**Joseph:**
- Momentarily lucid — talks to Josh, calls him tough.
- Orders a perimeter, guards on every side. Old Joseph flickers back.

**Emily:**
- "If anything like this ever happens again, I'm taking Josh and leaving."
- "I'm not asking, Marcus. I'm telling you."

**Camp mood:**
- Nobody talks. Nobody drinks. Nobody plays guitar.
- A five-year-old got shot today. The camp feels it.

### Section 7: Setting Up Camp
- They set up in the clearing where the ambush happened.
- Guards posted everywhere.

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 5 — Several Months Later"** appears.

### Mission Complete — End of Chapter 4

---

- Josh's shoulder healed — left a scar he's proud of
- Josh says "I love you daddy" for the first time
- Marcus/Emily party night happens — Marcus says "my family" out loud for first time
- Marcus's relationship with his family: nearly perfect by Chapter 5
- Joseph's brain damage: continued deterioration, lucid windows getting shorter
- Clark: still trusted by most, but Marcus and Charles have suspicions
