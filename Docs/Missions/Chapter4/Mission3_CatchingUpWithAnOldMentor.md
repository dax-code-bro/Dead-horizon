# Chapter 4, Mission 3: Catching Up with an Old Mentor Again

## Chapter: 4
## Location: Waterfall Cave → Las York (City)

---

## Setup
- Joseph is having a good day — one of his lucid windows.
- He takes Jeremiah into Las York for a father-son day. Fishing, poker, messing around, just walking.
- Joseph cycles between lucid days/weeks and episodes of confusion, aggression, and memory loss.
- The good days get shorter and rarer as the game progresses.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Part 1: Fishing
- River on the east side of Las York. Stone bridge overhead.
- Joseph talks about Jeremiah's father for the first time: "He was a stubborn son of a bitch. Just like you."
- Joseph: "He would've been proud of you. I'm proud of you too."
- Jeremiah catches a pathetic fish. Joseph calls it "a disappointment." Real laughs.
- **Brain damage moment:** Joseph's eyes go distant. Stares at the water. Blanks out. Comes back. Pretends it was a fish bite.

### Part 2: Poker
- Saloon in the heart of Las York. Nicer than Hope.
- They play poker against three men — a merchant, a rancher, a well-dressed man.
- Joseph is sharp — reading the table, bluffing. Looks like the old Joseph.
- Jeremiah: "Since when are you good at poker?" / Joseph: "I've always been good at everything."
- Jeremiah: "You burned eggs last week." / Joseph: "That was strategic."
- They cash out before the well-dressed man gets ugly.

### Part 3: The Karen
- Walking through the main street. Joseph buys hot drinks.
- A rich man in an expensive coat bumps into Joseph. Spills his drink.
- Rich man insults them — "People like you shouldn't be on this side of the city."
- Joseph shows his white 1911: "You talk like you're bulletproof."
- Rich man hands over cash, buys them new drinks, walks away shaking.
- Joseph: "Was that necessary?" / "Absolutely not. But it felt incredible."

### Part 4: The Walk
- Late afternoon. Quiet streets on the outskirts.
- Joseph quotes Charles: "The day you stop walking is the day you start dying."
- Joseph asks Jeremiah to promise to take care of everyone if something happens to him.
- Joseph: "You turned out alright. Your old man would've been proud. I'm proud."
- Jeremiah: "You already said that today."
- **Brain damage moment:** Joseph doesn't remember saying it at the river.
- Joseph: "Well. It's worth saying twice."
- They ride back to the waterfall in silence as the sun goes down.

### Mission Complete

---

## Joseph's TBI Behavior Pattern
- **Lucid windows:** Days or weeks of near-normal behavior. Sharp, funny, decisive.
- **Episodes:** Forgetting names, words, events. Talking to nobody. Disorientation. Aggression. Paranoia. Schizophrenia-like symptoms.
- **Progression:** Good periods get shorter. Bad periods get longer. By late chapters, lucid moments are almost gone.

## Key Story Points
- One of Joseph's last truly good days
- Two subtle brain damage moments the player can catch
- Joseph talks about Jeremiah's father — rare and meaningful
- Joseph asks Jeremiah to lead if something happens — foreshadowing
- He says "I'm proud" twice and doesn't remember the first time
- Father-son relationship at its warmest before the decline
