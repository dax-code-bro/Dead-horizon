# Chapter 4, Mission 2: Goodbye Old Friend

## Chapter: 4
## Location: Waterfall Cave → Las York → Execution Site → Waterfall Cave

---

## Required Pre-Mission Event: Marcus's Tent
- Before Mission 2 starts, the player witnesses Marcus inviting Emily and Josh to sleep in his tent.
- Full dialogue scene. Marcus: "I want you and Josh to come sleep in my tent."
- Josh is excited — "It has four walls!"
- Jeremiah watches Josh run to Marcus's tent, pulls out Thomas's melted toy soldier.

---

## Setup
- Michael Washington has been captured by the law and is set to be executed by firing squad.
- Washington's boys secretly rigged the firing squad rifles to explode when cocked — a rescue plan.
- Joseph learns about the rigged guns and plans to swap them for working ones.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Swap (Night Before)
- Joseph, Jeremiah, Clark, and Charles sneak into the town armory.
- **Stealth gameplay:** Find the rigged rifles, swap them for working ones. Clean, quiet.

### Section 2: The Execution (Next Morning)
- Town square. Crowd gathered. Washington led out in chains.
- Washington is laughing — he knows the guns are rigged. Confident. Smiling.
- The lawman reads his rights.
- "Ready!" — six lawmen raise rifles.
- "Aim!" — Washington still smiling.
- "Fire!" — rifles cock. They DON'T explode. Washington's face goes pale.
- Six shots. Washington collapses. Six bullets in his chest.
- Firing squad leaves.

### Section 3: Joseph's Revenge
- Joseph walks through the square. Stops at Washington's body.
- Washington is still wheezing. Still barely alive. Looks up at Joseph.
- Washington mumbles one word: "...You..."
- **Joseph:** "That's for my brother, you piece of shit."
- Joseph presses his white 1911 to Washington's forehead. Pulls the trigger.
- Joseph holsters the gun. Walks away without looking back.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- **Michael Washington is dead** — first main antagonist eliminated
- Joseph got personal revenge for his brother's murder
- The gang outsmarted Washington's rescue plan
- One of Joseph's last truly decisive, clear-headed moments
- Charles: "It's done." Jeremiah: "Yeah. It's done."
