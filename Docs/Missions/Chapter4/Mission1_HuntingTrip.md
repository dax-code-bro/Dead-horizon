# Chapter 4, Mission 1: (Name TBD — Hunting Trip)

## Chapter: 4
## Location: Waterfall Cave → Forest

---

## Setup
- The gang is settling into the waterfall cave after Chapter 3.
- A quiet mission to let the player breathe after Lenny's death and the Hill evacuation.
- Jeremiah and Trevor go on a peaceful hunting trip with a .50 cal rifle.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Hunt
- Jeremiah and Trevor ride into the forest. No plan. No target. Just vibes.
- Trevor: "So what are we hunting?" / Jeremiah: "No idea."
- They see a raccoon. Trevor gives Jeremiah the shot. Jeremiah uses the .50 cal — obliterates the front half of the raccoon. Nothing left to eat.
- Trevor takes over picking targets.

### Section 2: Ethical Hunting
- They get into a rhythm. Proper, ethical shots:
  - 1 elk
  - 2 ducks
  - 1 snake (Jeremiah hates snakes)
  - 1 moose (worthy of the .50 cal)
  - 3 deer
- Both horses are absolutely covered in meat and pelts. Look ridiculous.

### Section 3: Return
- They ride back to camp. First good thing in a while.
- The camp eats well tonight.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Light, comedic mission after heavy Chapter 3
- Jeremiah and Trevor bonding
- Camp morale boost from the food
