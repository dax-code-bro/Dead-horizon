# Chapter 5, Mission 7: Last Man Standing

## Chapter: 5
## Location: Waterfall Cave Camp

---

## Setup
- Morning. Normal day. People doing chores, talking, living.
- Then Marcus spots dust clouds on the horizon. A lot of them.
- He grabs binoculars. Military vehicles. Horses. Dozens of men in uniform.
- The old lawman — the marshal — finally found them. Clark gave him the location.
- They're coming fast.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Warning

**Marcus:** *(running into camp)* "THEY'RE COMING! MILITARY! EVERYONE MOVE!"

- Camp erupts into chaos. People grabbing belongings, children, weapons.
- Joseph tries to give orders but can't form sentences. Luisa guides him.
- Charles takes command: "Women and children first! Through the waterfall! NOW!"

### Section 2: Evacuation (Gameplay)
- **OBJECTIVE: Get everyone out through the back of the cave system**
- Player helps load wagons, gets people moving
- Emily grabs Josh. Marcus grabs their bags. They move.
- The cave has a back tunnel — narrow, dark, leads to a forest path on the other side of the mountain.
- The women, children, Joseph, and Luisa go through first.

### Section 3: The Rearguard
- Someone has to hold the cave entrance to buy time.
- The gang sets up at the waterfall — using the narrow canyon approach as a chokepoint.
- Jeremiah, Marcus, Charles, Phil, Clark, Earl, Hector, and Trevor take positions.
- **COMBAT: Waves of military pushing through the canyon.**
- The waterfall provides cover. The narrow entrance funnels them in.
- They hold. Wave after wave. But it's not stopping.

**Charles:** "There's too many! We need to go!"

**Jeremiah:** "Everyone fall back! Through the tunnel! NOW!"

### Section 4: Trevor's Choice
- Everyone starts retreating into the cave toward the back tunnel.
- Trevor is helping a wounded gang member — **Nate Briggs** — who took a bullet in the leg.
- Trevor is dragging him when more soldiers push through the waterfall.
- Trevor shoves Nate toward Hector: "Take him! GO!"
- Trevor picks up a rifle. Turns back toward the entrance.

**Jeremiah:** "Trevor! COME ON!"

**Trevor:** "Someone's gotta hold this entrance or they'll follow you through!"

**Marcus:** "We'll hold it together—"

**Trevor:** "No. You've got a boy who needs his father. Get out of here."

**Jeremiah:** "Trevor, don't do this."

**Trevor:** *(loading the rifle, hands steady for the first time)* "Jerry. I've spent my whole life cooking stew and patching people up. Let me do something that matters."

**Jeremiah:** "You've always mattered—"

**Trevor:** "Then let me matter one more time. GO."

*Trevor turns his back to them. Faces the cave entrance. Soldiers pouring through the waterfall. He raises the rifle.*

**Trevor:** *(to himself, quiet)* "Come on then."

*Jeremiah grabs Marcus. They run. Through the tunnel. Into the dark.*

### Section 5: The Tunnel
- The gang runs through the narrow back tunnel. Flashlights. Stumbling. Children crying.
- Behind them — gunfire. Trevor's rifle. Single shots. Steady. Controlled.
- Then faster. An exchange of fire.
- Then a burst.
- Then silence.

*Everyone stops. Listening. The waterfall echoes through the tunnel. But no more gunfire.*

*Jeremiah closes his eyes. Marcus puts his hand on Jeremiah's shoulder.*

**Marcus:** *(barely a whisper)* "...He's gone."

*They keep moving. Out the other side. Into the forest. Into the light.*

### Section 6: The Other Side
- The gang emerges from the tunnel into a dense forest.
- Everyone is here. Women, children, the wounded. All accounted for.
- Except Trevor.
- Emily is holding Josh. She sees Marcus and runs to him. Josh grabs Marcus's leg.
- Joseph is sitting on a rock. Luisa beside him. He doesn't fully understand what just happened.
- Charles is standing at the tunnel entrance. Staring into the dark. Waiting for footsteps that won't come.

**Phil:** *(sitting on the ground, head in his hands)* "...He didn't have to do that."

**Sadie:** *(kneeling next to Phil)* "He knew what he was doing."

**Phil:** "He was the cook. He was the damn cook."

**Clark:** *(leaning against a tree, arms crossed)* "He saved all of us."

*Marcus looks at Clark. Knows Clark is the reason Trevor is dead. Knows Clark gave up their location. His fists clench. Jeremiah sees it. Puts a hand on Marcus's arm. Not yet.*

---

**Night falls. They make camp in the forest. No fire — too dangerous.**

**No one eats. Trevor always made dinner.**

*Someone tries to heat water for coffee. Burns it. A small, stupid thing. But it breaks something. Because Trevor would've had coffee ready before anyone asked.*

### Chapter End
- Screen fades to black and white.
- **"CHAPTER 6"** appears.

### Mission Complete — End of Chapter 5

---

## Key Story Points
- **Trevor is dead** — held the cave entrance alone so the gang could escape
- The marshal found them because Clark gave up the location (Mission 5 confirmed this)
- Marcus knows Clark is responsible for Trevor's death but Jeremiah holds him back — not yet
- Trevor's last words: "Let me do something that matters" / "Let me matter one more time"
- The gang loses their caretaker — the man who cooked, healed, and held everything together
- Nobody eats that night. Trevor always made dinner.
- Someone burns the coffee. Because Trevor would have had it ready.
- Fifth camp lost. The waterfall cave is gone.
- Joseph barely understands what happened — brain damage worsening
- Charles stands at the tunnel entrance waiting for footsteps that won't come

## Trevor's Arc
- Chapter 1: Takes Jeremiah and Clark to get warmed up
- Chapter 2: Gifts Jeremiah a hunting rifle, fights a bear
- Chapter 3: Touches Lenny's shoulder, walks backward, couldn't stay
- Chapter 4: Tells Delia he watched Joseph execute an old woman
- Chapter 5: Senses trouble after the $900K score. Gives his life so everyone else can live.
- He spent the whole game taking care of people. In the end, he took care of them one last time.
