# Chapter 5, Mission 3: Payday

## Chapter: 5
## Location: Camp → Train Tracks → Train → Camp

---

## Setup
- Night falls. The gang gets ready immediately — no waiting.
- The landmine is already set on the tracks from the scouting mission.
- Everyone arms up. This is the biggest job they've ever attempted.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Ride to the Tracks
- The gang rides out at nightfall. Tense, quiet, focused.
- They reach the tracks and take position in the tree line.

### Section 2: The Mine
- The train approaches. Headlight cutting through the dark. The ground starts to rumble.
- The front car hits the landmine.
- **Massive explosion — the entire front car is blown off the rails.**
- The train grinds to a screeching halt. 12 cars behind it jackknifing.

### Section 3: The Assault
- Guards get out to check the explosion.
- **Phil opens up with a machine gun from the tree line.** Mows them down.
- Phil breaks position and rushes the train. Everyone follows.
- A passenger tries to fight back — Phil puts him down.
- Jeremiah drops everyone on the left side.
- The rest of the gang scatters across all 12 cars.

### Section 4: The Robbery (Gameplay)
- **OBJECTIVE: Rob the back car and middle car — cash locations**
- Player moves through the train car by car.
- Rob passengers for extra money along the way.
- Clear out remaining guards.
- Reach the safes in the middle and back cars.

### Section 5: The Haul
- They crack the safes.
- **Total haul: $900,000** — the biggest score in the game.
- They attach the safes to their horses and ride back to camp.

### Section 6: Camp Celebration
- The gang arrives at dawn dragging safes.
- Phil announces the total. Camp erupts.
- Each couple/character has a reaction moment:
  - Ray and Mae lose their minds
  - Nate: "Are we rich?"
  - Charles: "We're not rich. We're comfortable."
  - Phil: "Charles is wrong. For the first time in seventy-seven years, Charles is wrong."
  - Emily to Marcus — both thinking this is enough to leave and start over
  - Marcus picks up Josh: "Our future, kid."
  - Joseph and Luisa — quiet moment. He looks like the old Joseph.
  - Danny wants to buy Nell a dress with flowers on it
  - Earl and Faye nod at each other. That's their celebration.
  - Jeremiah and Trevor stand apart — both sensing this much money brings trouble
  - Clark watches from a tree, smiling differently than everyone else. Calculating.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- $900,000 — largest score in the game by far
- Joseph's plan actually worked perfectly — his lucid window produced results
- Marcus and Emily both thinking about leaving the gang and starting over
- Jeremiah and Trevor sense trouble coming
- Clark is calculating — the traitor sees opportunity
- The gang is at its highest point — which means the fall is coming
