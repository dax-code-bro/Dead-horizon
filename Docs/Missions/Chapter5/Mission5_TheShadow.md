# Chapter 5, Mission 5: The Shadow

## Chapter: 5
## Location: Camp → Las York → Alley Meeting → Camp

---

## Setup
- Clark volunteers to go into Las York for supplies. Nothing unusual.
- Marcus has been watching Clark since the ambush where Josh got grazed.
- Marcus tells Jeremiah he's going to follow Clark. Jeremiah tells him to be careful.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Clark Rides Out
- Clark saddles up Gunpowder and rides toward Las York. Casual. Whistling.
- A few minutes later, Marcus mounts Jim and follows at a distance.
- **Gameplay: Tailing mission.** Player controls Marcus (unique — first time playing as Marcus).
- Keep distance. Don't get spotted. Follow Clark through the city streets.

### Section 2: The Meeting
- Clark rides past the general store. Past the gunsmith. Doesn't stop at either.
- He ties Gunpowder at a hitching post near a run-down part of the city.
- Walks into an alley.
- Marcus dismounts and follows on foot. Crouching behind crates and barrels.
- Clark meets a man in a long coat. The man has a badge — but it's hidden under his coat. Marcus can barely see it.

### Section 3: The Conversation (Marcus overhears fragments)

**Clark:** "...They're at the waterfall. Behind it. Cave system."

**Badge Man:** "How many?"

**Clark:** "Thirty-something. Women and kids included."

**Badge Man:** "And Walkns?"

**Clark:** "Getting worse every day. He barely knows where he is half the time."

**Badge Man:** "What about Field?"

**Clark:** "He's distracted. Personal stuff. Now's the time."

**Badge Man:** "The marshal wants them all. No deals this time."

**Clark:** "I'll get them moving. Same as before. I pick the route, they follow. You set the welcome party."

*The badge man hands Clark an envelope. Clark pockets it.*

**Clark:** "Pleasure doing business."

### Section 4: Getting Out
- Clark turns to leave the alley. Marcus has to get out without being seen.
- **Stealth gameplay:** Player has to retreat through the back streets without Clark spotting them.
- If Clark sees Marcus — he plays it cool but the player knows Clark is now suspicious.
- If Marcus escapes clean — Clark has no idea he was followed.

### Section 5: Return to Camp
- Marcus rides back. Finds Jeremiah.
- Tells him everything he heard. Every word.

**Marcus:** "He's selling us out, Jerry. He told them where the cave is. He told them about Joseph. About you. He said he'd lead us into another trap — same as last time."

**Jeremiah:** "...You're sure."

**Marcus:** "I heard it. I saw the badge. I saw the money."

**Jeremiah:** "Did he see you?"

**Marcus:** "No."

**Jeremiah:** "Don't tell anyone else yet. Not Joseph — he can't handle this right now. Not Charles — he'll kill Clark on the spot."

**Marcus:** "So what do we do?"

**Jeremiah:** "We watch him. And when the time is right, we deal with it."

**Marcus:** "And if the time isn't right before the law shows up?"

**Jeremiah:** "Then we move first."

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- **First time player controls Marcus** — brief but significant. Foreshadows the protagonist switch.
- **Clark's betrayal confirmed** — Marcus has witnessed it firsthand
- Clark has been feeding the law the gang's location, Joseph's condition, and planning ambush routes
- Clark was paid — envelope of money
- The badge man mentions "the marshal" — the old lawman from the saloon
- Jeremiah and Marcus now share the secret — they can't tell Joseph or Charles yet
- Clark said "same as before" — confirming the Chapter 4 ambush where Josh got grazed was his doing
- If Marcus was spotted: Clark becomes more careful going forward, making him harder to catch

## Gameplay Innovation
- **First playable Marcus moment** — tailing/stealth mission as a different character
- **Eavesdropping mechanic** — overhear fragments of conversation
- **Stealth escape** — get out without being detected
- **Consequence system** — being spotted vs clean escape changes future interactions
