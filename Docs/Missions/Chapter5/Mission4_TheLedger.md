# Chapter 5, Mission 4: The Ledger

## Chapter: 5
## Location: Camp → City → Abandoned Farmhouse → Camp

---

## Setup
- Jeremiah still has the coded ledger from the burned-out office in Mission 1.
- He's been studying it at night when no one's watching.
- He's decoded enough to find a name — a Waterson accountant who might still be alive.
- He rides out alone again. Tells no one.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Ride (Solo)
- Jeremiah rides alone into the countryside.
- Same quiet, no-music atmosphere as Mission 1.
- He's carrying the ledger and the melted toy soldier.

### Section 2: Finding the Accountant
- Jeremiah tracks the accountant to an abandoned farmhouse outside town.
- The man is living off the grid — hiding since the Watersons were killed.
- His name is **Gerald Finch**. Thin, nervous, living in filth. He ran when he heard the Waterson family was massacred.

### Section 3: The Interrogation

**Jeremiah:** "You worked for the Watersons."

**Gerald:** "I was their bookkeeper. I just kept the numbers. I didn't—"

**Jeremiah:** "You kept the numbers for a hit on a house in North Carolina. A family. My family."

**Gerald:** *(going pale)* "...Oh God. You're Field."

**Jeremiah:** "Why did they burn my house."

**Gerald:** "It wasn't about you. It was never about you."

**Jeremiah:** "Then who."

**Gerald:** "Your father. He owed the Watersons money. A lot of it. From years back. Before he died. He borrowed from the old man — Harold Waterson — to fund something for his gang. He never paid it back."

**Jeremiah:** "So they killed my family for a debt my father owed."

**Gerald:** "The mother — Mrs. Waterson — she didn't forgive debts. She said the bloodline pays what the blood owes. Your father was dead so she went after the next generation."

**Jeremiah:** "My wife. My children. For money."

**Gerald:** "I'm sorry. I just kept the books. I didn't order it. I didn't—"

**Jeremiah:** "How much."

**Gerald:** "What?"

**Jeremiah:** "How much did my father owe."

**Gerald:** *(barely a whisper)* "...Twelve thousand dollars."

*Jeremiah stands there. His entire family — Sarah, Thomas, Lily — burned alive for twelve thousand dollars.*

### Section 4: PLAYER CHOICE

**[Kill Gerald]**
- Jeremiah: "You kept the books. You knew. And you did nothing."
- Gerald dies. *(Honor decreases)*

**[Spare Gerald]**
- Jeremiah: "Disappear. If I ever see you again, you won't see me first."
- Gerald runs. *(Honor increases)*

### Section 5: The Ride Back
- Jeremiah rides back in silence.
- He now knows the full truth: his father's debt got his family killed.
- The Watersons are dead. The accountant is dealt with. Dean Wyatt is dealt with.
- Everyone responsible is gone. But it doesn't feel like closure. It feels empty.
- He reaches camp. Walks past everyone. Goes to the back of the cave.
- He takes the melted toy soldier out. Looks at it. Then puts it in a small box and closes the lid.
- He doesn't take it out again for the rest of the chapter.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Revelations
- **Jeremiah's father borrowed $12,000 from Harold Waterson** — never paid it back
- **The debt was to fund something for the gang** — Joseph's gang benefited from this money
- **Mrs. Waterson's philosophy: "The bloodline pays what the blood owes"** — she targeted Jeremiah's family as punishment
- **Jeremiah's family died for $12,000 and a debt they didn't even know about**
- **Joseph may have known about the debt** — he and Jeremiah's father were in the gang together
- **Did Joseph know the Watersons killed Jeremiah's family?** — New question that haunts Jeremiah

## Jeremiah's Arc
- Mission 1: Learns family was murdered
- Mission 4: Learns WHY — his father's debt
- The Watersons are dead. No one left to confront.
- But did Joseph know? That question will simmer.
- The toy soldier goes in the box — Jeremiah is trying to move forward. Or shut down.
