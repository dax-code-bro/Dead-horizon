# Chapter 5, Mission 1: Ashes Don't Lie

## Chapter: 5
## Time Skip: Several months after Chapter 4
## Location: Waterfall Cave Camp → City → Dean Wyatt's Boarding House → Camp

---

## Chapter 5 Status (Several Months Later)
- Marcus and Emily nearly perfect — practically inseparable
- Josh healthy, scar on shoulder he shows off proudly
- Joseph having a bad week — hasn't left Luisa's side in days
- Gang is restless
- Jeremiah is quiet, withdrawn, sitting alone at camp edge with the melted toy soldier

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Stranger
- A retired fire investigator from North Carolina rides into camp looking for "a man named Field."
- Camp gets tense — hands on guns. Jeremiah stands up.
- The stranger isn't a threat. He's dying — cancer. Couldn't leave the world without telling the truth.
- He gives Jeremiah a folder: yellowed papers, photographs, a fire investigation report.

**Stranger:** "I investigated your house fire three years ago. They told you it was electrical. It wasn't."

**Stranger:** "Accelerant was found at three points of origin. Someone doused the walls in kerosene. That fire was set. Your family was murdered."

- The report was buried. Someone paid to make it disappear.
- He gives Jeremiah a name — **Dean Wyatt** — the last person seen near the house that night.
- The stranger rides off.

### Section 2: The Ride (Solo)
- Jeremiah rides alone to the city where Dean Wyatt was last seen.
- **No gang. No backup. This is personal.**
- No music. Just hooves and wind. Jeremiah doesn't talk. The ride is uncomfortably quiet.
- **Different mission type: Solo investigation. No shootouts.**

### Section 3: The Investigation
- **Bartender:** Knew Dean. "Haven't seen him in months. He used to drink here every night. Then he got money. A lot of it. And disappeared."
- **Dean's old apartment:** Jeremiah breaks in. Trashed. Finds letters — payments from someone. Name scratched out but partial address visible.
- **Burned-out office at the address:** Finds a ledger with coded entries. Takes it.
- No combat. Just Jeremiah walking through a dead man's life. His anger building — doors opened harder, conversations shorter, patience disappearing.

### Section 4: Finding Dean Wyatt
- Rundown boarding house at the edge of town. Room 6.
- Jeremiah kicks the door in.
- Dean is pathetic — thin, drunk, shaking. Sees Jeremiah and knows immediately.

**Dean:** "Oh God. Oh God no."

**Jeremiah:** "You know who I am."

**Dean:** "Please. I didn't — I was paid. I was just paid to—"

**Jeremiah:** "To what."

**Dean:** "To start the fire. But I didn't know there were people inside. They told me the house was empty. I swear to God—"

**Jeremiah:** "My wife was in that house. My son. My daughter. Thomas was seven. Lily was four."

**Dean:** *(crying)* "I didn't know. Please. They paid me five thousand dollars and said it was empty."

**Jeremiah:** "Who paid you."

**Dean:** "I can't — they'll kill me."

*Jeremiah grabs him by the throat. Pins him to the wall. Cattleman pressed under his chin.*

**Jeremiah:** "You're worried about them killing you?"

**Dean:** *(choking)* "It was... it was a man named... he worked for a company... Waterson. Waterson's people."

*Jeremiah goes still.*

### Section 5: PLAYER CHOICE

**[Kill Dean]**
- Jeremiah pulls the trigger. Dean drops.
- Jeremiah stands over him. No quip. No joke. Just silence.
- Holsters the Cattleman. Walks out.
- *(Honor decreases)*

**[Spare Dean]**
- Jeremiah lets go. Dean collapses gasping.
- Jeremiah: "You're going to live with what you did. Every single day. That's worse than what I'd do to you."
- Walks out. Dean sobbing on the floor.
- *(Honor increases)*

### Section 6: Return to Camp
- Jeremiah rides back. Doesn't tell anyone what he found.
- Walks past Marcus and Emily laughing with Josh.
- Walks past Charles smoking by the fire.
- Walks past Joseph mumbling in his tent.
- Goes to the back of the cave. Sits down. Opens the folder. Photographs of his burned house.
- Pulls out the melted toy soldier.
- **For the first time in the game, Jeremiah cries.** Silent tears in the dark while the waterfall roars outside and his family lives around him.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Revelations
- **Jeremiah's family was murdered — not an accident**
- **The Watersons ordered the hit** — kerosene at three points of origin
- **The fire report was buried — someone paid to cover it up**
- **Dean Wyatt was paid $5,000 to set the fire** — told the house was empty
- **Joseph killed the Watersons in Chapter 4 without knowing they murdered Jeremiah's family**
- **Jeremiah will never get to ask the Watersons why**

## Jeremiah's Personal Story Arc
- Chapter 1-4: The observer, the glue, everyone else's support
- Chapter 5: HIS story begins — his family was murdered, and the people responsible are already dead
- Mystery: Why did the Watersons target his family? Was it about the gang? About Jeremiah's father? Something else?
- The coded ledger may hold more answers

## New Mission Types Introduced
- **Solo investigation** — no gang, no backup
- **NPC interrogation** — talking to bartenders, searching apartments
- **Player moral choice** — kill or spare (affects hidden honor)
- **Quiet, tension-based gameplay** — no music, no combat, just atmosphere

## New Characters
- **The Stranger** — Retired fire investigator from North Carolina. Dying of cancer. Delivered the truth.
- **Dean Wyatt** — The arsonist. Paid $5,000 by Waterson's people. Pathetic, drunk, broken by guilt. Status depends on player choice (alive or dead).
