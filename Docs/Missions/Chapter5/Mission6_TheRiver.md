# Chapter 5, Mission 6: The River

## Chapter: 5
## Location: Camp → River

---

## Setup
- A quiet morning. No jobs planned. No drama.
- Josh runs up to Jeremiah and asks him to go fishing.
- Emily nods from across camp. Marcus is busy helping Charles with something.
- Just Jeremiah and Josh. A man and a boy at a river.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: The Walk
- They walk to the river. Not far from camp. Josh is carrying a stick he calls a fishing rod.
- Jeremiah has an actual rod.
- Josh is talking nonstop — about horses, about his scar, about how Marcus let him hold his Beretta (unloaded).

**Josh:** "Daddy said when I'm older he'll teach me to shoot."

**Jeremiah:** "Did he."

**Josh:** "He said you'll probably teach me better though."

**Jeremiah:** *(small laugh)* "Your dad's a fine shot."

**Josh:** "But you're a Marine."

**Jeremiah:** "I was a Marine. Long time ago."

**Josh:** "Once a Marine, always a Marine. That's what daddy says."

### Section 2: Fishing
- They sit by the river. Josh's stick isn't catching anything. Jeremiah helps him bait a real hook.
- Quiet for a while. Just the river.

**Josh:** "Uncle Jerry?"

**Jeremiah:** "Yeah."

**Josh:** "Did you have kids?"

*Jeremiah goes still.*

**Jeremiah:** "...Yeah. I did."

**Josh:** "What were their names?"

**Jeremiah:** "Thomas and Lily."

**Josh:** "Where are they?"

**Jeremiah:** "They're... they're not here anymore, Josh."

**Josh:** "Did they go away?"

**Jeremiah:** "Something like that."

**Josh:** "Like how daddy went away?"

**Jeremiah:** "No. Different. They... they went to a place where you can't come back from."

*Josh is quiet for a moment. Processing.*

**Josh:** "Like Lenny?"

*Jeremiah looks at him. Five years old and he already understands death.*

**Jeremiah:** "...Yeah. Like Lenny."

**Josh:** "Do you miss them?"

**Jeremiah:** "Every day."

**Josh:** "I'm sorry, Uncle Jerry."

**Jeremiah:** "It's okay, kid."

**Josh:** "Is that why you take care of me? Because you miss them?"

*Jeremiah can't speak for a second.*

**Jeremiah:** "...I take care of you because you're worth taking care of."

**Josh:** "Uncle Jerry?"

**Jeremiah:** "Yeah."

**Josh:** "If something happens to daddy, will you take care of me and mama?"

**Jeremiah:** "Nothing's gonna happen to your daddy."

**Josh:** "But if it does."

**Jeremiah:** "...Yeah, kid. I will. Always."

**Josh:** "Promise?"

**Jeremiah:** "Promise."

*Josh's line tugs. He jumps up.*

**Josh:** "I GOT ONE! UNCLE JERRY I GOT ONE!"

*Jeremiah helps him reel it in. It's tiny. Barely a fish.*

**Jeremiah:** "Well look at that."

**Josh:** "It's huge!"

**Jeremiah:** "It's... it's something."

**Josh:** "I'm gonna show daddy!"

**Jeremiah:** "He'll be real proud."

*Josh holds the fish up, beaming. Jeremiah watches him. This boy who isn't his. But could've been. In another life.*

### Section 3: Walking Back
- They walk back to camp. Josh carrying the fish like a trophy.
- Marcus sees them coming. Josh runs ahead.

**Josh:** "DADDY LOOK! I CAUGHT A FISH!"

**Marcus:** *(kneeling)* "That's... wow. That's a fish alright."

**Josh:** "Uncle Jerry helped me."

*Marcus looks at Jeremiah over Josh's head. Nods. A silent thank you.*

*Jeremiah nods back. Walks to his spot. Sits down. Doesn't reach for the toy soldier. For the first time, he doesn't need to. He just watched a boy catch a fish and that was enough.*

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Josh asks about Thomas and Lily — first time a child has made Jeremiah talk about them
- Josh already understands death at age 5 — "Like Lenny?"
- Josh asks Jeremiah to promise to take care of him if something happens to Marcus — devastating foreshadowing
- Jeremiah promises — a promise he'll have to keep after his own death (through whatever he sets in motion)
- No combat. No drama. Just a man and a boy at a river.
- Jeremiah doesn't reach for the toy soldier — for the first time, the present is enough
- The player is watching Jeremiah say goodbye to this kid without knowing it
