# Chapter 5, Mission 2: Boom Goes the Wagon

## Chapter: 5
## Location: Waterfall Cave Camp → Forest Clearing near Train Tracks

---

## Setup
- Joseph has a lucid day. He's sharp, focused — the old Joseph.
- He calls everyone to his tent. Lays out his biggest plan yet: robbing a government train.
- The whole gang rides out to scout and test their plan.

## Mission Start
- Player walks to Joseph's tent (icon: "JW").
- Joseph is inside with a map covered in writing and markings.
- He explains the plan: the train carries cash and weapons through the mountains. They're going to stop it with a landmine.

---

## Gameplay Flow

### Section 1: Ride Out
- The entire gang — all remaining men — saddle up and ride out together.
- First time the whole group has ridden together since the Washington assault.
- Energy is high. People are talking, joking. Feels different.

### Section 2: Building the Landmine
- Phil and Charles designed a makeshift landmine using dynamite.
- The gang sets it up on a road near the train tracks as a test.
- Phil rigs the charge. Charles supervises. Everyone else takes position in the tree line.

### Section 3: The Test
- They wait for a wagon to cross.
- A wagon with three passengers approaches.
- It hits the mine.
- **The entire front of the wagon launches meters into the air. Horses catch fire immediately.**
- The three passengers are killed instantly.
- The gang erupts — jumping up, screaming, cheering, proud of their invention.
- **Nobody cares about the casualties. Not even Jeremiah.** He's done worse.

### Section 4: The Plan
- Joseph pulls out the map with the full plan written on it.
- He walks everyone through the train route, timing, positions, and roles.
- Everyone confirms — this is the right plan.
- They ride back to camp to prepare.

### Mission Complete

---

## Key Story Points
- Phil and Charles designed the landmine together — demolition meets brains
- The test kills three innocent people and nobody blinks
- Shows the gang for what they are — outlaws, not heroes
- Joseph is lucid and in command — one of his last truly sharp moments
- Sets up Mission 3: the actual train heist
