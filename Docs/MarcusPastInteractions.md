# Dead Horizon — Marcus's Past & Relationship Interactions

---

## IMPORTANT: Marcus and Emily's relationship is UNBREAKABLE.
All past encounters happened BEFORE Marcus and Emily were together. These interactions exist to show the strength of their bond, not to threaten it. By the epilogue, this is history they joke about. Their relationship is the one constant in a world where everything else falls apart.

---

## Marcus's Past (Before Emily)
During his year away from the gang and early gang days, Marcus was with:
- **Rosa Salinas** — one time, before Emily
- **Mae Colburn** — one time, before Mae and Ray, before Marcus and Emily
- **Delia Harmon** — one time, both lonely, meant nothing

---

## Women Who Approach Marcus (2019 — Gang Days)

### Rosa Tries (Chapter 2)
- Approaches Marcus at the stables. "I'm good with my hands."
- Marcus shuts her down: "She yells at me because she cares enough to yell."
- Walks away.

### Delia's Attempt (Chapter 3)
- Brings Marcus a drink at the campfire. Sits too close. "Remember that night?"
- Marcus: "I have a son. And Emily. This conversation is over."
- Delia: "She's a lucky woman." / Marcus: "No. I'm a lucky man."

### Mae Tests the Water (Chapter 2)
- "Ray's asleep. Maybe I don't want Ray right now."
- Marcus: "I've got a kid. I've got Emily. Whatever we had is dead. Let it stay dead."

### Nell (Innocent, Chapter 3)
- Young, nervous, bumps into Marcus. Offers to help with "anything."
- Marcus nods toward Emily's tent: "I've got everything I need. Go find Danny."

### Bess (Chapter 4)
- Gives Marcus a hand-stitched holster. "I'd do anything for you, Marcus."
- Marcus: "I'm going to pay you for the holster. But that's all it is. I've got Emily."
- Makes sure Emily sees him pay Bess the next day.

## Marcus Apologizes to Emily

### After Rosa (Chapter 2)
- Marcus tells Emily voluntarily that Rosa approached him and he shut it down.
- Emily: "If Rosa comes near you again—" / Marcus: "I'll send her to you."
- Emily: "Don't. I'll handle it myself."

### After Mae Revelation (Chapter 3)
- Marcus tells Emily about Rosa, Mae, and Delia — all of them at once. Charles told him to.
- Emily: "I slept with more men than that to feed your son. And I didn't have a choice. You did."
- Marcus: "I'm not comparing. I'm apologizing. For being a stupid kid who didn't know what he had."
- Emily: "Do you know what you have now?" / Marcus: "Everything."

### The Big Apology (Chapter 4)
- Marcus apologizes for all of it — running, leaving her at sixteen, everything she had to do.
- "You were sixteen years old and I left you. And you became the strongest person I've ever met because you had no choice."
- "I'm never going to be good enough to deserve you. But I'm going to spend every day trying."
- Emily: "I've been waiting three years to hear that. It had to come from you. Not because I asked. Because you meant it."
- Emily: "We're not perfect. We're never going to be. But we're here. And that's enough."

## Epilogue Callbacks (2026)

### Rosa and Emily in Las York
- Rosa brings up the past. Emily: "I've always known. It was a long time ago."
- No drama. Two grown women. History acknowledged and dismissed.

### Delia and Marcus on the Porch
- Delia asks if Emily knows. Marcus: "I'll talk to her."
- Both stand up too fast when Emily calls for dinner. Guilty body language.

### Emily Addresses It (Epilogue)
- "Nobody told me. I have eyes. You two stood up like the house was on fire."
- "I slept with half the gang to feed our son. You think I'm going to lose sleep over that?"
- Marcus reveals Mae too. Emily: "MAE? Ray's Mae?"
- Emily: "You're buying me another daisy tomorrow." / Marcus: "Done." / Emily: "Two daisies."

Marcus and Emily's relationship is the strongest thing in the game. It survived:
- Marcus running away for a year
- Emily's survival relationships with gang members
- Marcus's denial of Josh

Nothing breaks them. Nothing.
