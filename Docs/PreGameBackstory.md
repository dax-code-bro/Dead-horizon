# Dead Horizon — Pre-Game Backstory: The Train Heist

---

## The Incident
**Location:** Redwood / Sandy Dunes border region
**What happened:** A train heist planned by Joseph went catastrophically wrong.
**Result:** Five gang members killed. Marcus shot and nearly died. The gang destroyed, broken, fleeing north. The military locked down both regions permanently — kill on sight order for anyone associated with the gang.

**This is why Chapter 1 begins in the Mount Everest Range.** The gang was running from this disaster.

---

## The Five Who Died

### 1. Davis Malloy
- Attacked by a guard on top of the train.
- The guard pushed him off while the train was crossing a mountain pass.
- Massive drop. Gone instantly. Body never recovered.

### 2. Bond Malloy (Davis's brother)
- Shot in the arm during the robbery.
- The wound went untreated. Infection set in.
- Held on for days as the gang fled north.
- **Died just days before Chapter 1 begins.**
- The gang buried him in the snow somewhere in the Mount Everest Range.

### 3. Maria Sinclair
- Joseph's former lover.
- Was part of the robbery crew.
- A guard got his hands around her throat during the chaos.
- Choked to death on the train.
- Joseph either watched it happen or found out after.

### 4. Finn Butler
- Shot five times in the chest during the robbery.
- Fell off the train.
- Still alive somehow when the patrol found him on the ground.
- The patrol mercy-killed him.

### 5. Jamie
- Survived the heist but was injured.
- During the gang's escape north, got separated in the same blizzard that opens Chapter 1.
- Lost in the storm. Mauled to death by wolves.
- Would have died from his heist injuries anyway.
- Never found. Never seen again.
- **The player walks through Jamie's death storm in the opening mission without knowing it.**

---

## Marcus During the Heist
- Marcus was on the train.
- Got shot during the robbery.
- Fell off the back of the train.
- Adrenaline carried him into the nearby forest.
- Collapsed in a bush. Fell unconscious.
- Someone from the gang found him and brought him back.
- He was out for most of the robbery — missed the worst of it.

---

## Why Jeremiah Can't Go to Redwood or Sandy Dunes
- The military set up a permanent kill order on anyone from the gang in those two regions.
- No arrest. No bounty office. Helicopters and military vehicles come immediately.
- If the player tries to enter: instant "WANTED DEAD OR ALIVE" and overwhelming military force.
- This persists throughout Jeremiah's entire story (2019).

## Why Marcus Can Go There in 2026
- Seven years have passed.
- Marcus was unconscious for most of the robbery — his face isn't as well known.
- Different look, older, rancher now. Not recognizable.
- Military presence has relaxed over time.

## Dialogue References Throughout the Game

### Chapter 1 — Around the Campfire (After Welcome Home)

*Optional camp interaction. Joseph is sitting by the fire. Staring at it. Jeremiah sits down.*

**Jeremiah:** "How are you holding up?"

**Joseph:** "Five people, Jerry. Five of my people."

**Jeremiah:** "It wasn't your fault."

**Joseph:** "It was my plan. My call. My train."

**Jeremiah:** "You couldn't have known—"

**Joseph:** "Davis. Bond. Maria. Finn. Jamie. Say their names."

**Jeremiah:** "...Davis. Bond. Maria. Finn. Jamie."

**Joseph:** "I say them every night. Before I sleep. So I don't forget what my plans cost."

*Silence. The fire crackles.*

**Jeremiah:** "Bond held on as long as he could."

**Joseph:** "He held on for his brother. And his brother was already dead. He just didn't know it yet."

**Jeremiah:** "Joseph—"

**Joseph:** "Don't tell me it gets easier. It doesn't."

---

### Chapter 2 — Marcus and Jeremiah (During a ride)

*Marcus and Jeremiah are riding together. Marcus touches his side — where the bullet hit him during the train heist.*

**Jeremiah:** "Still hurts?"

**Marcus:** "Sometimes. When it's cold."

**Jeremiah:** "You're lucky to be alive."

**Marcus:** "I know. I remember falling. The ground coming up fast. Then trees. Then nothing."

**Jeremiah:** "You crawled into a bush and passed out."

**Marcus:** "That's what they tell me. I don't remember anything after the fall."

**Jeremiah:** "Phil found you. Followed the blood trail."

**Marcus:** "Phil?"

**Jeremiah:** "Carried you on his back for two miles. Wouldn't put you down. Kept saying 'he's breathing, he's still breathing.'"

**Marcus:** "...He never told me that."

**Jeremiah:** "He wouldn't. That's Phil."

**Marcus:** "I owe him my life."

**Jeremiah:** "You owe a lot of people your life, Marcus. Start by living it."

---

### Chapter 3 — Charles and Jeremiah

*Charles is sitting in his chair. Jeremiah brings him coffee.*

**Charles:** "You know what I think about sometimes?"

**Jeremiah:** "What."

**Charles:** "The Malloy brothers. Davis and Bond."

**Jeremiah:** "What about them?"

**Charles:** "Bond knew his brother was dead. Somewhere inside, he knew. Davis went off that cliff and Bond felt it. But he kept fighting. Kept running. Held on for three more days with a bullet in his arm because he thought maybe — maybe — he was wrong."

**Jeremiah:** "He wasn't wrong because he hoped."

**Charles:** "No. He was wrong because hoping doesn't change what's real. Davis was dead the second he went over that edge. And Bond died three days later chasing a ghost."

**Jeremiah:** "Why are you telling me this?"

**Charles:** "Because one day you're going to lose someone and hope is going to tell you to hold on. And I need you to know the difference between holding on and letting go."

---

### Chapter 4 — Joseph Mentions Maria

*Joseph is having a lucid moment. He and Jeremiah are alone.*

**Joseph:** "Jerry. Did I ever tell you about Maria?"

**Jeremiah:** "Maria Sinclair?"

**Joseph:** "She was... before Luisa. Before everything. She was the first person who ever looked at me like I wasn't just some outlaw kid with blood on his hands."

**Jeremiah:** "I remember her. She was kind."

**Joseph:** "She was too kind for this life. I told her that. She said she'd rather be kind in a cruel world than cruel in a kind one."

**Jeremiah:** "That sounds like her."

**Joseph:** "She died on that train. Some guard put his hands around her throat and... and I couldn't get to her. I was ten feet away and I couldn't get to her."

**Jeremiah:** "Joseph—"

**Joseph:** "Luisa knows about Maria. She's never said a word about it. Not once. That woman has more grace than I deserve."

**Jeremiah:** "She loves you."

**Joseph:** "I know. That's what scares me. Because everyone who loves me ends up paying for it."

---

### Chapter 5 — Phil and Marcus (After the $900K score)

*Phil and Marcus are sitting on crates. Celebrating.*

**Marcus:** "Phil."

**Phil:** "Yeah?"

**Marcus:** "Jerry told me something. About the train heist. Before everything."

**Phil:** "What about it."

**Marcus:** "He said you carried me. Two miles. On your back."

*Phil goes quiet. Drinks.*

**Marcus:** "You never told me."

**Phil:** "Wasn't worth telling."

**Marcus:** "You saved my life."

**Phil:** "You were breathing. I just moved you. That's not saving a life. That's carrying a body that happened to be alive."

**Marcus:** "Phil."

**Phil:** "What."

**Marcus:** "Thank you."

**Phil:** *(long pause)* "...You were heavy as hell, by the way. I thought about putting you down twice."

**Marcus:** *(laughing)* "But you didn't."

**Phil:** "No. I didn't."

### Chapter 7 — Jeremiah Alone (On the roof, dead horizon moment)

*Jeremiah is standing on the roof. Looking south. Toward the Redwood. Toward the Sandy Dunes. The regions he can't go back to.*

**Jeremiah:** *(to himself)* "Jamie's still out there somewhere. In the snow. In the wolves. Five people died so we could stand here. And what did we do with it?"

*He looks at the camp below him. The divided gang. Joseph mumbling. Clark whispering.*

**Jeremiah:** "...Not enough."

### Epilogue — Marcus Tells Josh (At the ranch, 2026)

*Marcus and Josh are on the porch. Sunset.*

**Josh:** "Dad. Can I ask you something?"

**Marcus:** "Sure."

**Josh:** "How'd you get the scar on your side? The one you don't talk about."

*Marcus looks at him. Josh is twelve. Old enough.*

**Marcus:** "I was shot. During a train robbery."

**Josh:** "You robbed a train?"

**Marcus:** "I was part of a gang, Josh. A long time ago. Before you were born. Before any of this."

**Josh:** "Uncle Jerry's gang?"

**Marcus:** "Yeah. Uncle Jerry's gang."

**Josh:** "What happened?"

**Marcus:** "It went wrong. Five people died. I got shot and fell off the train. A man named Phil carried me two miles on his back so I wouldn't die in the dirt."

**Josh:** "Phil? The guy who was just here?"

**Marcus:** "The same Phil."

**Josh:** "...Whoa."

**Marcus:** "Five people died that day, Josh. Good people. People with names and families and lives. And they died because someone thought the money on that train was worth more than their lives."

**Josh:** "Was it?"

**Marcus:** "No. It never is."

**Josh:** "Then why'd you do it?"

**Marcus:** "Because I didn't know any better. Because I followed a man I loved into something I shouldn't have. And because I was too young and too stupid to say no."

**Josh:** "...Do you miss it? That life?"

**Marcus:** *(looking at the ranch, the cows, the sunset, his son)* "Not for one second."

- Playing as young Jeremiah or young Marcus during the heist
- The player would experience the deaths of Davis, Bond, Maria, Finn, and Jamie firsthand
- Seeing Phil carry Marcus through the forest
- The escape north to the mountains
- The blizzard that kills Jamie — the same blizzard that opens DH1
- DH3 ends where DH1 begins
