# Dead Horizon — Clark's Manipulation Interactions

---

## Clark Brings Joseph Coffee
- Walks in with coffee. "Nobody appreciates what you do."
- Plants the idea that Jeremiah, Marcus, and Charles are all pulling away.
- "When's the last time any of them came in here and just asked how you were doing?"

## Clark Talks About Jeremiah
- Tells Joseph that Jerry's been acting different — going off alone, not telling anyone where.
- "Him and Marcus whispering all the time. You notice that?"
- "If he's got something to say, he should say it to your face."

## Clark Feeds Paranoia
- Points out Marcus and Jeremiah talking by the horses.
- "You ever wonder if they talk about you?"
- "Probably nothing. Forget I said anything." — But Joseph can't forget. His damaged brain holds onto it.

## Clark the Caretaker
- Joseph having a bad episode. Clark guides him to his tent. Brings him water.
- "You rest. I'll handle things out there."
- Joseph: "You're a good kid, Clark." / Clark: "I learned from the best."
- Camp sees Clark stepping up. They don't see the smile when his back is turned.

## Clark Plants the Seed
- Late night chess. Clark talks about loyalty.
- "Some of them are already planning what happens when you're gone."
- "Who's been spending all their time with Marcus? Who stopped coming to you for advice?"
- Joseph stares at the bishop on the board. Jeremiah's piece. His hands are shaking.

## Clark Comforts Luisa
- Sits with Luisa outside Joseph's tent.
- "If you ever need anything — anything at all — you come to me."
- Luisa thinks Clark is a good man. She shouldn't.
- Clark is building trust with the one person Joseph cares about most — the Queen on the chess board.

---

## Clark's Strategy
1. Position himself as Joseph's only loyal supporter
2. Poison Joseph against Jeremiah and Marcus
3. Build trust with Luisa — control the Queen
4. Appear helpful to the camp — "Clark stepped up when nobody else did"
5. Feed information to the law — keep the gang running and vulnerable
6. Wait for Joseph's brain damage to do the rest
