# Dead Horizon — McAllen Tent Interactions

---

## System
- If you wait until Marcus closes his tent flaps and everyone is confirmed asleep, you can sneak in by crouching.
- If you accidentally talk to someone or stand up, you wake them.
- Each sleeping interaction is randomly selected when you enter.
- You can greet or antagonize whoever wakes up.
- Different combinations of people waking create different dialogue.

---

## Sleeping Interactions (What You Can Witness)

1. **Marcus holding Emily** — arm wrapped around her
2. **Emily's head on Marcus's chest** — rising with his breathing
3. **Josh curled between them** — hands touching both parents
4. **Marcus's hand on Josh's back** — protective
5. **Josh holding Marcus's shirt** — fist clenched on fabric
6. **Emily's hand on Marcus's arm** — checking he's still there
7. **Josh mumbling about horses** — full dream conversations about Cerberus

---

## Single Wake-Ups

### Interaction 1: Marcus Holding Emily

**Greet Marcus:**
- Marcus wakes, pulls arm back fast. "I wasn't— it's cold in here. Body heat."

**Antagonize Marcus:**
- "Body heat, huh?" / "Shut up, Jerry." / "Big tough Marcus—" / "I will shoot you in your sleep tonight."

**Greet Emily:**
- Emily wakes smiling, then sees Jeremiah. "How long have you been standing there?" / "Go."

**Antagonize Emily:**
- "He's got a good grip on you." / "Yeah he does. And unlike you, he's welcome here."

### Interaction 2: Emily's Head on Marcus's Chest

**Greet Marcus:**
- Opens one eye. Sees Emily on his chest. "Don't move her. She hasn't slept this good in weeks. Get out. Quietly."

**Antagonize Marcus:**
- "You make a good pillow." / "Jerry, I have a gun under this blanket."

**Greet Emily:**
- Lifts head, realizes, lays back down. "Don't have to tell me twice."

**Antagonize Emily:**
- "His heartbeat that relaxing?" / "More relaxing than your voice. Go away."

### Interaction 3: Josh Curled Between Them

**Greet Marcus:**
- Looks at Josh between them. "We're fine. Better than fine."

**Antagonize Marcus:**
- "Sandwiched by your own family." / "If you wake him up, Emily will kill both of us."

**Greet Emily:**
- "He does this every night. Holds onto both of us. Like he's afraid we'll disappear."

**Antagonize Emily:**
- "The kid's got you both pinned down." / "He's five. He can pin me down all he wants."

**Greet Josh:**
- Looks at both parents. "They're both here, Uncle Jerry." / "Every night."

**Antagonize Josh:**
- "You sleep like a starfish." / "That's so daddy and mama can't leave." / "I'm making sure."

### Interaction 4: Marcus's Hand on Josh's Back

**Greet Marcus:**
- Hand stays on Josh. "What." / "Nothing important." / "Then why'd you wake me up."

**Antagonize Marcus:**
- "Protective father. Never thought I'd see the day." / "Things change."

**Greet Emily:**
- "He started doing that a few nights ago. Every night now."

**Antagonize Emily:**
- "He's got dad hands now." / "He's always had them. He just didn't know how to use them."

### Interaction 5: Josh Holding Marcus's Shirt

**Greet Marcus:**
- "He does this every night. Grabs on and won't let go."

**Antagonize Marcus:**
- "You couldn't leave now even if you wanted to." / "Good. Then we don't have a problem."

**Greet Josh:**
- "Feel how strong I am." / "Daddy's not going anywhere." / "I'm making sure."

**Antagonize Josh:**
- "You're gonna rip his shirt." / "It's my shirt now." / "Since I grabbed it."

### Interaction 6: Emily's Hand on Marcus's Arm

**Greet Marcus:**
- "She does this. Every night. Like she's checking." / "That I'm still here."

**Antagonize Marcus:**
- "She's got a tracker on you." / "It's her hand, not a tracker." / "Same thing with Emily."

**Greet Emily:**
- Doesn't pull hand away. "You're in my tent again." / "This is becoming a problem."

**Antagonize Emily:**
- "Can't let go even in your sleep?" / "Can't mind your own business even in the middle of the night?"

### Interaction 7: Josh Mumbling About Horses

**Greet Josh:**
- "I was riding Cerberus..." / "He could fly." / "In my dream he can."

**Antagonize Josh:**
- "He's my horse." / "Not in my dreams."

**Greet Marcus (already awake listening):**
- Marcus is smiling. "He's been going for ten minutes. Earlier he was telling Cerberus to jump over a mountain."

**Antagonize Marcus:**
- "Your son is having a full conversation with my horse." / "At least someone in this tent is having a good night."

## Combination Wake-Ups

### Marcus + Josh (Greet)
- Marcus: "Jerry..." / Josh: "Is it morning?" / Marcus: "No. Uncle Jerry is just lost." / Josh: "Again?"

### Marcus + Josh (Antagonize)
- "Father-son sleepover. Cute." / Marcus: "Josh, cover your ears." / "Because daddy's about to say words mama doesn't like."

### Emily + Josh (Greet)
- Josh: "Mama, Uncle Jerry's here." / Emily: "I know, baby. He's leaving." / Josh: "Can he stay?" / Emily: "No. He also invited Cerberus. Goodnight."

### Emily + Josh (Antagonize)
- Josh: "Yeah! Leave!" / Emily: "Josh, go back to sleep." / Josh: "But I'm helping you."

### Marcus + Emily (Greet)
- Marcus reaches for Beretta. Both: "We're fine. Without you in this tent." / "What she said."

### Marcus + Emily (Antagonize)
- "You two were looking real cozy." / Marcus: "I'm counting to three." / Emily: "I'm counting to two." / Emily: "One." / Jeremiah leaves fast.

### All Three (Greet)
- Josh: "Uncle Jerry, do you not have a bed?" / Marcus: "Great question, son."

### All Three (Antagonize)
- Josh starts crying about his Cerberus dream. Emily throws a boot. Marcus: "Good arm." / Emily: "I've been saving that."

## Caught Standing Up (Not Crouching)
- Marcus bolts awake with Beretta aimed. Emily screams. Josh cries.
- Marcus physically pushes Jeremiah out of the tent.
- "If you ever do that again, I won't check who it is first."
- From inside: Josh: "Was it the bad guys?" / Marcus: "No, baby. Just Uncle Jerry being an idiot."
