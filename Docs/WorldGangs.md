# Dead Horizon — Gangs of the World

---

## Gang System
- Every non-story gang has **random interactions** in their territory (infinite, repeatable)
- Gangs have **hideouts** and/or a **main base**
- **Clear all hideouts + main base = gang permanently eliminated from the region**
- Random interactions stop once the gang is eliminated
- The region becomes safer — NPCs comment on it
- **Intervening in gang encounters = honor up**
- **Watching/ignoring = honor down**

---

## Story Gangs

### Walkns Gang (Player's Gang)
- **Leader:** Joseph Walkns
- **Region:** Various (moves throughout the story)
- **Status:** Disbanded during the story. Members scattered by 2026.

### Washington Gang
- **Leader:** Michael Washington
- **Region:** Great Watson Plains
- **Structure:** 10 sub-gangs. No main base. 3 hideouts.
- **Hideout 1:** Cleared in Chapter 1 (Marcus rescue mission — ~100 members)
- **Hideout 2-3:** Remaining. Can be cleared for extra loot/XP.
- **Elimination:** Automatic after Chapter 4 Mission 2 "Goodbye Old Friend" — Michael Washington executed. Killing the leader ends all sub-gangs. They had no structure without him.
- **Trait:** Quantity over quality. Washington would throw 600 men into a suicide mission for a score. He didn't care about his people.

## World Gangs

### The Harker Clan
- **Region:** Redwood
- **Vibe:** Modern rednecks with the mind of the Murfree Brood but not the looks. Flannel shirts, jeans, boots, trucker caps. They look normal until they put a shotgun in your face. Psychotic, no empathy, kill for fun, territorial.
- **Weapons:** Shotguns exclusively
- **Signature:** Red pickup trucks. You see a red truck in the Redwoods, you know who's coming.
- **Structure:** No known leader. No hideouts. 1 main base only.
- **Main Base:** Treehouse compound built into the redwood canopy. Platforms, walkways, shacks in the trees. Red trucks parked on the forest floor below. **125 members.**
- **Elimination:** Clear the main base. One and done. They're gone from the Redwoods forever.

**Random Interactions (Infinite):**
1. **Hostage on the Road** — Two red trucks blocking road. Harkers with hostage on knees.
2. **The Hanging Tree** — Three bodies hanging from a redwood. Two Harkers eating sandwiches beneath.
3. **Red Truck Chase** — Red pickup screams out of tree line. Shotguns from the truck bed.
4. **Campfire Ambush** — Inviting campfire. Abandoned looking. It's bait. Harkers emerge from all sides.
5. **The Crying Woman** — Woman crying on roadside waves you down. She's one of them. Harkers from the tree line.
6. **The Roadblock** — Fallen tree across road. Get off horse to move it = ambush. Turn around = red truck blocks escape.
7. **The Dinner Party** — Table set for dinner in a clearing. Dead man propped up as a guest. Harkers invite you to sit. Fight either way.
8. **The Warning Post** — Wooden post with severed animal head. "TURN BACK" written in blood. Nothing happens. Just a warning.
9. **The Dog Pack** — Three Harkers with attack dogs. Dogs released first, then gunfire. Dogs are fast.
10. **The Moonshine Run** — Red truck hauling moonshine jugs. Chase and shoot the moonshine = explosion chain reaction.
11. **Night Patrol** — Nighttime only. Two Harkers with flashlights patrolling. Spot you = call backup truck within a minute. Stay hidden = follow them to base.
12. **The Porch Sitter** — Old shack. Harker in rocking chair with shotgun. Gives wrong directions into an ambush.
13. **Livestock Thieves** — Three Harkers herding stolen cattle through woods. Intervene = reward from nearby ranch.
14. **The Burning Cabin** — Family watching their cabin burn. Harkers driving away laughing in red truck.
15. **The Trophy Hunter** — Lone Harker selling victims' wallets, watches, rings on the roadside.

### The Copperhead Boys
- **Region:** The Mire (swamp)
- **Vibe:** Swamp rats. Moonshine runners. Know every inch of the swamp. Use boats more than horses. Lure people in and they're never seen again.
- **Weapons:** Lever-action rifles, machetes, bear traps
- **Signature:** Gator teeth necklaces on every member
- **Structure:** 2 hideouts + 1 main base
- **Main Base:** Stilt village deep in the swamp, accessible only by boat. **~80 members.**
- **Elimination:** Clear 2 hideouts + main base.

---

### The Iron Sons
- **Region:** Renovates and surrounding area
- **Vibe:** Junkyard gang. Scrap metal armor on trucks. Makeshift weapons welded from car parts. Loud, crude, violent. They run Renovates behind the scenes — few law officers are paid off.
- **Weapons:** Pipe shotguns, homemade explosives, sledgehammers, stolen military guns
- **Signature:** Brand burned into forearm — a gear symbol
- **Structure:** 3 hideouts + 1 main base
- **Main Base:** Massive junkyard compound outside Renovates. Walls made of crushed cars. **~150 members.** Largest non-story gang.
- **Elimination:** Clear 3 hideouts + main base.

---

### The Ridgeback Runners
- **Region:** Mount Everest Range
- **Vibe:** Mountain survivalists. Live in abandoned mines and caves. Hunt everything — including people. Rob travelers on mountain passes. Silent, methodical, patient. Watch you for hours before striking.
- **Weapons:** Hunting rifles with scopes, bows, hatchets, traps
- **Signature:** White fur cloaks from mountain goats. Hard to see in snow.
- **Structure:** 2 hideouts + 1 main base
- **Main Base:** Abandoned mining town built into the mountainside. **~60 members.**
- **Elimination:** Clear 2 hideouts + main base.

---

### The Dusters
- **Region:** Sandy Dunes
- **Vibe:** Desert road pirates. Dirt bikes, modified trucks, bandanas. Control every road through the desert. Nobody passes without paying a toll or getting robbed. Fast, aggressive, hit and run.
- **Weapons:** SMGs, revolvers, sawed-offs, Molotov cocktails
- **Signature:** Red bandanas. Every member.
- **Structure:** 1 hideout + 1 main base
- **Main Base:** Abandoned gas station compound with watchtower. **~90 members.**
- **Elimination:** Clear 1 hideout + main base.
- **Access:** Jeremiah CANNOT encounter them (Sandy Dunes blocked). Marcus can in the epilogue.

---

### The Thornfield Family
- **Region:** Great Watson Plains (rural areas between towns)
- **Vibe:** Ranching family turned outlaw. Look legitimate — own land, cattle, horses. But rob, extort, and murder anyone who settles near their territory. The plains are THEIR land.
- **Weapons:** Lever-action rifles, revolvers, lariats (lasso you off your horse)
- **Signature:** Cattle brand on horses — a T with thorns
- **Structure:** 2 hideouts + 1 main base
- **Main Base:** Fortified ranch compound with watchtowers and fences. **~70 members.**
- **Elimination:** Clear 2 hideouts + main base.

---

### The Krewe
- **Region:** The Mire (swamp) — opposite side from Copperheads. They hate each other.
- **Vibe:** Mysterious. Led by **Mama Delacroix**, an old woman half the region thinks is a witch. Deal in poisons, herbs, information, and fear. Not violent unless provoked — then horrifyingly violent. Voodoo dolls and animal bones as warnings.
- **Weapons:** Poisoned knives, shotguns, blowguns
- **Signature:** Painted faces (black and white), bone jewelry
- **Structure:** 1 hideout + 1 main base
- **Main Base:** Hidden compound deep in swamp, surrounded by totems and hanging bones. **~40 members.** Smallest gang but most feared.
- **Elimination:** Clear hideout + main base. **OR** Mama Delacroix offers a deal instead of fighting — unique interaction. Player choice.
- **Special:** Only gang with a non-combat resolution option.
