## Charles Burns Clark

*Clark is running his mouth near the supply area. Charles is loading bullets into a gun rack.*

- Clark insults Charles and Joseph — calls them old men playing pretend
- Charles pops the tip off a bullet with his knife
- Pours gunpowder into his palm
- Pinches it like salt and throws it in the air in front of Clark's face
- Lights it with a lighter — flash ignites, severe first-degree burns on Clark's face
- Charles spits on Clark while he's on the ground
- **Charles:** "I was thinking of telling you to eat dirt. But then I thought you'd want to feel a bit of the place you're gonna go when you die."
- Kicks Clark. Goes back to loading bullets like nothing happened.
- Nobody helps Clark up.

## Clark and Rosa Salinas

### The Lead-Up (Night, by the creek)
- Clark approaches Rosa when camp is asleep
- Smooth talks her — "You're the most interesting person in this camp"
- Rosa: "This is a bad idea." / Clark: "Probably."
- They walk into Clark's tent

### Inside the Tent (Audio only — player hears muffled dialogue)
- Rosa laughing about his shotgun digging into her
- Light banter

### The Morning After
- Clark is already up, cleaning his M11, won't look at Rosa
- "We had a good time. Don't make it into something it's not."
- "I'm not the relationship type. You knew that walking in."
- Rosa walks back to the women's side. Clark goes back to whistling.

### Camp Reacts
- **Mae** — sees Rosa leave Clark's tent. Shakes her head.
- **Sadie to Emily** — "Clark? Really? Of all people?"
- **Emily** — "It's her business, Sadie."
- **June and Faye** — whispering, go quiet when Rosa passes
- **Bess** — won't even look at her
- **Alma to Rosa** — "You're a grown woman who made a choice. I also think it was a bad one. Clark is not a good man."

### Hector Confronts Clark
- "That's my sister. If you go near her again, I'll gut you like a fish. And I'll do it slow."
- Clark's smile fades. No comeback for once.

### Clark Acts Like Nothing Happened
- Phil warns him: "Hector sharpens that knife every morning, Clark."

### Rosa Owns It
- Tommy makes a comment. Rosa snaps back: "You — the man who used to beg Emily for five minutes of her time?"
- Tommy goes quiet. Rosa walks away head high.

### Rosa and Emily Bond
- Emily: "I know what it's like. Having the whole camp talk about you."
- Emily: "Us girls in this camp gotta stick together. Nobody else is going to."

## Marcus and Emily — Camp Party (Chapter 4)

- Marcus and Emily by the fire. Both have had drinks.
- Emily gets Marcus to dance — just swaying, nothing fancy
- Phil whistles. Marcus flips him off without letting go of Emily.
- Emily whispers in Marcus's ear. They walk to the tent. Flap closes.

### Inside the Tent (Audio only)
- Emily laughing about Marcus's boots being stuck
- Marcus: "It's dark in here." / Emily: "It's a tent."
- A thud — Marcus hit his ankle
- Emily: "You're a disaster." / Marcus: "Yeah well you picked me."
- Emily softer: "Yeah. I did." / Marcus: "Come here."

### The Morning After (Both still half drunk)
- Laying together. Josh between them. Hungover.
- Marcus: "I think I'm still drunk." / Emily: "Good. You're nicer when you're drunk."
- Josh kicks Marcus in his sleep
- Emily: "He gets the violence from you." / Marcus: "He gets the stubbornness from you."
- Marcus: "I'm laying here with my family and I'm not moving."
- Emily: "Say that again." / Marcus: "...My family."
- Emily: "Took you long enough."
- **First time Marcus says "my family" out loud.**

### Camp Reactions (Morning)

- Josh: "Why is daddy still in bed?" / Emily: "Because daddy is lazy." / Marcus: "I am... recovering." / Josh: "From what?" / Emily: "From being stubborn for two years."
