# Dead Horizon — Hidden Honor System

---

## Overview
- **Completely hidden** — no meter, no bar, no button to check
- Player never knows the system exists unless they figure it out
- The world reacts to you differently based on honor level
- **15 good deeds = +1 honor level**
- **15 bad deeds = -1 honor level**
- **Player starts at Level 4 (Neutral)**

---

## Good Deeds
- Helping strangers
- Donating to camp
- Sparing people when given the choice
- Being kind in dialogue (greet vs antagonize)
- Taking care of your horse
- Returning lost items
- Defending the innocent

## Bad Deeds
- Killing innocents
- Robbing people outside of missions
- Antagonizing camp members
- Ignoring people who need help
- Cruelty to animals
- Unprovoked violence

---

## Honor Levels

| Level | Title | Access | Deeds Required | Effects |
|-------|-------|--------|---------------|---------|
| **0** | Demon | Pre-order only | 50 worst crimes from Level 1 | Towns shoot on sight. Las York citizens start riots to kill you. Law brings out machine guns. The entire world wants you dead. Basically unplayable chaos. |
| **1** | Worst | Normal | 15 bad deeds from Level 2 | People are scared of you. Store prices raised. Random NPCs lash out and attack you. |
| **2** | Bad | Normal | 15 bad deeds from Level 3 | Bad comments about you. People still scared. NPCs challenge you to duels (draw your gun). |
| **3** | Low | Normal | 15 bad deeds from Level 4 | Store prices still elevated. Bad comments continue. The law doesn't mind you as much. |
| **4** | Neutral | Normal | **Starting level** | Default. Normal store prices. No special treatment. People don't love or hate you. |
| **5** | Good | Normal | 15 good deeds from Level 4 | NPCs randomly give you small amounts ($5). Law greets you. Store prices drop 5%. |
| **6** | High | Normal | 15 good deeds from Level 5 | NPCs greet you before you even look at them. Law acts like your friend. Store prices drop 15%. |
| **7** | Great | Normal | 15 good deeds from Level 6 | NPCs randomly give you items: guns, ammo, horses, leftover clothing. Great way to unlock stuff early. Store prices drop 25%. |
| **8** | Legend | Normal | 15 good deeds from Level 7 | People adore you. Better gifts: tuxedos, silk vests, high-class horses. NPCs walk up and give you $50. Store prices drop 50%. |
| **9** | Saint | Pre-order only | 50 best deeds from Level 8 | Prices drop 70%. Best possible treatment. Ultimate good honor. |

---

## What Honor Affects
- **Store prices** — from raised (low honor) to -70% (highest honor)
- **NPC dialogue** — scared/hostile to warm/adoring
- **NPC gifts** — nothing at low honor, free guns/horses/clothes at high honor
- **Random encounters** — more hostile or more positive
- **Law enforcement** — from machine guns on sight to greeting you as a friend
- **Camp interactions** — gang members warmer or colder
- **Character reactions** — Emily, Josh, Marcus respond differently to Jeremiah
- **Music** — subtle ambient tone shifts
- **Animals** — horses calm faster or spook easier
- **Ending** — may determine how Jeremiah's story ends

---

## Design Notes
- The system is NEVER revealed to the player in-game
- No UI element, no settings option, no way to check
- Players discover it through world reactions or community discussion post-launch
- Gives strong replay value — play as a saint, play as a demon
- Pre-order levels (0 and 9) reward dedicated fans with extreme experiences
- Level 0 is designed to be almost unplayable — a challenge mode for hardcore players
