# Dead Horizon — World Systems: Destruction, Repair & Consequences

---

## Realistic Destruction Physics
- Heavy weapons and explosives cause real, persistent damage to the world
- The Boar Shotgun can blow holes in brick walls
- Molotov cocktails in dry areas can start forest fires
- Dynamite can collapse parts of buildings
- All destruction is persistent until repaired

---

## Repair System — NPCs Fix What You Break

### Brick/Stone Wall (Hole)
- Worker arrives with wheelbarrow of bricks and mortar
- Sweeps rubble, lays new bricks one by one, plasters over
- Fresh plaster lighter than old wall for a while

### Wooden Door (Destroyed)
- Carpenter arrives with planks and toolbox
- Pulls broken door off hinges, measures frame
- Builds new door on the spot — sawing, hammering
- Hangs it, tests the swing

### Glass Window (Shattered)
- First: boarded up with planks nailed in an X
- Next day: glazier arrives with wrapped glass pane
- Pries off planks, picks out shards, sets new glass, putties edges, wipes clean

### Wooden Fence
- Worker comes with fence posts and wire/rails
- Pulls broken posts, digs new holes, sets posts, strings wire

### Market Stall/Vendor Table
- Vendor returns cursing, picks up scattered goods
- Restacks everything. Glares at you if you walk by.

### Street Lantern/Oil Lamp
- Worker at dusk with ladder and replacement glass
- Replaces chimney, fills oil, lights with match

### Roof (Fire/Explosion)
- Two workers with ladders and shingles
- Strip burned shingles, lay new ones, nail down

### Water Trough
- Worker drags new trough on cart, removes broken one, fills with water

### Hitching Post
- Worker digs out stump, drops in fresh post, packs dirt, ties rope

### Wagon/Cart (Destroyed in road)
- Wreckage blocks road, people route around
- Man with horse team hooks chains, drags it off road

### Church Bell (Shot)
- Cracks or falls. Notice posted: "Bell under repair"
- Blacksmith arrives days later with new bell on cart
- Hoisted with ropes, hung, tested with one ring

### Campfire (Kicked apart)
- Gang member walks over annoyed
- Restacks stones, gathers firewood, relights

### Whole House (Destroyed)
**Day 1:** Smoldering rubble. Displaced family standing outside. Neighbors bring blankets/food.
**Day 2-3:** Crew of 4-5 clears rubble with horse-drawn cart. Foundation swept clean. Salvageable materials stacked.
**Day 4-5:** Foreman measures foundation. Lumber deliveries arrive. Post holes dug, corner supports set.
**Day 6-8:** Frame goes up. Workers on ladders nailing crossbeams. Sawing sounds through town.
**Day 9-11:** Walls go up plank by plank or brick by brick. Chimney starts. Window frames installed.
**Day 12-14:** Roof goes on. Front door hung. Glass installed. Porch rebuilt.
**Day 15-16:** Interior work. Painter does exterior.
**Day 17:** House complete. Looks brand new — cleaner/brighter than surroundings. Family moves back in.
- High honor: Owner thanks you
- Low honor/player caused it: "Stay away from my home."
- Destroy it again: Owner may leave town permanently. Empty lot remains.

### Multiple Houses Destroyed
- Town starts looking abandoned
- Fewer NPCs, shops close
- Town slowly dies
- Newspaper: "RESIDENTS FLEE [TOWN] — ONCE THRIVING COMMUNITY NOW A GHOST TOWN"

## Consequence System — Making the Player Feel Guilt

### The Orphan
- Kill someone → their kids show up at the funeral
- Days later: orphaned kids wandering streets, dirty, begging
- "Have you seen my daddy?"

### The Widow
- Kill a man → wife appears at general store selling his belongings
- Wearing black. "That was my husband's. He won't be needing it anymore."

### The Empty Chair
- Kill a shopkeeper → shop closes for days
- Reopens under new management. Empty chair remains. Photo of dead owner with flowers.

### Animal Guilt
- Kill a horse → it screams and falls. Rider crawls to it, holds its head as it dies.
- Kill a dog → owner comes running screaming the dog's name.

### The Grave Visit
- Cemetery grows with people you killed
- Fresh graves with their names
- NPCs kneeling at graves crying

### The Kid Who Remembers
- Kill someone in front of a child → child remembers forever
- Later chapters: the kid is older, confronts you. "You killed my father."

### The Beggar
- Rob a man of everything → weeks later he's begging on a street corner
- Recognizes you: "You... you took everything from me."

### The Dog That Waits
- Kill an NPC who had a dog → dog sits outside their house
- Stays there for days. Gets thinner. Waiting for owner to come home.

### The Funeral You Caused
- Walk through town → stumble onto a funeral procession for someone you killed
- Priest describes them: "a loving father," "a good honest man"
- Mourners glare at you

### Fire Survivors
- Destroy a home with fire → family standing outside in blankets
- Little girl holding a burnt stuffed animal. Looks at you. Says nothing.

### The Thank You Note
- Loot a body → find a letter from their kid
- "Dear papa, thank you for the toy horse. I named him thunder. Come home soon. I love you."

## Stranger Mission Weapons

### Oak's Lab Experiment (Chapter 3)
- 3-part stranger mission chain (unlocked after Strange Things Part 3)
- Oak is a crazy inventor from Oklahoma trying to build everyday helpful inventions
- Mission 1: Raid enemy base for reactor core
- Mission 2: Bring generator, gasoline, metal pieces
- Mission 3: Oak gifts you **Red MK 18 (.50 cal, fully loaded with attachments)**
- Oak reveals his invention — a plane. Flies 3 seconds. Crashes. Explodes. Oak dies.
- Player finds Oak's skeleton, burnt and impaled with wire
- Jeremiah: "Well, I'll just have to say something to the university."

### The Tribe (Multi-chapter, finished as Marcus)
- Part 1: Help tribal community defeat US Army camp pushing them out
- **Reward: Talisman — permanent +30% XP boost**
- Part 2 (Marcus only): Save tribe member from animal attack
- **Rewards: Animal pelt + Tiger's Cloak (unique clothing)**

### Bounty Hunter's Reward (30 bounties completed)
- Sheriff sends letter to post office
- Wrapped package contains **Crystal Revolver** — diamond grip, long barrel
- Comes with thank you note for keeping people safe
