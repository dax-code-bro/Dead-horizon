## Joseph's Chess — Hidden Story

Joseph's chess pieces map to real people:

| Piece | Person | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **King** | Joseph | Most important by rule, but he doesn't protect it. Doesn't value himself. |
| **Queen** | Luisa | The ONLY piece he truly cares about. Taking her = table flip, rage quit. |
| **Bishop 1** | Jeremiah | His "son." Closest protector. |
| **Bishop 2** | Charles | His oldest partner. The brain. |
| **Knight 1** | Marcus | Unpredictable. Moves in ways you don't expect. |
| **Knight 2** | Clark | Appears where you don't expect. (The traitor hiding in plain sight.) |
| **Rook 1** | Phil | The tower. Heavy, powerful, direct. |
| **Rook 2** | Trevor | The foundation. Holds everything together. |
| **Pawns** | Rest of gang | Lenny, Jeff, Nate, Ray, Tommy, etc. Front line. First sacrificed. |

- **15% chance** Joseph plays the Immortal strategy — keeps all pieces, then sacrifices them all
- **If you take his Queen** — he flips the table and storms off, even though his King is untouched
- The Immortal strategy foreshadows Joseph's endgame plan for the gang

## Random Interactions — Joseph's Decline

### Emily Checks on Joseph
- Emily talks to Joseph. He can't look in her direction. Says "I'm fine, I'm fine."
- Starts saying random words and mumbling. Walks off.

### Joseph Forgets Names
- Walks up to Phil, can't remember Lenny's name. Gets frustrated, snaps, storms off.

### Joseph Clings to Luisa
- Won't leave Luisa's side all day. Tells Jeremiah "my family comes first" — hypocritical after telling Marcus that Emily is a distraction.

### Joseph Talks to Nobody
- Late night. Jeremiah hears Joseph talking in his tent. Goes in — Joseph is talking to an empty chair. "You understand, don't you? You always understood."

### Joseph's Hypocrisy
- Walks past Marcus and Emily with Luisa on his arm. Tells Marcus to "do something useful" instead of sitting with family. Marcus sees Luisa on Joseph's arm but says nothing.

### Joseph's Breathing / Chess (Player Choice)
- Late night. Player hears stuttered, loud breathing from Joseph's tent.
- **Player can choose to investigate or walk away.**
- If investigated: Joseph is playing chess alone. Both sides. Using the Immortal strategy. Sweeps his own pieces off the board. "That's how you win." Jeremiah thinks he's just being crazy. He's not.

## Random Interactions — Emily and Gang Members

### Clark Stirs the Pot (Solo)
- Clark brings up Emily's past at the creek. Emily shuts him down. Threatens to tell Marcus.

### Clark Tries It (Marcus Present)
- Clark makes a comment at the fire. Marcus is on his feet instantly. Clark backs down.

### Phil (Drunk)
- Drunk Phil tries to reminisce about his past with Emily. Emily tells him he gave Josh food and she "tolerated" him. Phil goes quiet.

### Tommy Roe Pushes His Luck
- Tommy gets too close, implies he could replace Marcus. Emily threatens to break his hand. Marcus appears, shoves Tommy. Nobody helps Tommy.

### Ray Colburn (Drunk)
- Drunk Ray calls Emily "Queen Emily" and comments on her past. Jeremiah appears, tells Ray to go to bed. Ray complies.

### Owen Pratt (Drunk) — Marcus's Doubt
- Owen makes a comment about Emily "making the rounds." Marcus goes quiet. Walks to his tent. Sits alone in the dark. Emily follows. Marcus: "I did this to you." Emily tells him to stop punishing himself. He eventually comes back to the fire.

### Joseph Confronts Emily
- Joseph tells Emily to back off Marcus. Calls her "a distraction" and "not worth anything." Emily doesn't flinch: "Say that to me again and see what happens."

### Joseph Confronts Marcus
- Joseph tells Marcus that Emily is a distraction and not worth it. Marcus pushes back for the first time: "Emily, Josh — they're off limits. Even from you."
