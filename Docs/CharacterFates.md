# Dead Horizon — Character Fates (2026)

---

## Main Characters

### Jeremiah Field — DEAD
- Killed Chapter 16. Shot by Joseph, finished by Clark (low honor) or bled out alone watching the sunrise (high honor).
- All belongings died with him: silver Cattleman, melted toy soldier, letter to Joseph, coat.
- Letters to Marcus, Emily, and Josh were in Cerberus's saddlebag — never delivered.

### Marcus McAllen — ALIVE
- 33 years old. Rancher. Married to Emily for 6 years. Raising Josh on a 40-acre ranch.
- Killed Clark in the epilogue. "That was for Jerry."
- Living the life Jeremiah died to give him.

### Emily McAllen — ALIVE
- Late 20s/early 30s. Marcus's wife. Running the household. Strong. Never needed saving.
- Kept Marcus's old gear under the bed for 7 years. Just in case.

### Josh McAllen — ALIVE
- 12 years old. Ranch hand. Hunter. Named his calf Cookie. Named his goat Brick. Wants a potato garden.
- Still has the bullet scar from Chapter 4. Still has Uncle Jerry's drawing on his wall.

### Joseph Walkns — UNKNOWN
- Walked into the redwoods after turning his gun on Clark. Left alive but broken.
- Seven years of brain damage and Clark's manipulation. In the final moment, he chose not to kill. Holstered his revolvers and walked away.
- Fate intentionally ambiguous.

### Luisa Walkns — LIKELY DEAD
- Either killed herself, killed by Joseph during an episode, or killed by Clark.
- The Queen on the chess board didn't survive.

### Clark Jones — DEAD
- Killed by Marcus in the epilogue. Shot in both shoulders, pinned against a door, bullet to the head.
- "That was for Jerry."
- The white coat turned red. The smile gone forever.

## Gang Members — Alive

### Phil — ALIVE
- Shot in the stomach by Joseph during the epilogue. Survived thanks to Delia.
- Married to Sadie. Still has dynamite in his truck "for emergencies."
- Riding south with Delia after the Redwood confrontation.

### Sadie Voss — ALIVE
- Married to Phil. Bounty hunting in South America. Still the toughest person alive.

### Delia Harmon — ALIVE
- Traveling with Phil. Still patching people up. The medic who never stopped.

### Hector Salinas — ALIVE
- Started his own gang after the split. It failed fast. Found by Phil and Delia.

### Earl Mackey — ALIVE
- Had a complication with Faye. Left her. Started his own gang. It fell apart. Found by Phil and Delia.

### Ray Colburn — DEAD
- Killed by Clark a few months before the epilogue. Phil and Delia found out too late.

### Mae Colburn — LIKELY DEAD
- Unknown fate after Ray's death. Likely killed by Clark or Joseph as well.

### Nate Briggs — ALIVE
- The kid who grew up. Left the outlaw life. Became a magician/performer/actor.
- Took confidence, quick hands, and people skills from the gang and went legit.

### Danny Voss — ALIVE
- Left in Chapter 10 with Nell. Made it to Canada. Likely started a family.

### Nell Carter — ALIVE
- With Danny in Canada. The new couple became a real couple. The quietest ending for the quietest members.

### Rosa Salinas — ALIVE
- Works at a clothing store in Las York. Went legit. Still doesn't take nonsense from anyone.

### Bess Tanner — ALIVE
- Can be seen in Hope. Sent letters to check on Emily and Marcus. Stopped when she knew they were fine. Quiet care from a quiet person.

### Vinny Cross — ALIVE
- Took over a small ranch. The smooth talker probably talked his way into a good deal on the land.

### Wade Perkins — ALIVE
- Became an artist. The man who never spoke found a way to say things without words.

### Ray Colburn — DEAD

### Faye Mackey — ALIVE (Outlaw)
