# Dead Horizon — Story Bible

## Setting
- **Year:** 2019
- **World:** A world where survival gangs operate outside the law. Horses are preferred over cars — more reliable, no fuel needed, harder to steal.

## The Gang
Led by Jacob Walkns. Members refer to each other as family — Jacob calls them his "children" or "brothers."

---

## Characters

### Jeremiah Field (Protagonist)
- **Age:** Early 30s (born ~1986-1989)
- **Origin:** United Kingdom
- **Background:** His father was a notorious outlaw, secretly a member of the gang. His father was killed, and had previously told Jacob and Charles to look after Jeremiah. Jacob and Charles adopted him at age 12 (~1998-2001). Raised in the gang.
- **Military:** Joined the US Marines to earn money for the gang. Rose to E-7 (Gunnery Sergeant).
- **Family:** Had a wife, son, and daughter. They were killed in a fire while he was out on patrol — came back to find "just ashes." This happened a few years before 2019.
- **Mother:** Name unknown, mentioned in later missions.
- **Personality:** Disciplined, loyal, distant but caring. Carries deep grief over his lost family. Tries to push Marcus to reconnect with Emily and Josh because he knows what it's like to lose everything.
- **Nickname:** Clark calls him "Jerry."
- **Role in gang:** Third longest-standing member. Jacob calls him "son."
- **Key quote:** "I had a wife, a son and daughter. They died a few years back. I was out on patrol, came back... just ashes. That's why you got to keep them while you still can. You got something most of us don't."

### Jacob Walkns (Gang Leader)
- **Age:** Late 40s (born ~1970-1972)
- **Background:** Wanted to be an outlaw since childhood. Became one at age 14 (~1984-1986). First kill in 1999 — shot a general store worker who fought back during a robbery.
- **Personality:** Sees the gang as his family. Calls members "his children" or "his brothers." Pragmatic leader — doesn't push Marcus and Emily together because he sees their drama as a distraction to "his children." Co-raised Jeremiah with Charles.
- **Weapons:** Carries an M4 and a lantern.

### Luisa Walkns
- **Relationship:** Jacob's partner. Not married. They broke up twice before getting back together. She chose to take Jacob's last name despite not being married.

### Charles
- **Age:** 77 (born ~1942)
- **Role:** The brain of the gang. Second longest-standing member (after Jacob). Co-adopted and raised Jeremiah.
- **Personality:** Tough despite his age — still tosses gang members around if they get in his way. Easy to earn his respect. Tries to get Marcus back on his feet.

### Marcus McAllen
- **Age:** Early 20s (born ~1996-1999)
- **Background:** Jeremiah's brother in arms. Had his son Josh at age 20. The pressure caused him to run away. Was found and brought back to the gang.
- **Personality:** Distant from his family. Keeps distance from Emily. Jealous of Jeremiah's closeness to Emily and Josh. Has paranoia that Emily is having an affair (she isn't). Tells Jeremiah he might as well marry Emily. Extreme doubts that his family loves him.
- **Arc:** Slowly reconnects with Emily across chapters. Relationship improves over time but struggles with trust issues with his 5-year-old son Josh. Marcus pretends not to care about Emily, but his mask slips — he shoves gang members who try to mess with her.

### Emily McAllen
- **Background:** Marcus's partner. When Marcus ran away, she was mentally broken. In the past, she had relationships with multiple gang members who offered food and care for Josh in exchange — she never loved any of them, did it purely for her son's survival. Since Marcus's return, she is strictly loyal to him.
- **Personality:** Similar to Abigail from Red Dead Redemption 2 but her own character. Was highly capable and not afraid to get dirty before having Josh. Stepped back to become a mother. Now actively trying to get Marcus to spend time with her and Josh. Loved Marcus at first sight and has never wanted anyone else.
- **Key detail:** Shuts down any gang members who try the old tricks on her. Also shuts down Clark when he brings up her past.

### Josh McAllen
- **Age:** 5 (born ~2014)
- **Personality:** Has trust issues with Marcus because his father left. Takes time to warm up to him even as Marcus improves.

### Clark Jones
- **Age:** Not specified
- **Time in gang:** ~2.5 years (joined ~2016-2017). One of the newer members.
- **Personality:** Confident. Wears a white coat. Sometimes stirs the pot — brings up Emily's past but gets shut down by Emily or Marcus. Calls Jeremiah "Jerry."

### Trevor
- **Role:** Caretaker. Takes people in, gets them warmed up and back in shape.

## Timeline
- **~1942** — Charles born
- **~1970-1972** — Jacob born
- **~1984-1986** — Jacob begins outlaw life at 14
- **~1986-1989** — Jeremiah born in the UK
- **~1996-1999** — Marcus born
- **~1998-2001** — Jeremiah's father killed. Jacob and Charles adopt Jeremiah at age 12
- **1999** — Jacob's first kill (store robbery)
- **~2014** — Josh McAllen born. Marcus is ~20
- **~2015** — Marcus runs away after Josh's birth
- **~2016-2017** — Clark Jones joins the gang
- **~2016-2018** — Jeremiah's wife, son, and daughter die in a fire while he's on patrol
- **2019** — Game begins. Marcus has been brought back. Events of Dead Horizon.
