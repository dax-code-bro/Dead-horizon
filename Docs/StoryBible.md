# Dead Horizon — Story Bible

## Setting
- **Year:** 2019
- **World:** A world where survival gangs operate outside the law. Horses are preferred over cars — more reliable, no fuel needed, harder to steal.

## The Gang
Led by Joseph Walkns. Members refer to each other as family — Joseph calls them his "children" or "brothers."

---

## Characters

### Jeremiah Field (Protagonist)
- **Age:** Early 30s (born ~1986-1989)
- **Origin:** United Kingdom
- **Background:** His father was a notorious outlaw, secretly a member of the gang. His father was killed, and had previously told Joseph and Charles to look after Jeremiah. Joseph and Charles adopted him at age 12 (~1998-2001). Raised in the gang.
- **Military:** Joined the US Marines to earn money for the gang. Rose to E-7 (Gunnery Sergeant).
- **Family:** Had a wife, son, and daughter. They were killed in a fire while he was out on patrol — came back to find "just ashes." This happened a few years before 2019.
- **Mother:** Name unknown, mentioned in later missions.
- **Personality:** Disciplined, loyal, distant but caring. Carries deep grief over his lost family. Tries to push Marcus to reconnect with Emily and Josh because he knows what it's like to lose everything.
- **Nickname:** Clark calls him "Jerry."
- **Role in gang:** Third longest-standing member. Joseph calls him "son."
- **Horse:** Cerberus
- **Key quote:** "I had a wife, a son and daughter. They died a few years back. I was out on patrol, came back... just ashes. That's why you got to keep them while you still can. You got something most of us don't."

### Joseph Walkns (Gang Leader)
- **Age:** Late 40s (born ~1970-1972)
- **Background:** Wanted to be an outlaw since childhood. Became one at age 14 (~1984-1986). First kill in 1999 — shot a general store worker who fought back during a robbery. His brother was killed by Michael Washington.
- **Personality:** Sees the gang as his family. Calls members "his children" or "his brothers." Pragmatic leader — doesn't push Marcus and Emily together because he sees their drama as a distraction to "his children." Co-raised Jeremiah with Charles.
- **Weapons:** Carries an M4 and a lantern.
- **Horse:** Snow

### Luisa Walkns
- **Relationship:** Joseph's partner. Not married. They broke up twice before getting back together. She chose to take Joseph's last name despite not being married.
- **Personality:** Caring — seen talking to and looking after Josh.

### Charles
- **Age:** 77 (born ~1942)
- **Role:** The brain of the gang. Second longest-standing member (after Joseph). Co-adopted and raised Jeremiah.
- **Personality:** Tough despite his age — still tosses gang members around if they get in his way. Easy to earn his respect. Tries to get Marcus back on his feet.

### Marcus McAllen
- **Age:** 26 (born ~1993)
- **Background:** Jeremiah's brother in arms. Had his son Josh at age 20. The pressure caused him to run away. Was found and brought back to the gang.
- **Personality:** Distant from his family. Keeps distance from Emily. Jealous of Jeremiah's closeness to Emily and Josh. Has paranoia that Emily is having an affair (she isn't). Tells Jeremiah he might as well marry Emily. Extreme doubts that his family loves him.
- **Arc:** Slowly reconnects with Emily across chapters. Relationship improves over time but struggles with trust issues with his 5-year-old son Josh. Marcus pretends not to care about Emily, but his mask slips — he shoves gang members who try to mess with her.
- **Horse:** Jim

### Emily McAllen
- **Age:** 20-24 during the game (2019). Was 16-18 during the worst period of her life.
- **Background:** Marcus's partner. Had Josh as a teenager (~16-17). When Marcus ran away, she was mentally broken — a teenager alone with a baby. Had relationships with multiple gang members who offered food and care for Josh in exchange — she never loved any of them, did it purely for her son's survival. Since Marcus's return, she is strictly loyal to him. She loved Marcus at first sight and has never wanted anyone else.
- **Personality:** Similar to Abigail from Red Dead Redemption 2 but her own character. Was highly capable and not afraid to get dirty before having Josh. Stepped back to become a mother. Now actively trying to get Marcus to spend time with her and Josh.
- **Key detail:** Shuts down any gang members who try the old tricks on her. Also shuts down Clark when he brings up her past.

### Josh McAllen
- **Age:** 5 (born ~2014)
- **Personality:** Has trust issues with Marcus because his father left. Takes time to warm up to him even as Marcus improves. Calls Jeremiah "Uncle Jerry."

### Clark Jones
- **Age:** Not specified
- **Time in gang:** ~2.5 years (joined ~2016-2017). One of the newer members.
- **Personality:** Confident. Wears a white coat. Sometimes stirs the pot — brings up Emily's past but gets shut down by Emily or Marcus. Calls Jeremiah "Jerry." Not the type to take blame — tried to fight Emily when she blamed him for Marcus's capture.
- **Horse:** Gunpowder

### Trevor
- **Role:** Caretaker and medic. Takes people in, gets them warmed up and back in shape. Set Marcus's broken leg and bandaged him up. Also capable of scouting — found the location where Marcus was being held.

### Phil
- **Role:** Gang member. Good with Josh — volunteered to take him during the wagon ride so Marcus and Emily could talk.

### Jeff
- **Role:** Gang member. Helped get Marcus off the horse at camp.

### Michael Washington (Antagonist)
- **Role:** Leader of the Washington gang.
- **History:** Old rival of Joseph. They go way back — constantly stealing scores from each other. Killed Joseph's brother.
- **Actions:** His gang captured and tortured Marcus for information about the gang and Joseph.

## Locations

### Camp 1: The Snow Camp (Chapter 1, Missions 1-3)
- Snowy environment. Tents, stables, barn.
- Abandoned after the raid on the Washington gang town — too dangerous to stay.

### Camp 2: The Gorge (Chapter 1 Ending onward)
- A narrow canyon path leads into a gorge — natural chokepoint, easy to defend with just a couple guards.
- **Cave (Interior Camp):** Deep cave at the back of the gorge. Sleeping quarters, armory, supply storage, leadership area. Lanterns hung from rock walls.
- **Gorge Clearing (Exterior Camp):** Stables, wagons, fire pit, cooking area, common gathering space.
- **Lookout:** Post on top of the hill/cliff above the cave entrance.
- **Defense:** Wooden walls and fences on the open side. Narrow entrance is a natural fortress.

- **Cerberus** — Jeremiah's horse. Thoroughbred. Black front (pitch black hair and skin), white back (snow white hair and skin). Striking two-tone.

- **Jim** — Marcus's horse. Belgian Draft. Golden body, white neck, white spots/patches, white mane, shaggy forelock hanging over eyes. Big, gentle, dependable.
- **Gunpowder** — Clark's horse. Akhal-Teke. Fully black with metallic sheen.

## Chapter Summary

### Chapter 1: Missions
1. **Welcome Home** — Blizzard intro, ride to camp, camp chores
2. **Old Friends, and Time with an Old Mentor** — Hunting with Joseph, Marcus captured by Washingtons
3. **Fine, We'll Get Him Back** — Rescue Marcus from Washington gang town
4. **A New, Better Place** — Gang relocates to the Gorge

### Chapter 2: Missions (A Few Months Later)
1. **Settling In, the McAllens** — Marcus/Emily fight, Beretta 92FS purchase, bar night, Marcus fishes with Josh
2. **Weapon Buy / Saloon Trap** — AK-47 bulk buy, lawman ambush, Joseph's plan abandoned, Joseph's sanity cracks
3. **Hunting Trip** — Elk hunt with Trevor and Lenny, bear attack, Lenny injured
4. **The Hope Robbery** — General store heist, shootout, $3,000 score, Snow shot in leg
5. **Supply Run** — Restocking camp, Washington gang spreading into territory
6. **Washington Outpost Assault** — 19 vs 300, total victory, zero casualties, looted weapons
7. **The Evacuation** — Marshalls incoming, 30-minute evacuation, Marcus/Emily breakthrough, arrive at the Hill

### Chapter 3: Missions
1. **The One Thing We Have in Common** — Waterson horse theft, Barry killed, stealth mission
2. **Just Counter Measures** — Scouting with Marcus, Jeremiah reveals family, waterfall cave found
3. **Helping with a Few Things** — Chores with Emily, Josh runs to Marcus (major shift)
4. **He's At It Again** — Trolley robbery, $250K, Joseph's brain damage, Marcus injured
5. **Bank Robbery** — Lenny killed by old lawman, militia chase, battle at Hill camp
6. **Waterfall Arrival** — Evacuation to waterfall cave, Marcus sees Immortal strategy

### Chapter 4: Missions (Las York)
1. **Hunting Trip** — Comedy mission with Trevor, .50 cal raccoon
2. **Goodbye Old Friend** — Michael Washington executed by firing squad
3. **Catching Up with an Old Mentor Again** — Joseph/Jeremiah day in Las York
4. **Waterson Ranch Raid** — Entire Waterson family killed, all 9 members

## Game Systems
- **Checkpoint system** — On failure, player returns to last checkpoint
- **Fail conditions** — Mission-specific (shooting wrong target, rope snapping, etc.)
- **Optional camp interactions** — Talk to gang members between missions for extra story/dialogue
- **Horse riding** — Core traversal mechanic
- **Transport mechanic** — Hauling cargo behind horses with speed/turn management
- **Performance-based pay** — Better play = bigger share of scores
- **Weapon unlock progression** — New weapons available as chapters/missions are completed
