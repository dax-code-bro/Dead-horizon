# Dead Horizon — Weapons Guide

---

## Character Signature Weapons

### Jeremiah Field (Protagonist)
- **Custom silver Cattleman revolver (black grip)** — signature sidearm
- Other weapons are mission-given, not personal

### Joseph Walkns
- **Custom white 1911 (skull engravings covering entire gun)** — sidearm
- **Gray M4 Carbine** — primary
  - Suppressor attached
  - EOTech holographic sight with unique cross symbol (player cannot replicate)
  - Retractable stock

### Marcus McAllen
- **Beretta 92FS Bronze** — sidearm, bought in Chapter 2
- **Standard hunting rifle** — practical, nothing fancy

### Charles
- **Worn, beat-up 1911 (original model)** — ancient, scratched, faded, grip worn smooth. Looks like it should've been retired decades ago. Still deadly.

### Clark Jones
- **Custom white sawed-off shotgun** — matches his white coat aesthetic
- **Black M11 compact (MAC-11) with skull symbol in the middle**

### Luisa Walkns
- **Silver snub-nose .38 revolver with pearl grip** — small, elegant, always carried

### Emily McAllen
- **No weapon** — stepped away from fighting when Josh was born

### Phil
- **Two sawed-off shotguns** — dual wield
- **Fully black semi-auto shotgun** — primary
- **Stick of dynamite on belt**

### Trevor
- **9mm 1911** — simple self-defense sidearm
- **.308 hunting rifle** — for hunting, not combat

---

## Weapon Unlock System
- Weapons unlock as the player completes missions and chapters (similar to Red Dead Redemption)
- Some weapons are purchasable after completing specific missions
- Some weapons unlock when completing entire chapters
- Character-unique weapon features CANNOT be replicated by the player

---

## Weapon Notes
- Horses are preferred over cars in this world; similarly, weapons trend toward reliable and obtainable rather than cutting-edge military hardware
- The gunsmith in the town near the Gorge is the primary weapon source in Chapter 2
- Gang members have unique weapon features that the player cannot copy or attach to their own guns
